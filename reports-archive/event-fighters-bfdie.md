# Event Fighters: the BFDIE Crew

*Design only. Nothing in the game was changed.*

## The idea in short

BFDIE has 12 contestants. Seven are already fighters: Rose, Money, Fern, Sidewalky, David, Ruler and Toothpaste. Beach Ball arrives in phase 1. This plan adds the last four: **Hot Dog, Needy, Jammy and Sticker**. After that, the whole BFDIE cast is playable.

Each kit comes from that fighter's BFDIE story, and each one plays off the others:

- **Hot Dog** brings his deadly pole and kicks its traps out one by one.
- **Needy** injects her evil serum. The target gets stronger for a while, then crashes.
- **Jammy** spreads sticky jam, cracks when hit hard, and shouts that nobody listens to.
- **Sticker** has a bicycle pump, a trampoline and a knife. They are also the one fighter in this set who can steal a buff.

The links between them show up as team bonuses, matchups and rival lines on the results screen. None of them are drawn in a match.

Each fighter gets one **event week**. It is a twist match on a stage you already have, and it earns tokens that unlock that fighter for good.

| Fighter | Role | Weight | Event week |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hot Dog (he/him) | Stubborn bruiser | 100 | Nine Lives |
| Needy (she/her) | Lazy hexer | 72 | Injected! |
| Sticker (they/them) | Dirty glider | 60 | Lightweight |
| Jammy (she/her) | Whistleblower | 74 | Expose the Alliance |

**Art group: the classic look (BFDI, BFDIA, IDFB) for all four.**

- BFDIE uses the early seasons' style on purpose; the season page says so.
- The seven BFDIE fighters already in the game use BFDIE art.
- The Announcer boss is drawn as the BFDI Speaker Box.
- Hot Dog and Needy were BFB recommended characters first (Needy as "Syringe", with arms). So we use only their BFDIE pictures, never the BFB ones, and each fighter keeps one style.

**Rules every kit follows:**

- No telegraphs. The only cue is the fighter's own short startup: 12 frames for finishers, 14 for a Final Smash wind.
- Anything they spawn grows in harmlessly first, one thing at a time. The grow-in is 12 frames since you made spawning faster on 25 Oct.
- No words are drawn in a match.
- Computer only.
- Online, the host runs everything.
- CPUs press at human speed (Hard is about 6 inputs a second).

*(60 frames = 1 second. "px" is the game's distance unit.)*

## 1. Hot Dog (he/him): Stubborn bruiser

**Who he is.** Hot Dog was the first one out of BFDIE (12th). He is stubborn and blunt, and he follows the rules.

- In "Elsewhere on Earth" he got the worst pole: buzzsaws, spikes, a syringe and a flamethrower. He refused to switch: "I can do THIS one." He died again and again, and each time he came back from the Hot Dog Recovery Center angrier.
- He got within a few inches of the top. He died 9 times in the season.
- In BFDIE 6 he ran Fern over ("go through me").
- In BFDIE 7 he wouldn't leave the ocean floor.

In Smash Island he is a slow, sturdy mid-heavy who brings his own pole. He claims a spot, kicks traps at anyone near it, runs people over, and climbs to recover.

**Stats:**

- Weight 100, between Toothpaste (88) and Fern (104).
- Run 0.95 · air 0.96 · fall 1.04 · damage 1.
- Recovery is measured like everyone's. If it lands in the poorest group, he gets 1.15.
- Long, low body, so he is easy to hit from the side.
- Generous movetech: a 0.7 s wall cling, and his down air jump-cancels on hit.

**Art.**

- Picture: his BFDIE infobox image (Bfdi_Season_6_Hot_Dog.png). I have not downloaded it.
- Animation: soft bread, so a gentle squash and a lean on swings.
- Props, in flat classic style with a black outline: the pole and its four traps, and the Recovery Center box.
- His Final Smash needs new buzzsaw art.
- His moves are written bun-and-legs first, so they work whether or not his picture has arms (question 6).

**Moves:**

- **Jab: Bun Bump.** He rocks back, then bumps with his bun. 7%, short reach.
- **Side-tilt: Bun Shove.** He plants his back legs and shoves the flat of his bun ahead. 7.2%, startup 6. *(Side-tilts are off, so in play this is the jab aimed sideways, as for everyone.)*
- **Up-tilt: Bun Flip.** He rears up and flips his front end over in an arc. 6%, pops them straight up.
- **Down-tilt: Sand Kick.** He scuffs a back foot and kicks sand at their feet. 4.8%, pops them up.
- **Neutral air: Roller Grill.** He tucks in and spins along his length like a hot dog on a grill. 4 hits of 3.3%.
- **Forward air: Headlong.** He throws himself bun-first ahead. 8.6%.
- **Back air: Mule Kick.** Both legs kick out behind him. 10%, his strongest air hit.
- **Up air: Onion Pop.** The onions on his top pop up. 5.6%.
- **Down air: All the Way Back Down.** He stiffens (9 frames), then drops end-first, the way he fell off his pole. 11%, spikes. He can jump out of it once it hits.
- **Grab: In the Bun.** He clamps them in his bun (reach 32).
- **Pummel: Bun Press.** 1.8% a squeeze.
- **Throws:**
  - Forward, Serve It Up: 7.8%.
  - Back, Bun Buck: 8.4%.
  - Up, Up the Pole: 5.3%, sets up an up air.
  - Down, Relish: 5.6%, mashes them flat.
- **Special: I Can Do THIS One.**
  - He stamps twice (10 frames). His pole grows up just ahead of him, harmless while it grows. It is 140 px tall, and its top holds one fighter.
  - Press again while he is touching its base and he kicks it. The next trap slides out harmlessly, then it can hit. The traps come in canon order:
    1. A buzzsaw out both sides: 3 × 2%, plus bleeding.
    2. Spikes at the foot: 7%, plus a knockdown.
    3. A black syringe near the top: 5%, plus poison.
    4. A flame off the top: 9%, sets them burning, launches.
  - All four together do 27% over about 1.5 s of work.
  - His pole never hurts him or his teammates.
  - Anyone may stand on top. Anyone weighing 110 or more who lands on it sinks it into the floor (Sidewalky's pole sank in BFDIE 1).
  - The pole goes after its 4th trap, after 5 s, or once foes have hit it for 12%. Only one stands at a time.
- **Side special (stored only, while side specials are off): Chase the Chest.** He puts his head down and sprints, shoving whoever is in the way. 7.6%.
- **Up special: Climb It Anyway.**
  - He hops and grips as a pole shoots up under him. The buzzsaw on top spins just over his head and hits whoever is above: 4% about every tenth of a second, the standard spin hits.
  - He rises about 150 px with little drift: up, not out.
  - No armour. In canon the saw got him every time.
  - If his own pole is right beside him, he climbs to its top instead and gets his air jump back.
- **Down special: Go Through Him.**
  - He paws the ground (10 frames), then runs 180 px straight ahead.
  - The first foe he meets is knocked flat: 8% plus a knockdown. A foe bracing in armour goes down too, as with every knockdown in the game.
  - Anyone else in the way is just shoved aside: 4%.
  - He stops at ledges. If he runs into a wall, he bonks and is stunned for 20 frames.
  - No armour for him. Cooldown 70 frames.
- **Smash: Not Leaving.** While it charges he digs in, and sand and kelp cling to him. Then it bursts all round him. 24% on the card, slows them. Radius 60. A miss roots him for 26 frames.
- **Finisher: Hot Dog Recovery Center.**
  - He stamps impatiently (12 frames). His Recovery Center grows in just ahead of him, harmless while it grows.
  - Once it is whole, the door bangs open and flings whoever is in front of it high: 8%, nearly straight up.
  - A teammate in front gets the same lift with no damage. That is what it did for Beach Ball in BFDIE 1, flinging them to safety near a pole top.
  - Afterwards the box stays for 3 s and you can stand on it. Foes break it with 12% of hits.
- **Final Smash: Twelve Poles.**
  - He climbs his pole during the 14-frame wind.
  - Eleven more poles shoot up outward both ways, one after another, each with a buzzsaw on top. They throw whoever they come up under.
  - 13% (26% as a Final Smash). You can jump between the poles.
- **Passive, only if he tests low: Recovered, Even Angrier.** Each lost stock gives Go Through Him and Climb It Anyway 10 more frames of armour, up to 30. This uses the comeback table you already have.

**BFDIE links:**

- **Sidewalky and David** (and anyone weighing 110 or more) sink his pole by landing on it. Sidewalky is the bully he wouldn't vote for.
- **Fern:** Go Through Him is the "go through me" moment. Hot Dog voted for Fern and cheered him on, so his results line over Fern is grudgingly friendly.
- **Money** begged him to switch poles, and later dragged him off the ocean floor with a pipe.
- **Beach Ball:** the Recovery Center door that saved them in BFDIE 1 becomes his teammate lift.
- **Needy:** on a team, her doses refill twice as fast while she rests on top of his pole. In BFDIE 1 she won a spot by lying on a fallen pole.
- **The Cheaters' Alliance** (Rose, Beach Ball, Sticker): he hates cheaters, so he fights on your side in Jammy's event.
- **Ruler** claimed the first pole. Anyone can stand on Hot Dog's pole top, Ruler included.
- The black syringe on his pole is not Needy's. The wiki keeps them apart, and so do we.

**CPU:**

- Plants the pole when a foe is 60 to 200 px ahead on his floor. Never near an edge he would have to cross.
- Kicks a trap only while touching the base, with a foe beside the pole.
- Uses Go Through Him on a foe 60 to 180 px ahead, with no wall or ledge in between.
- Climbs to recover, or as an anti-air.
- About a dozen lines of new CPU code.

**Balance.**

- Aim: mid-pack.
- Strengths: he owns a spot with the pole, his tackle opens one combo, and he has a heavy back air and spike.
- Weaknesses, kept on purpose: he is slow, his body is big, and he has no projectile. Each trap costs a press, and he has to be touching the pole. The pole can be stood on, broken or sunk. His climb goes up, not out, so edged stages worry him.
- If he tests low, raise his run or recovery first, then turn on the comeback armour.
- Watch for pole camping at a ledge. The pole lasts 5 s, then the cooldown starts.

## 2. Needy (she/her): Lazy hexer

**Who she is.** Needy placed 5th. She is an armless, horizontal syringe, half full of a pink serum she can't explain ("I dunno").

- At first she was lazy, apathetic and amoral. She won a pole by lying on a fallen one, got Money eliminated, and took the Announcer's example necklace for immunity.
- In the BFDIE 3 cold open she injected Beach Ball. They wobbled, turned evil and purple, slammed her, then crashed.
- She can walk on water and suck up liquids, but her glass barrel breaks easily.

In Smash Island she is a light, patient trickster. The pink level in her barrel is her meter. Standing still refills it, and she spends it on the Evil Serum.

**Stats:**

- Weight 72.
- Run 0.92 · air 1.05 · fall 0.94 · damage 1.
- Recovery 1. Her jet, with a dose, is her reach.
- Long, low body. The needle tip can't be hit.
- Plain movetech: short hop, fast fall, a plain wall jump. No cling, because glass doesn't grip.
- Doses: 0 to 4. She starts at 2 (half full, as in canon).

**Art.**

- Picture: her BFDIE intro pose (NeedyPoseIntro.png), which is armless. Never BFB's "Syringe".
- Animation: rigid glass, so no squash. She only leans. The plunger slides, and the pink level rises and falls.
- Props: a water column and a sandcastle.
- Her Final Smash needs new serum art.

**Moves:**

- **Jab: Lazy Poke.** She barely bothers. 6%.
- **Side-tilt: Long Poke.** Her needle goes out long and thin. 5.6%.
- **Up-tilt: Plunger Pump.** She tips onto her needle end and pumps her plunger up and over. 4.8%, pops them up.
- **Down-tilt: Drip.** A sticky pink drop at their feet. 3.6%, slows them for half a second.
- **Neutral air: Barrel Roll.** She rolls over and over like a dropped syringe. 4 hits of 2.6%.
- **Forward air: Point First.** Her needle goes straight ahead. 6.6%.
- **Back air: Plunger Kick.** Her plunger end knocks into whoever is behind her. 7.6%.
- **Up air: Squirt Up.** A little squirt of pink straight up. 4.5%.
- **Down air: Stuck in the Ground.** She drops needle-first (7 frames). 7.8%, spikes. If she hits the floor instead, her needle sticks for a moment (16 frames of landing).
- **Grab: Nozzle Grip.** Suction at her needle holds them (reach 30).
- **Pummel: Sip.** 1.3%.
- **Throws:**
  - Forward, Squirt Off: 6.6%.
  - Back, Plunger Flick: 7.2%.
  - Up, Pump Up: 4.7%, sets up an up air.
  - Down, Bare Minimum: 4.4%. She just flops on them, and it turns out to be enough.
- **Special: Evil Serum.**
  - She draws her plunger back (9 frames) and lunges a short step. 5%.
  - With a dose (it spends one), the foe turns evil and purple for 2.5 s. They run and drift 15% faster and hit 15% harder.
  - For the last third of a second they wobble on their own body, as Beach Ball did. Then they crash: 9% and a knockdown.
  - The crash waits until they are on the ground, for up to 1 s more. If they are still in the air by then, it does 9% with no knockdown, so it never costs anyone a recovery.
  - No new injection until the crash has happened.
  - Teammates can never be injected, so nobody can grief a friend online.
  - Bosses take just the 5%.
  - Nobody can steal or strip evil.
  - With an empty barrel it is just a poke. Cooldown about 1 s.
- **Side special (stored only, while side specials are off): Walk on Water.** She skates along on the tips of her legs, shoving. 5.2%.
- **Up special: Plunger Jet.**
  - She squirts straight down and rides the jet up. Whoever is right below takes 4% and is slowed.
  - Below the stage edge, it spends a dose automatically (if she has one) and she rises about a fifth higher.
- **Down special: Draw Up.**
  - She tips her needle forward (6 frames) and sucks for half a second.
  - A foe touching the needle is drained: 3 × 2%. She heals 3% and gains a dose.
  - Any foe's trap or puddle in reach is sucked up and gone, for 1 dose. That means anything the game counts as a trap or puddle, such as Toothpaste's paste, Barf Bag's puddle or Jammy's splat.
  - Cooldown about 1.2 s.
- **Smash: Full Plunge.** While it charges she draws her plunger all the way back. Then she rams it home: a hard, straight squirt 320 px long. About 20% on the card, and it slows them for 2 s.
- **Finisher: Sandcastle Through the Water Column.**
  - She stabs the floor and draws up (12 frames).
  - A water column grows up just ahead, harmless while it rises. Once it stands, it lifts whoever is in it: 4%, pops them up.
  - A sandcastle forms at her feet. She kicks it through the column, and it arcs at the nearest foe ahead (up to 420 px), then bursts into sand: 9%, slows them.
  - Watch the castle in the air. This is her BFDIE 7 trick.
- **Final Smash: Draw Up Everything.**
  - She inhales until her barrel glows (14-frame wind).
  - For two-thirds of a second, everyone near her is pulled toward her needle. Walking away faster than the pull gets you out.
  - Then she plunges: 21% (42% as a Final Smash), and they are injected: evil for 2.5 s, then the crash. The crash is never doubled.
- **Passive: Why Move?**
  - After 1.5 s standing still on the ground with no keys pressed, she gains a dose, then one more every 1.5 s, up to 4.
  - The pink level in her barrel shows it. No HUD, no words.
  - On top of a teammate Hot Dog's pole, it is twice as fast.
- **Weakness: Glass Barrel.** She is light, and if you agree she joins the glass list, so Marshmallow's Shattering Scream does 17% to her.

**BFDIE links:**

- **Money:** arch-enemies. Needy got Money eliminated, and Money hit her with Hot Dog's body. Results lines go both ways.
- **Beach Ball:** the only one ever injected. Her wobble-then-crash comes straight from their BFDIE 3 cold open.
- **Toothpaste:** "Needy's my enemy now, she looked at me funny." Draw Up can suck up Toothpaste's traps for 1 dose each.
- **Rose:** the rival who made her care. Rose's existing Shh! already switches Needy's specials off for 3 s.
- **Sidewalky:** her Team Death partner in the three-legged race.
- **Hot Dog:** the pole-top refill, as a team bonus.
- **Jammy:** dressed her up as Ruler, which could be a skin later. Jammy's Cracked Jar splat is a puddle Needy can drink.
- **Needle** already says "Don't call me Needy." Needy's results line over Needle: "It's Needy."

**CPU:**

- Injects a foe within 50 px when she has a dose.
- Backs off about 150 px during their rush, and comes back when they wobble. That is the same cue a human sees.
- Stands still to refill when nobody is within 260 px.
- Uses Draw Up when a foe touches her needle or a trap or puddle is in reach.
- Uses the jet to recover.

**Balance.**

- Aim: mid-pack trickster.
- Strengths: with a dose she has a planned 14% plus a knockdown. Draw Up heals her and cleans traps away. Her beam smash is long, and her recovery is good with a dose.
- Weaknesses, kept on purpose: she is light glass with a slow run and short reach. The serum makes her target stronger for 2.5 s, so she has to survive that. Her doses run out, and refilling means standing still.
- If she tests low: one dose every 1.2 s, or evil lasting 2 s so the crash comes sooner. Never change other fighters to fit her.

## 3. Sticker (they/them): Dirty glider

**Who they are.** Sticker placed 3rd in BFDIE, and they win in an alternate ending. They are non-binary.

- They were bouncing on a trampoline with their best friend Beach Ball when the Announcer crashed in. They used the trampoline instead of climbing their pole, and launched Beach Ball into space with a giant rock.
- They are the strong fighter of the Cheaters' Alliance. They beat Beach Ball in the fighting contest by pumping them up until they popped.
- They chased Toothpaste with a knife and slipped on toothpaste twice. Toothpaste: "STICKER! You're my enemy now!"
- They secretly swapped slips with David, swooped in on Sidewalky's speck of sand, and called Fern a friend just before kicking him.
- Canon gives them two traits: lightweight (they glide and fall slowly) and fragile (paper rips easily).

In Smash Island they are a light, floaty glass cannon, with a pump, a trampoline, a knife, and the one buff-stealing move in this set.

**Stats:**

- Weight 60, with Needle (59) and Bubble (60).
- Run 1 · air 1.08 · jump 1.06 · damage 1.
- Fall 0.88, tied with Balloony for the floatiest.
- Recovery 1.
- Plain movetech: short hop, fast fall, a plain wall jump, and a 0.7 s wall cling (a sticky back). Stick the Landing jump-cancels on hit.

**New status: Inflated.**

- For 3 s the foe is puffed up and falls 15% slower, so they hang in the air and drift farther. Knockback is unchanged.
- They are drawn a little rounder, with a small balloon status icon. Their hurtbox does not change, so the picture never lies.
- Inflatable fighters (Balloony, Bubble, Puffball, Basketball, Beach Ball) get exactly the same effect. They just look puffier.

**Art.**

- Picture: their BFDIE infobox image (Sticker_idle.png). Their colours get sampled from it.
- Animation: paper flexes, so a little squash, a bend on swings, and a gentle sway while falling.
- Props: bicycle pump, trampoline (also used for the pad), knife, giant rock.
- Their Final Smash needs new trampoline art.

**Moves:**

- **Jab: Corner Slap.** They slap with their peeling corner. 7.6%.
- **Side-tilt: Edge Slice.** A quick slice with their paper edge. 6.2%, startup 5.
- **Up-tilt: Peel Up.** They peel a corner up and over. 4.7%, pops them up.
- **Down-tilt: Walk All Over You.** They stomp across the foe's feet and carry on a step forward. 3.6%, pops them up.
- **Neutral air: Loose Flutter.** They flutter round like a sticker caught on the wind. 4 hits of 2.5%.
- **Forward air: Sticky Side.** 6.2%.
- **Back air: Backing Paper.** They whip their backing paper out behind them. 7.5%, their strongest air hit.
- **Up air: Corner Flick.** 4.4%.
- **Down air: Stick the Landing.** They go flat (7 frames), then drop and stick to whoever is below. 6.8%, spikes. Jump-cancels on hit.
- **Grab: Make Friends** (reach 32).
- **Pummel: Peel.** 1.3%.
- **Throws:**
  - Forward, Walked Over: 6.4%.
  - Back, Betrayal: 7.3%. "Friends!", then a kick.
  - Up, Up You Go: 4.5%.
  - Down, Stuck Flat: 4.3%.
- **Special: Bicycle Pump.**
  - They plant a pump and draw up the handle (10 frames), then push. A jet of air shoots straight ahead, about 90 px.
  - 5%, plus Inflated for 3 s.
  - A second pump refreshes the time; it never stacks.
  - Cooldown about 1.1 s.
- **Side special (stored only, while side specials are off): Slip Switch.** A quick sidelong dash past a foe. 5%.
- **Up special: Trampoline Cheat.**
  - A trampoline flips out under them and bounces them high, as high as Fern's Pole Jump. It hits nobody.
  - Cast on the ground: the trampoline grows in harmlessly, then stays for 2 s. Anyone who lands on it bounces just as high, whether Sticker, a teammate or a foe. Anyone weighing 110 or more breaks it instead (Sidewalky broke it in one bounce).
  - Cast in the air: no trampoline, and the bounce ends in a short float.
  - Only one trampoline at a time.
- **Down special: Swoop and Swipe.**
  - They go flat and crouch (8 frames), then swoop forward low, about 130 px.
  - The first foe they touch takes 5%.
  - If that foe has a buff from one of their own moves running, Sticker takes it for whatever time it had left (up to 3 s). That means armour from a move (not from an up special), a charged-up next hit, or healing over time.
  - If the foe is holding a throwable, Sticker takes that instead.
  - Sticker never takes:
    - item power-ups (Attack Up, Speed Up, star, Yoyle metal)
    - counter or reflect stances
    - Needy's evil
    - a Final Smash glow
  - A miss leaves them open for 16 frames. Cooldown about 1.3 s.
- **Smash: Knife Chase.**
  - While it charges they raise a knife, then they lunge. 21% on the card, plus bleeding, launches far.
  - If the lunge touches nobody, they slip, as they always did: they lie flat on their back for the miss time, and the knife clatters harmlessly to the floor.
- **Finisher: Pump Until It Pops.**
  - They raise the nozzle (12 frames) and jam it on the foe right in front of them (40 px).
  - Three pumps of 2.4% while the foe swells up, then POP: 4.8% and a burst away. About 12% in all.
  - The foe can mash to wriggle out sooner.
  - A miss leaves them open. Bosses take one plain 12%.
- **Final Smash: Off the Trampoline, Into Space.**
  - They heave a giant rock overhead (14-frame wind).
  - The nearest foe ahead, and anyone right beside them, are standing on a big trampoline. The rock slams onto its far end and catapults them straight up.
  - 24% (48% as a Final Smash).

**BFDIE links:**

- **Beach Ball:** best friend and trampoline partner. The pump beat them in canon; here it only makes them look puffier, with no extra damage.
- **Rose:** fellow Alliance member who popped Beach Ball and burned Sticker. They get results lines. The Alliance story itself is Jammy's event.
- **Jammy:** Sticker dumped her off Team Dandelion, and she stamped on them in the Ruler costume. Results line: "Nobody's listening, Jammy."
- **Toothpaste:** her paste and traps already catch Sticker mid-lunge by ordinary rules. That is the canon slip, with no extra code. Her results line over Sticker: "STICKER! You're my enemy now!"
- **David** got slip-switched, and the knife that hit his head becomes a results quip.
- **Sidewalky, Fern, Money and David:** Swoop and Swipe can take buffs from their moves, such as Sidewalky's Heavy Brace, Fern's healing, Money's Bank It and David's Big Armor. It is the only new answer to buffs in this set, and it is melee and dodgeable.
- **Hot Dog:** results line over Sticker: "Cheater."

**CPU:**

- Pumps when a foe is 30 to 100 px ahead, near their height, and not already Inflated.
- Swoops only when a foe within 130 px ahead shows a buff from a move or is holding a throwable. Never as a blind approach.
- Uses the trampoline to recover or to escape a corner.
- Uses Knife Chase on a grounded foe within 110 px, with no paste or trap in between.
- Uses the finisher within 40 px.

**Balance.**

- Aim: mid-pack glass cannon.
- Strengths: great movement, the steal, a 21% knife smash, a strong back air and back throw.
- Weaknesses, kept on purpose: weight 60, so they are KO'd early. Their paper reach is short, the pump takes 10 frames to start, a missed knife or swoop leaves them open, and foes can use the trampoline too.
- If they test low: pump cooldown down from 66 to 58 frames, Knife Chase knockback up from 14 to 15, then damage 1.05.
- If they test high, buff the fighters below them instead.

## 4. Jammy (she/her): Whistleblower

**Who she is.** Jammy is a meek glass jar of peach jam ("Oh, thank Peach!"). She tied for 8th–9th.

- She is one of the smartest contestants, and the only one who saw how bad the prize was.
- She keeps trying to expose the Cheaters' Alliance, starting with Sticker's trampoline. Nobody listens.
- Sidewalky bullies her.
- Ruler caught her when she fell, and she became his henchman.
- She wore a cardboard Ruler costume and stamped on Sticker, and she threw snowballs at Rose.

In Smash Island she is a sticky zoner. Her jam gives foes sticky feet, her glass cracks and spills jam, and her shout pushes people back.

**Stats:**

- Weight 74.
- Run 0.95 · air 1.05 · jump 1.04 · damage 1.
- Recovery is measured. I expect 1.15, because her recovery is a hop with a pause, plus a cling.
- Plain movetech, plus a 0.5 s wall cling (sticky jam). No cancels.

**New status: Sticky feet.**

- While it lasts, a jump from the ground is only about a short hop.
- Air jumps, wall jumps and up specials are untouched, so it can never cost anyone a recovery.
- It shows as a small amber jar status icon.
- It openly shares the short-jump effect Golf Ball's sand uses, but only for jumps from the ground.
- The name is "sticky feet" so it never reads as Remote's "jam".

**Art.**

- Picture: her BFDIE infobox image. The opaque version (Jammynotseethrough.png) reads better on busy stages (question 17).
- Animation: rigid glass, so no wobble. She leans, and the jam shows through colour.
- Props: her lid and the cardboard costume.
- Her Final Smash needs new art of the Announcer as the BFDI Speaker Box, matching the boss.

**Moves:**

- **Jab: Lid Bonk.** She tips forward and bonks with her lid. 7%.
- **Side-tilt: Jar Nudge.** She leans in and nudges her jar ahead. 6.2%, startup 5.
- **Up-tilt: Twist Off.** She twists her lid up in an arc ahead of her. 4.8%, pops them up.
- **Down-tilt: Toast Spread.** She spreads jam at their feet. 3.6%, pops them up, sticky feet for 1 s.
- **Neutral air: Jar Rattle.** She spins with the jam sloshing round inside. 4 hits of 2.6%.
- **Forward air: Glass Shove.** 6.2%.
- **Back air: Back Slosh.** Jam sloshes out behind her. 7.4%, her strongest air hit.
- **Up air: Screw Top.** She twists her lid straight up. 4.5%.
- **Down air: Heavy Bottom.** She tips upside down (7 frames), then drops jar-bottom first. 7.4%, spikes. No cancel: glass lands heavy.
- **Grab: Henchman's Grip** (reach 30).
- **Pummel: Jam Dab.** 1.4%.
- **Throws:**

  - She is caught and held for a beat (6 frames, her startup), then heaved straight up. The lift is between Rose's and Paper's.

  - She points and draws herself up tall (14 frames), then shouts. A low wave of sound rolls along the floor ahead of her, about 180 px. You can jump it.
  - On hit: 5%, and they are pushed away from her.

  - It never touches anyone's buffs.

  - Cooldown about 1.2 s.
- **Smash: Pop the Lid.** While it charges she grips her lid and strains. Then she wrenches it off and jam erupts all round her. 22.5% on the card, sticky feet for 2 s, radius 58. A miss roots her for 24 frames.
- **Finisher: Ruler Costume March.**
  - She pulls a cardboard costume on (12 frames), then marches stiffly ahead for 1 s, up to 150 px. She can't turn.
  - She stamps whoever is under her front foot: 3 stamps of 4%, and the last one knocks them down.
  - A hit on her knocks the costume off and ends it.
  - The costume looks like Ruler only once you have unlocked him. Before that, it is plain cardboard.
  - Bosses take one plain 12%.
- **Final Smash: The Announcer Finally Listens.**
  - She points with all her might (14-frame wind).
  - The nearest foe ahead, and anyone right beside them, are caught. The Announcer swoops in, zaps them 5 × 3%, then fires them away for 17%, doubled like every Final Smash.
  - It is the one time anyone listens to her.
- **Passive: Cracked Jar.**
  - A single hit of 8% or more cracks her jar, and a splat of jam drops at her feet.
  - It grows in harmlessly over 12 frames, and never under anyone standing on that spot.
  - For the next 1.5 s, the first foe to step in gets sticky feet for 1 s. The attacker can be caught too.
  - No damage. Only one splat at a time.
  - If you agree, she also joins the glass list (Marshmallow's scream does 17%).

## What the review changed

I applied every one of the reviewer's 31 points. None were rejected outright; three were done in a different way from the one suggested, and they are marked "Done differently" below.

- **Online.**
  - The new timers and Needy's dose count now ride the host's snapshot, and guest movement now accounts for them.
  - The trampoline is now a spawned object, busy the way the spring is.
  - Done differently: the review asked for new result fields for tokens. Instead, the token rule now uses only place or team result, which the results already carry.
- **Events.**
  - There is now one schedule, one token rule, and one stage per event.
  - RUNNING! is out entirely.
  - The first version uses only existing stages and twists the CPUs already handle. The big ideas moved to "Later".
  - The carried Hot Dog, glows drawn on bodies and the shell counter are all gone from the first version.
- **Jammy.**
  - Call Them Out is now a slow shout wave you can jump (180 px), and it never stuns anyone in the air.
  - Done differently: the review asked for only one new answer to buffs in the set, and picked Sticker's swoop because it is melee and dodgeable. I agreed, so Call Them Out no longer touches buffs at all, rather than pausing them as Soap's move does.
  - "The Alliance is always caught" is gone, because it nerfed Rose.
  - Clashing move names were renamed.
  - "Jam" became "sticky feet", and the design now says openly that it shares Golf Ball's short-jump effect.
  - Pop the Lid went down to 15 damage and 13 knockback.
  - The Ruler cameo appears only after Ruler is unlocked.
  - The Cracked Jar splat can catch the attacker once it has grown in.
