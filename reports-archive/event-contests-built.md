## Shared pieces (for the next builder)
- A contest rule may have `winText(f)` and `score(f)` (the win banner; how the rest rank). Cloudy's rule still uses the old defaults.
- `twists:['fast']` (or 'lowgrav', 'dark', 'gusts') on a rule: `twistOn()` answers true for it, so the row keeps `twist:'contest'` and `eventMatchReady` is untouched. `contestIs('rule')` says whether this is that rule's match.
- `world(c, layer)`: art in the world under or over the fighters. `contestPanel(...)`, `contestPill(...)`, `contestWalk(...)` are the shared panel, head pill and CPU walk.
- Goal line: `EVENT_TWIST_WORDS['contest:<rule>'].goal`.

## Clock: Fast Forward (REBUILT 2026-11-13 to the owner's concept; branch g-ct-clock2 on 958654b1; not pushed)
- Why: the first version (stand in a moving light to run your own clock down) was not the owner's idea. The owner's words: a zone you stand in for points, a timer each that goes down, clocks outside the zone that give time back, a hit takes time off, the clocks spawn less and less; later: the match is fast, most points wins.
- What: Goiky Field, A-twos-ment Park behind, you vs Clock, Ice Cube and Cake, 3 stocks, items off, the fast twist on.
- How it plays: a 300 px zone in the middle of the floor gives 1 point per 6 frames while you stand in it. Everyone has a 60 s timer that always counts down (cap 90 s). Clocks grow in on the floor or a platform outside the zone (at most 3, never from the sky, they do not time out): +10 s. A hit takes 2 s off (a combo is one hit). The gap to the next clock grows by 0.75 s with each one (3 s, 5 s, 8 s, 12 s ...), so they come less and less. Most points wins. HUD: points, a timer bar and the time for each fighter, seconds over each head, the 5 s goal line 'Stand in the zone for points. Grab clocks for time!'.
- Defaults for what the owner did not say (all in the CK table beside the rule): at 0:00 you are out of the contest (no more points or clocks, points kept) but you stay on stage (CK.atZero 'stay'; 'out' removes you); the match ends when every timer is out or at 3:00 (CK.time); a fighter out of stocks is off the stage and cannot win.
- Checked: 10 CPU-only runs 52-98 s (52, 98, 56, 60, 58, 63, 64, 98, 62, 52; mean 66 s); every one ended by all timers running out (none by the cap or by one left on stage) and the winner had the most points in all 10; 0 errors; no CPU still for 5 s+ outside the zone. Scripted human: won 1 of 3 (108 s, 675 points); in the other two it had the most points (565, 558) but was KO'd out of all 3 stocks and a stock-out cannot win.
- Open questions for the owner: (1) at zero, do you leave the stage, or only stop scoring (default: stay on stage, out of the contest)? (2) How does it end: when every timer is out or at 3:00 (default; the 3:00 cap was never reached, matches end in about a minute at these numbers), or when one fighter is left? (3) Should a fighter who loses all 3 stocks but has the most points still win (default: no, they are off the stage)? (4) Should a KO also cost time or points (default: no, a KO costs a stock only)? (5) The pace numbers (60 s timer, +10 s a clock, -2 s a hit, zone 10 points a second) are mine; the first guess (30 s, -3 s a hit) ended matches in 20 s.
