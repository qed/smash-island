# Plays that work

Every play below was found, then checked a second time on the live game (main at fbc9009, after the balance pass). The checks used a simulated player who reacts like a person (about 0.2-0.3 s late) against Expert CPUs. The foes are named in each line. Only plays that held up are listed.

Keys use the game's own names: ← → move, ↑ jump, X attack, ↑+X / ↓+X tilts, C special, ↑+C / ↓+C up and down special, V smash, X+C grab (then an arrow throws), V+C finisher. "Toward" means ← or → pointing at the foe. "Combo" means the foe can't do anything between the hits once the first hit lands. The game runs at 60 frames a second, so 15 frames is a quarter second.

## Best 10

1. **Lightning: Chain Bolt edge-guard** (a KO, foe at 70%+). Stand at the edge and tap C at the recovering foe. KO 20/20 at 80-100%, 13-16/20 at 70%. Doing nothing: 0-1/20.
2. **Tree: grab, down throw, Catapult** (0-30%). X+C, a fresh ↓, then V+C. Ruler 20/20, Loser 19/20.
3. **Tree: Root Trip into Catapult** (0-30%). ↓+X, wait a beat, V+C. Ruler and Loser 20/20. Ruler still 15/20 at 35-50%.
4. **Lightning: Ground Arc into Lightning Rod** (0-45% with a step). ↓+X, then ↑+X. Ruler 19/20, Loser 20/20.
5. **Coiny: Coin Skid into Backhand Rise** (0-30%). ↓+X, then ↑+X. Ruler 19/20, Loser 20/20.
6. **Fries: Low Fry into Fry Flick** (0-30%). ↓+X, then hold toward and press ↑+X. Ruler 19/20, Loser 20/20.
7. **Bow: Tower into Chair Slam** (0-40%, 25%). V+C from 180-260 px, then ↑+C up close. Match 20/20 at 10%, Coiny 19/20, Nickel 17/20 at 30%.
8. **Bracelety: Power Up, Wrist Tap, 20% jab** (0-40%; a KO at the ledge from 25%). ↓+C first, then ↓+X, step in, X. Nearly every Wrist Tap that landed was followed up: Pen 7/7 and 11/12, Coiny 9/9.
9. **Puffball: Flight into a powered puff** (any %, 21%). C, fly level with them, X. Match and Coiny took the full 21% 20/20 times.
10. **Loser: Ice Skid into smash** (0-45%, about 28%; a KO at the ledge from 30-40%). ↓+X, then V straight away. Every landed skid was followed up in two of three runs (Pen 7/7, Coiny 6/6, Pen 9/12).

Plays 2-6, 8 and 10 are true combos, but the first hit reaches only about 30-75 px, and landing it on an Expert is the hard part: for most of them, half the tries or fewer landed. Two more plays work almost every time, but only on CPUs: **Ruby's Gem Trap** and **Blocky's Trap** (↓+C, then wait; the Expert walks onto it 16-20 times in 20). A person will just walk around them.

## By fighter

### Blocky
- **Trap + Prankster's Switcheroo** (a sure 20% at any %, never a KO, even at 130%). ↓+C (Trap) when the foe is 120-300 px away, wait at least half a second for it to arm, then V+C when they are within ~290 px. The swap (8%) and the trap (12%) hit on the same frame. **vs Expert:** Coiny 15/20 at 40%, 19/20 at 130%. Nickel 7/20. Match 0-1/20. **Beaten by:** a foe who hits Blocky while he waits or winds up (Match almost always), staying 320+ px away or in the air, or stepping on the trap first.
- **Trap bait.** ↓+C when the foe is 120-300 px ahead, then stand behind it. 12%, arms in half a second, lasts 6 s, and the Expert walks onto it. **vs Expert:** within 4 s, Match 18/20, Coiny 20/20, Nickel 17/20. **Beaten by:** a person, who walks around it or jumps over it.

### Bow
- **Tower into Chair Slam** (combo, 0-40%, 25%). V+C (Topple the Chair Showroom) while the foe is on the floor 180-260 px ahead. When the crash lands (12%), walk in and press ↑+C (Chair Slam, 13%) once within ~40 px, about a quarter second after the crash. A very early press can be ignored. The crash stuns long enough for the slam, and the slam stuns again. **vs Expert:** Match 20/20 at 10%, 14/20 at 30%. Nickel 17/20 at 30%. Coiny 19/20 at 10%. 0/20 at 50%. **Beaten by:** hitting Bow during the 0.7 s stack (starting closer than 180 px gets her hit 14-19 times in 20), being in the air or 280+ px away. Above 40% the crash pushes them out of slam reach.

### Bracelety
- **Power Up, then the 20% jab** (a KO move). ↓+C (Power Up: armour for about half a second, and it banks her next jab with no time limit), then X on a foe in front. The jab goes from 8% to 20% and looks like any jab. KOs at the ledge from about 20%, mid-stage from about 70-80% on Pen and 60% on Coiny. **vs Expert:** Pen at the ledge at 40%: 6/10 (all 6 that landed KO'd). Mid-stage Pen 80%: 4/15 (4 of 5 landed). Mid-stage 60%: 4/15. **Beaten by:** her short jab reach (8-10 of 15 missed), and a whiffed jab spends the bank. Ledge openings against a moving Expert are rare.
- **Power Up, Wrist Tap, 20% jab** (combo, 0-40%; a KO at the ledge). With the jab banked: ↓+X (Wrist Tap), step toward, then toward+X 6-26 frames after the tap lands. As a rhythm, X about 14 frames after pressing ↓+X. The tap stuns for 28 frames and the 20% jab lands well inside it. At the ledge (foe within 120 px) it KOs Pen at 25-50% and Coiny at 10-50%. **vs Expert:** at 20%, Pen 7/20 (7 of 7 taps converted), Coiny 9/20 (9 of 9). Timed at 30%: Pen 11/20 (11 of 12). **Beaten by:** Wrist Tap's reach (8-13 of 20 missed). Above about 50% it stops linking.

Coin Skid stuns for 28 frames and Coiny is free after 14. **vs Expert:** Ruler 19/20, Loser 20/20, and every Coin Skid that landed was followed up. **Beaten by:** nothing once it lands. Coin Skid reaches only about 60 px.

Loser 13/20, but the Slap landed only 14 times in 95 tries. **Beaten by:** staying out of point blank.

### Fries
- **Low Fry into Fry Flick** (combo, 0-30%). ↓+X, then hold toward and press ↑+X. The step is needed: without it the Flick missed even at 0%. Low Fry stuns for 28 frames and Fries is free after 15. **vs Expert:** Ruler 19/20, Loser 20/20. **Beaten by:** nothing once Low Fry lands. It reaches about 70 px.
- **Fry Dart into Low Fry** (combo, 0-60%). From point blank, C, then ↓+X 4-6 frames later, typed as one quick roll. At 90% only within 4 frames. **vs Expert:** Ruler 9/20, Loser 14/20. Most landed darts converted, but the darts rarely hit an Expert (12 of 75 throws). **Beaten by:** jumping, or the darts hitting from more than ~60 px.
- **Carton cap into charged smash** (extra damage, never a KO). V+C lobs the carton. Let go of both keys, then about a quarter second later hold V and walk in. Release next to the capped foe after at least 0.4 s of charge. A capped foe is rooted, so the smash lands (18% + 4%), but it knocks the carton off and loses its 9% burst: net about 10-13% more. **vs Expert:** the smash landed Ruler 7/20, Loser 10/20. **Beaten by:** stepping out of the lob. A capped foe can still hit Fries while he charges.

### Lightning
- **Ground Arc into Lightning Rod** (combo, 0-30% standing, 0-45% with a step). From point blank, ↓+X, then ↑+X. Above about 30%, hold toward between them. You can press ↑+X early and hold it: the Rod comes out on her first free frame. The Arc stuns for 28 frames and she is free after 14. **vs Expert:** Ruler 19/20 at 0-50%, 12/20 at 50-60%. Loser 20/20. **Beaten by:** nothing once the Arc lands. It reaches only ~66 px, and from 50% it pops them over the Rod.
- **Chain Bolt edge-guard** (KO). Stand at the edge facing out, don't walk off, and tap C whenever the recovering foe is within ~250 px in front. Mashing is fine: after a hit it fires once every 1.2 s. The zap is instant (8%), and a stunned foe can't jump or up-special, so each zap uses up their recovery. **vs Expert, KO rate:** 100% 19-20/20, 80% 20/20, 70% 13-16/20, 60% 6/20, 50% 2/20. Doing nothing: 0-1/20. **Beaten by:** recovering from 260+ px out, or from below. Under 60% it is only chip damage.

## The owner's own plays

- **Bow's tower as a read, from range.** In your words: "I use bow's finisher as something that can be used to punish a read, but I get height, so I dont get punished for reading." The stack lifts her above most ground attacks, and a foe who jumps in at her is caught by the falling tower and dragged down (12% once they're on the floor). One caution from our checks: Match and Nickel still hit her during the stack when she started it closer than about 180 px, so it is safest from range, the way you use it.
- **Your ledgeguard.** In your words: "going to the ledge, and when opponents try to get back up, use a projectile, or if they jump, use a strong jab." Tested against Expert CPUs (ledgeguard-test.md): wins went from 37% to 50%, damage per off-stage trip from 1.48% to 2.24%, and KOs on trips with time to guard doubled (2.1% to 4.6%). The shots do most of the work, and the jab alone adds nothing, but together they are a real gain. Lightning's Chain Bolt is the best shot by far (0.55% to 3.35% per trip), because it aims itself and reaches foes above the ledge. Fries had the biggest win gain (31% to 69%). Expert CPUs come back high (below the ledge only 2% of the time), so fire when the shot will meet them, not whenever they're off stage. A smash (V) instead of the jab KOs more, but didn't win more matches.

In the CPU-only World Cups (world-cup-sim-potm.md), the Expert Bot chained **Spaggers!** (↓+X, which tangles and roots) into **The One and Only!** (V) for KOs. It took Marshmallow's stock at 45% (root, smash, smash), and Loser's at 94% (smash, root, smash, smash). This is what the CPU did, picked out by the play detector. ## Didn't work

- **Tennis Ball, spaced jab from just outside their reach:** not really judged, because the claim arrived cut off. The finder's own setup landed clean hits on Coiny 14/20, Nickel 12/20, Match 2/20. The idea is doubtful anyway: Match's and Coiny's own reach is as long as Tennis Ball's.
- **Lightning, Chain Bolt into Ground Arc as a 0-90% combo:** a combo only at 0-20%. After that the bolt carries the foe up and away, and the Arc misses. Live: 5/20.
- **Tree, Root Trip into Catapult as a 45-50% ledge KO:** the Catapult whiffs or doesn't KO there. The combo part works (above).
- **Teardrop, THE KICK as an edge-guard:** only 2/10 and 4/10 kicks landed on a recovering foe. The finder's high numbers counted foes who had already landed back on stage. The kick also has startup and a 1 s+ cooldown, though the claim said it had none.
- **Loser, Stay Cool then the 16% jab:** 2/15 at 90% mid-stage. Pen survives at centre stage at 84-90%. At the ledge his short jab almost never found an opening.
- **Weaker than first claimed (still listed above with the real numbers):**
  - Bow's tower must start from 180-260 px, not 90-220.
  - Toothpaste's glob into rocket is not a true combo and misses at 120%+.
  - Blocky's trap + Switcheroo and Toothpaste's glob into rocket almost never work on Match (0-2/20).
  - Teardrop's up throw kick KOs at 35% only right at the edge (0/8 live).
  - Bracelety's "54% mid-stage KO" came from a lighter Pen than the real one.
  - Loser's grab, down throw, smash fails if you react instead of timing it.
