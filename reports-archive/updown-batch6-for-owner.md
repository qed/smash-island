# Up and down smashes, batch 6: old against new (branch g-smash6, 2026-11-19)

Built and committed on the branch. NOT pushed, NOT merged, nothing is live. Please say yes, no, or "change this one" per move before anything merges.

## Who

15 fighters share the "Rising Lunge / Low Lunge" pair. 10 of them are public. These are the first 8 by name:
**Apple, Bracelety, Firey Jr., MeTag, Pickle, Poppy, Profily, Silver Spoon.**
Still on the pair after this: Trophy and Yin-Yang (public), and five event fighters (Eraser, Clock, Pie, Stapy, Foldy), not touched.

## The old pair (the same for all 8)

- **Up: Rising Lunge** - a wide swing over the head that launches straight up.
- **Down: Low Lunge** - a low sweep that hits both sides at once and sends them low and out.

## How to read the numbers

- "dmg / launch" is per hit. Every new hit is at or under the old one, and the most one foe can take from the whole move (burn included) is at or under the old damage. No move is shorter in frames than the old one.
- "frames" = startup (including the smash charge) / active / end lag, measured with the frame data script, the same way as every other row.

## Things you should know before saying yes

- **Hooks that could make a move stronger than the numbers say** (not proven safe, no win-rate run): MeTag's 2 s track, Profily's 3 s tag, Profily's 8 untouchable frames, Poppy's half-second stun, Apple's slip.
- **Look-alike check: 15 of 16 have no match.** MeTag's Padded Cell matches Fries' "Hands Off My Fries!" by shape and numbers (an upright hit on each side). The check cannot see that MeTag's throws the foe IN and Fries' hits close in, one side then the other. I listed it rather than change it. Apple's down was redesigned once on the way: my first version (a stamp in front and a bump behind) matched 38 old down smashes, so it is now Waaah!.
- **Range, where a move covers less than the old one:** MeTag up and Profily up hit only above them, not a foe standing beside them; Firey Jr. up hits a standing foe only right against him. Pickle up and Poppy up hit only in front. MeTag down and Firey Jr. down miss a foe right against them (the walls and the embers are a step away).
- **Pickle's up smash starts 7 frames later** (36 against 29). That is the idea of it.
- **Where the ideas come from:** what the game already quotes from the show wikis (Apple's "Where's my pony?", Bracelety's "Oh, my grip!", Pickle's catchphrase, Profily wanting to be remembered, MeTag and the cell, Silver Spoon and the palanquin, Poppy fixing things), plus my memory. Not checked against episodes. Waaah! (Apple crying), Kicks Up a Fuss and Blows His Top are from their personalities, not a scene.
- **I have not seen any of the 16 drawings** (no browser). They run with 0 errors.
- **Not done:** CPU matches with these moves, the online check, a win-rate run.
