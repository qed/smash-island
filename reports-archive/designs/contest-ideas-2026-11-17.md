# Contest ideas: making every event as cool as Sticker's (2026-11-17)

Every idea keeps the contest's own goal and rules (your designs). They add places, set pieces, pickups and ways to mess with each other.
In-match things are pictures and sounds, with no phase names. Pick by number, for example: "Cloudy 1, 4, 9 / Pie all / Clock none".

---

## Cloudy: The Collection (hold all 9 items for the hold time)
1. **Storm front**: a dark storm cloud chases whoever holds the most items. Rain slows them, and a lightning bolt (flash warning first) knocks one item loose.
2. **Rainbow bridge**: after each storm a rainbow appears. For 8 s it's a slide across the stage, and the Golden Seed sprouts where it ends.
3. **Sky islands**: the stage is several floating cloud islands that drift apart and together. Items left on a drifting island float away with it.
4. **Storm-charged hits**: stand on a thundercloud platform to charge up (you crackle). Your next hit makes the victim drop an item at once, not after 3 hits.
5. **Thief crow**: now and then a crow swoops at the leader and steals one item, then flies a loop of the stage. Hit the crow and it drops the item.
6. **Glazier's bench**: hail cracks the Window if you hold it. A cracked Window is worth nothing until you stand 2 s at the glazier's bench to fix it, out in the open.
7. **Overflowing jar**: when the Raindrop Jar is full you can pour it out to make a slippery puddle trap. Then it has to fill again.
8. **Swap pedestal**: drop one of your items on the pedestal and get whatever random item is sitting there. It's a gamble that skips a hunt.
9. **Lost and found**: items knocked off the stage come back in a lost-and-found box after 10 s. Opening it takes 1 s and you can be hit while you do.
10. **Wind gusts**: gusts (with leaf trails as a warning) blow loose items across the stage. You can catch them mid-air.
11. **Umbrella**: a rare pickup that blocks one lightning strike and the rain, but while it's open you can't attack.
12. **Leader glow**: the closer someone is to finishing their hold, the brighter their items glow, so everyone can see who to gang up on.
13. **Item backpack**: your items hang on your back as little pictures, so rivals can see exactly what you have.
14. **Hiding spots**: bushes where you can stash ONE item. It doesn't count toward your 9 while stashed, but nobody can steal it.

## Eraser: Spleef (you said "dont fix eraser" about its rules, so these only ADD things; skip any you like)
1. **Tile kinds**: rubber tiles bounce you up once before they break (a save), glass tiles crack loudly and break 0.5 s later, and heavy steel tiles survive two people.
2. **Stomp-through**: landing on a rival from above breaks THEIR tile too, so you both drop.
3. **Ink spill**: an ink bottle tips over (wobble warning) and the ink spreads along one layer. Inked tiles break twice as fast.
4. **Rubber stamps**: giant stamps slam down on tiles (shadow warning), breaking them and shoving anyone nearby.
5. **Doodle critters**: little pencil doodles walk along a layer, breaking tiles as they go. Hit one to erase it.
6. **Conveyor layer**: one middle layer slides sideways like a conveyor belt.
7. **Ruler drop**: now and then a ruler falls across a gap as a tilting bridge. It tips toward whoever stands on it.
8. **Eraser rain from the knocked-out**: knocked-out players float above as erasers and can drop one eraser crumb every 8 s, which breaks one tile. That gives them something to do.
9. **Footprint colours**: the tiles you break show your colour, so you can see who's been where.
10. **Shaving drift**: broken tiles drop shavings onto the layer below, making it slippery.
11. **Sharpener vortex**: at the bottom a pencil sharpener spins faster as fewer players remain. It's only the picture you fall into.
12. **Trapdoor tiles**: a few tiles look normal but are trapdoors that drop instantly. Once one goes, the ones like it are easier to spot.

## Spongy: Get Digging (dig for your moves, then the arena)
1. **Rock layers**: hard rock takes longer to dig through, but gems inside rock give a bigger arena buff.
2. **Water pockets**: dig into one and it floods your tunnel. You swim slowly, and Spongy-style sponge blocks soak it up.
3. **Gas pockets**: dig into one and it pops you back a few tiles (a puff of green).
4. **Worm tunnels**: old winding tunnels already dug. They're fast to travel, but no moves are buried in them.
5. **Treasure chests**: a one-time arena buff (armour for your first hit, or a speed boost).
6. **Fossils**: dig up all 3 bones of a fossil for an extra buff.
7. **Mole**: a mole digs random tunnels. Following it is fast but random.
8. **Cave-ins**: a rumble, then a section of dug tunnel fills back in with dirt.
9. **Hot deep layer**: the bottom of the grid is hot. Digging there costs you a bit of starting percent in the arena.
10. **Arena entry order**: the first one out picks their spawn spot in the arena (high ground, near a platform, and so on).
11. **Waiting room**: arrivals wait on a balcony over the arena with a punching dummy and see the others' exit doors open.
12. **Dig-proof bedrock maze**: bedrock walls you can't dig shape the grid into a maze, so routes matter.
13. **Arena dirt**: the arena floor has soft spots you can dig into once to dodge a hit.
14. **Mushroom glow**: glowing mushrooms in the dirt light your tunnel. They're decoration, and they show where people have been.

## 8-Ball: jawbreakers and 8 permanent assists
1. **Candy factory**: the stage is a candy factory where conveyor belts carry jawbreakers around.
2. **Flavour colours**: a jawbreaker's colour shows the kind of assist inside (fire, ice, heavy, sneaky), so you choose which to break.
3. **Giant jawbreaker**: needs much more damage, but holds a rare assist.
4. **Bowling**: hit a jawbreaker hard and it rolls into rivals like a bowling ball.
5. **Candy press**: a hydraulic press slams down (warning); any jawbreaker under it cracks at once. Drag them under it.
6. **Licking**: hold Down beside a jawbreaker to lick it. It slowly damages the jawbreaker and gives you a short sugar rush (speed).
7. **Melted candy floor**: sticky patches that slow you, and they spread when the fall starts.
8. **Shattering hail**: in the falling phase, big jawbreakers crack into smaller bouncing pieces.
9. **Wall 8-ball**: a giant magic 8-ball on the wall flips every 30 s and shows a picture (assists hit harder, jawbreakers are slippery, and so on).
10. **Flavour combo**: holding two assists of the same flavour makes their summons team up.
11. **Candy tunnels**: shelters from the fall, but jawbreakers can roll in.
12. **Gumball machine**: hit it to pop out a small normal item.
13. **Wrapper decoys**: some jawbreakers are empty wrappers. They break fast but hold nothing.

## Black Hole: endless climb (last one alive)
1. **The world falls in**: houses, trees and bits of Yoyleland fly past toward the hole. You can ride a flying chunk for a moment.
2. **Shockwave**: the hole lets out a pulse now and then (warning), pushing everyone up and out.
3. **Low-gravity bands**: bands of the sky where you float, for huge jumps.
4. **Wormhole pairs**: enter one to come out somewhere else higher up, but which one is random.
5. **Orbiting rings**: ring platforms that circle around a planet chunk.
6. **Slow time near the hole**: close to the hole, YOU move in slow motion (the event horizon).
7. **Falling stars**: grab one to refill your jumps once.
8. **Asteroid belt**: a wide band of slow asteroids you hop across.
9. **Satellites**: moving platforms with solar panels that fold up now and then.
10. **Spaghetti stretch**: near the hole you stretch out (drawing only) and drift toward it.
11. **Swallowed players aim comets**: knocked-out players pick where the next comet comes from.
12. **Crunching ledges**: ledges near the hole shake before they're eaten.
13. **Lifeboat rocket**: a rare rocket that blasts you up a few ledges, with a long warning trail so rivals can knock you off it.

## Clock: Fast Forward (zone time; clock pickups outside)
1. **Rewind**: a pickup that snaps you back to where you were 3 s ago, to escape a combo or a fall.
2. **Pause**: a pickup that freezes rivals for 1 s.
3. **Clock face stage**: the stage is a giant clock, and its hands sweep round as moving platforms.
4. **Zone on the hour**: the zone moves to the number the hour hand points at.
5. **Cuckoo**: on each "hour" a cuckoo bird bursts out of the wall and shoves whoever is in the zone.
6. **Alarm clock**: a pickup that rings and stuns everyone near you.
7. **Pendulum**: a giant pendulum swings through the zone (warning: its shadow).
8. **Gears**: floor gears turn and carry you along.
9. **Hourglass**: sand trickles into the zone, slowing whoever stands in it until a hit scatters it.
10. **Fast and slow halves**: one half of the stage runs fast and the other slow, and they swap now and then.
11. **Clock tower bell**: ring the bell (hit it) to knock everyone in the zone up into the air.
12. **Stopwatch race**: a rare golden stopwatch spawns far away. Whoever brings it into the zone gets bonus time.
13. **Broken clocks**: some pickups are broken and give no time, but they look a little off.

6. **Cream spills**: slippery steps.

10. **Pie cart**: a cart you can ride up a ramp, and others can knock you out of it.

13. **Dumbwaiter**: a tiny lift only one fighter fits in, so whoever gets in first takes it.

10. **Opposite day card**: flips the current rule.
11. **Hot dog stand**: a small heal.
