# Recovery types: proposal for the owner (2026-11-20, branch g-recov)

You said every up special has its own name and text "but they all feel the same". The headless audit agrees: of 117 public fighters, **65 do the same thing**: rise straight up 95-224 px in about 20 frames with one small hit (49 are a hop with one ring of hit on the cast frame, 16 a rising spinning aura). 8 more are the same hop with no hit. Nobody is helpless after (everyone can act on the next frame), everyone gets exactly one use per airtime, and everyone has the same 30 px ledge catch. Only 5 send foes down (Bow, Tissues, Tea Kettle, Beach Ball, Needy). Steering is just normal air drift for almost all of them.

Nothing here is merged. Batch 1 (12 fighters, marked "batch 1, built") is built on the branch g-recov for you to try; pick what stays.

## The types (one line each)
- **CHARGE** (9 fighters): hold C to power it (you sink while you hold); let go, or it fires by itself when full: longer = higher and harder. Timing matters.
- **AIM BURST** (10 fighters): a short hang while you aim with the arrows (8 ways), then a fast straight burst that hits whoever it passes; helpless after.
- **TELEPORT** (10 fighters): vanish (untouchable, not drawn), aim with the arrows, reappear far that way; little or no hit; helpless after.
- **TETHER** (10 fighters): reaches for the nearest ledge in range and reels you onto it; great near the ledge, only a small hop when nothing is in reach; hits along the line.
- **MULTI-HOP** (9 fighters): up to 3 smaller hops, each a fresh press of C, each steered; small hits each.
- **GLIDE** (3 fighters): a rise, then hold C to glide down slowly and far forward; X or letting go ends it.
- **HOVER** (6 fighters): hold C to hang at your height and drift for a while; bumps who touches you.
- **FLOAT** (7 fighters): a slow, steady, steerable rise while C is held; no hit; easy to hit out of.
- **CARRY** (10 fighters): a rising multi-hit that drags foes up with you, then launches them at the top.
- **SPIKE** (7 fighters): the top or the falling part sends foes DOWN: an edge-guarding tool; in the air the drop lets go so it is not a suicide.
- **COUNTER RISE** (4 fighters): a short stance at the start: hit in it and the hit does nothing, you rise higher and hit back; nothing came, a smaller ordinary rise.
- **ARMOURED RISE** (7 fighters): a slower rise with armour the whole way and a heavier hit at the top.
- **FAST LINE** (8 fighters): very fast and far along a fixed line, no steering once it goes; helpless after; predictable for an edge-guarder.
- **keep** (17): event fighters and a few others that already have their own kind.

- Loser's up special is now a counter, and his finisher (Effortless Catch) also catches. Too close? Needle could have the counter rise instead.
