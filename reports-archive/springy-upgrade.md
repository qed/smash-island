# Springy upgrade (2026-11-20)

The owner, after Live Boss Rush: "springy is the only bad thing; by using him as a model, we forgot that he was supposed to get better alongside them."

Springy keeps his concept and all four of his moves, unchanged. What the other bosses got and he did not, he has now.

Already level, so not changed: his ending, his body tells and shadows, his four show renders, his arena (boxes, fence, spring pad, holes).

## The two new moves (both from the show)

**TRAPPED IN A JAR!** (from phase 2). From A Jury of Your Fears: Springy "traps him in a jar, laughing as he shakes him around". A glass jar comes down from the top of the screen onto where you stand. Its shadow follows you during the wind-up, then stops. If it lands on you, you are trapped and shaken for about 1 second, then the jar breaks and throws you toward the middle: one normal boss hit. If it misses, it cracks for a moment, then throws one glass shard each way along the floor. Hop the shard, or stand further away. Phase 3: a second jar follows you after the first lands. It never comes down on someone who is already in a jar.

**GLITCHING!** (phase 3). From his page: he glitches when he gets too excited, because MeLife made him. Static gathers on four spots on the floor: one to each side of you, the spot where you stand, and a last one that follows you. He glitches out of each spot in turn, with a burst. The brightest static goes next. It hits you once at most.

## Fairness (the boss audit tool, 8 seeds, 4 start spots)

Every Springy move in every phase has a clean dodge. The new moves are clean at all 4 start spots in every phase they are in (oracle 4/4).

- TRY NOT TO FALL! (unchanged): the audit's short list of lines finds a dodge at 3 of 4 spots in phase 1 and 1 of 4 in phase 3. A wider search finds clean dodges at every spot (phase 1 left: 44 lines; phase 3: 42 left, 42 right, 6 middle). The same was true before this change.
- The phase 3 entry hole: fixed. A fighter standing on the crack who walks off 18 or 30 frames after it shows takes no hit. Standing still drops you in, as designed. The audit still logs a hit at frame 12 in phase 3. That hit is the tool itself: it moves you onto a hole that already cracked and opened while it was setting up. I did not change the tool.
- The audit's reacting bot rarely dodges JUST WANTED A HAND! (unchanged) or GLITCHING!. Scripted lines do dodge both.
