# Final Smash animations: the designs for you to approve (2026-11-02)

You asked for an in-character entrance animation on every Final Smash (Pencil's van is the model), a knock-back out of the blast where it fits, and a full Bow-style redesign for some. This is the design for each of the other 101; Bow's chairs and Pencil's van are already being built. Nothing below is built. **Reply with 'all OK' or list the changes.** The blast, damage and effects of each Final Smash stay exactly as they are now unless a redesign is approved.

- 101 fighters. Cost: 36 small, 61 medium, 4 large. Knock-back suggested for 30.
- Redesign candidates: 7 (Dough, Balloony, Profily, Teddy Bear, Paintbrush, Yellow Face, Loser).
