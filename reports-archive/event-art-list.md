# Event fighters: the pictures to download (list only, nothing downloaded)

Private report, not in git. 26 Oct 2026, for the event-fighters build (branch g-events).

This is every picture the 14 event fighters and the new assist bag need from the wikis, so you can say yes or no to each one by name.
I downloaded nothing: every number comes from the wiki's own file data (its imageinfo API). Because nothing was opened, no picture
on this list has been looked at yet. They were picked by their wiki captions, the gallery section they sit in, and their shape.
Please look at each one when you OK the download.

How to read the tables: **For** is what the picture is used for. **Pixels** is width x height. **Size** is the file on the wiki
(1 KB = 1,024 bytes, the way Windows shows it). **API** opens the wiki's data for that file, with its download address.

## What I could not find, and what I tried

How I searched: each character's gallery page (its captions and which section each picture sits in), the file search, and
"where is this file used" when a caption was missing. About 115 wiki calls in all, one a second. Nothing was downloaded.

- **Spongy's "suction frames".** Only one picture shows him sucking something up: `Spongy Sucking Stapy Up0024.png` (a BFB asset,
  from the gallery of The Escape from Four). Stapy is probably in it and would have to be cut out. The other suction pictures on the
  wiki (`Spongy Getting Sucked By 40001` to `0004`) show Spongy being sucked into Four, the wrong way round, and Four is a spoiler,
  so I left them out. If the one frame is no good, Soak Up can be drawn as his render swelling.

  his page captions it "Clock with red bruise on his cheek (TPOT 22)".

  BFB picture of the moment he comes out of his trance. Second choice: `Stapy ow.png` (2800 x 3060, 258 KB).
