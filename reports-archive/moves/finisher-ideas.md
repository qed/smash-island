# Finisher ideas: one per fighter (plan for your approval)

Nothing here is built yet. You said "finishers can be creative!" and picked "Yes, plan first", so this is the plan: one new finisher idea for each of the 102 fighters on the board. Say yes, no or "change it" to any row.

## What a finisher is

- **Finisher (V + C):** a strong move on a cooldown. Today 21 fighters have their own (74-frame cooldown) and they are mostly plain big hits. The other 81 share one 14% swing (3 s cooldown).
- **Final Smash:** the Smash Ball super. It is a different button and a different move. Every idea below is checked against that fighter's Final Smash, smash (V) and specials so it does not repeat them.
- **Your example**, for a hidden fighter: a big file they stand on. It moves in one direction and speeds up their attacks for as long as they stay on it. That is the model here. A finisher can be a ride, a field, a buff, a summon, a trap, terrain, a status or a combo starter, not just damage.

## The rules every idea follows

- It comes from who they are: BFDI or Inanimate Insanity canon, plus how their kit plays.
- It is readable: a clear tell or start-up, a way to dodge or answer it, no endless traps and no one-shots.
- It works in FFA, Teams, online and Boss Rush (bosses get a plain hit where a status would make no sense), and the CPU can use it.
- It is built from pieces the game already has wherever possible: projectiles, summons, platforms, belt/updraft/ice/glue zones, stun/slow/burn/poison/bleed/root, buffs, armour and teleports. Only 9 ideas need a new system (listed at the end).
- The three hidden prize fighters are left out on purpose.

## Ideas that need a new engine system (9)

Everything else above is built from pieces the game already has. These 9 need something new:

1. **Bomby, Hot Potato Fuse:** a status that passes from fighter to fighter on touch.
2. **Bell, Swinging From Her String:** swinging on a fixed anchor (pendulum movement).
3. **Lollipop, Sugar Rush:** an attack-speed buff. Your own file-ride example needs this same piece, so it gets built once and used twice.
4. **Saw, Cut a Hole in the Ledge:** switching a ledge off for a few seconds. The crumble zone is the closest thing the game has.
5. **Tennis Ball, Deuce Rally:** a ball that any fighter's attack sends back.
6. **Nickel (II), Roasted Live on Air:** damage when an attack misses.
7. **Test Tube, Shrinking Formula:** changing a fighter's size for a few seconds.
8. **Fan, Number One Fan Won't Let Go:** one fighter riding on another.
9. **Clover, Borrowed Luck:** a chance for a foe's hit to fizzle.

Smaller stretches of what exists, not new systems: summons people can stand on, built the way Spongy is (Bracelety, Book, Tree, Bow, Blueberry, MeTag); the ice slide on a single fighter (Gelatin); and a new Firey assist row for Firey Jr.

## Two questions before anything is built

1. **Cooldown for the long ones.** Many of these last 3 to 6 s, which is longer than today's 74-frame finisher cooldown. My suggestion: one at a time (using it again replaces the old one), with the cooldown starting when it ends. Is that OK, or should each one get its own cooldown?
2. **The 21 fighters who already have their own finisher** (Firey through Puffball, in board order): should their current big hits be replaced by these ideas, or kept for now?
