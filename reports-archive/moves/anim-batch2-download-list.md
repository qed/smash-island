# Batch 2 download list (animation plan)

This is the list to approve before anything is downloaded. **No image was downloaded or saved.** Every row was looked up through the wikis' own API: the file's name, pixel size, file size and upload date, which gallery section and episode lists it, and which pages use it. Nobody has looked at the pictures yet.

Sources: Inanimate Insanity wiki (inanimateinsanity.fandom.com) for Lifering, Candle, Cammy and Yin-Yang. BFDI wiki (battlefordreamisland.fandom.com) for Leafy, Needle, Snowball, Bomby, Bubble and Teardrop. The poses come from each fighter's row in `animation-plan.md`.

## In short

- **Every file name the plan gives exists**, under that exact name. But this time each fighter's pictures must all be one art style, matching the picture the game already uses for them. So 16 of the plan's files are **dropped** for being from a different season's style, or for showing the wrong character. Where a same-style file exists, it is listed in their place.
- **To download:** the core pictures are **46 files, 7.45 MB**. Everything listed (core plus alternatives, extras and one fix) is **72 files, 12.52 MB**.
- **The four Inanimate Insanity fighters** have 2024 or Season 4 pictures in the game. Season 3 art uses its own faces and limbs, so the plan's Season 3 pictures are dropped. Candle's are replaced by Season 4 episode frames, which need cutting out by hand. Yin-Yang has almost no Season 4 poses, so most of their moments are drawn.
- **The six BFDI fighters** have TPOT pictures in the game. The wiki says their BFB art is the same design as TPOT, so the BFB files stay. Three files changed:
  - Needle's BFDIA fighting pose is out, and a TPOT pose replaces it.
  - Needle's BFB 16 Walk is out (a CGI-body period, a different style from her TPOT render). Walk is optional in the plan, so it is skipped.
  - Bomby's BFB happy picture is a different colour from TPOT, so a TPOT one replaces it.
- **Some pictures already in the game are Season 3 art:** Candle's two swaps, Yin-Yang's three, and probably Lifering's first aid. One ready Season 4 replacement is listed (marked **fix**). Others are covered by new core pictures, and the rest would need drawing. You decide whether to swap them (see the end).
- **Good news:** Cammy's KO now has a real Season 4 frame. The plan had it as drawn.

**How to read the tables:**

- Each file is marked:
  - **core**: the pick.
  - **alt**: the other half of an "or" in the plan, or a same-style alternative. Pick one.
  - **extra**: more frames of a set the plan named.
  - **fix**: a same-style replacement for a picture already in the game.
- Pixels are the file's full size. The game shrinks pictures to 200px tall.
- "Episode frame" means a scene picture with a background or other characters in it, which needs cutting out by hand. "From the episode-scenes section" means the wiki files it with the scenes, so it may still have a background.
- "Dropped" files are the plan's picks that were left out. They are not counted in any total.

**How the style was judged** (from the wikis' own design notes, upload dates, and the gallery and episode pages; no picture was opened):

- **BFDI.** All six use TPOT pictures in the game: `Tpot renders0008/0015/0032/0043.png`, `TPOTLeafyColorCorrected.png`, and `Bubble 18 Stance 5.png` from her TPOT poses. Each fighter's "Changes" list on the wiki gives one design that runs through BFB and TPOT:
  - Leafy and Snowball: one design since late BFDIA.
  - Needle: since BFDIA 6, except for a CGI-body period (BFB 11 to TPOT 2), which is a different style from her TPOT render.
  - Bubble: since late BFDI.
  - Teardrop: BFB onwards.
  - So BFB and TPOT files are mixed freely. BFDI and BFDIA frames are left out, following your Book rule.
  - The exception is Bomby: his gradient is "even less purplish in TPOT", so his BFB pictures are dropped.
  - Snowball's limbs are green in TPOT 19-25, and no file from those episodes is used.
  - BFB was kept on purpose as core for Teardrop's Run (`Running so fast TD.png`) and Leafy's Hurt (`Angry Leafy.png`), where the plan named them. The TPOT alternative is listed next to each (`Td running away.png`, `LeafyOuch.png`). I left them as alt rather than swapping them to core.
- **Inanimate Insanity.** The game uses these pictures:
  - Lifering and Yin-Yang: their 2024 Pose renders.
  - Candle: the picture the wiki captions "Season 4 style".
  - Cammy: their Season 4 picture.

  The wiki's design notes for Lifering say Season 3 (Invitational, 2020-23) drew him with "Season 3 exclusive face and limbs", no shines and a pinkish red. The shines came back in the 2024 Season 2 finale and Season 4. So S3 art is treated as a different style. S4 and 2024 Pose art is the match. The 2024 Season 2 finale episodes (S2E16-18) are close but are marked "check". Lifering's 2024 render may be closer to his S2E18 look than to S4, so both S4 and S2E16-18 pictures of him are checked by eye against the render.
