# Smash thought review: which v2 smashes were made without thought (2026-11-15)

You said: "change the smashes of leafy and anybody else you stupidly changed without putting thought into it."

This is a design document only. Nothing in the game was edited, nothing was run, no picture was looked at.
It covers the three v2 sets that sit on the preview build (not live): 24 up/down smashes, 7 main smashes, 12 Final Smashes. 43 moves.

## The test

One question per move: **does it come from THIS character?**

- **THOUGHTFUL**: it comes from a show moment, a line, or a trait of this character.
- **OK**: it comes from what the object is, or from the theme of their kit. It fits, but another fighter of the same kind could have it.
- **THOUGHTLESS**: borrowed, a "default", a stock shape with a new skin, an effect bolted on, or it works against who the character is.

## Honest limits

- **I could not check any episode.** Every show source below is from memory. Where I am unsure I say so. Please correct me; do not take my sources as fact.
- The new numbers are on paper. Nothing was built or measured.
- "OK" is a judgment call. 24 of the 43 are OK. If you want more of them redone, name them; I did not redo them, because you said before not to change everything.

## Result: 6 THOUGHTLESS, 24 OK, 13 THOUGHTFUL

Four more are OK but need one small fix (marked "fix").

# The six THOUGHTLESS moves, old against new

(Sections 1 and 3 to 7 are the six. Section 2 is Leafy's down smash, which is OK, with options in case you want it changed too.)

Rule kept for every replacement: no number is higher than the move it replaces, and none is higher than today's live move. Nothing falls from the sky.

## 1. Leafy, up smash

**What went wrong.** The v2 design was a knife throw (from the show). It was then swapped for "leaf blades" as a default. Without the knives it is two leaves flung in a spread, and her main smash is already three leaves blown out in a fan.

| | Live today | v2 on the preview | 
|---|---|---|
| Name | Rising Volley (generated) | Leaf Blades |
| Shape | wedge over the head | two blades, up-left and up-right, gap overhead |
| Effect | none | none |
| Damage / launch | 15.8 / 11.8 | 13.7 / 10.5 |

Pick one of two.

### Option A: Ferris Wheel

- **Where it comes from:** the Ferris wheel on Dream Island that Leafy and Firey rode together, the memory she holds on to (from memory; I am fairly sure of the wheel, not of the episode).
- **Shape:** a wheel rim appears over her, 46 px in radius, its lowest point at her shoulder in front. One car rides the rim from that low front point up and over the top. It takes 9 frames. A foe the car touches on the way up is picked up and rides with it.
- **Effect:** the ride is a light hit that carries. At the top the car tips and throws the rider straight up. No burn, bleed or stun.
- **Numbers:** ride 4, tip 9.5 = 13.5 on a foe caught low; a foe met at the top takes only 9.5. Launch 10.5, straight up. 37 frames, same as now.
- **Fit with her kit:** her finisher also carries foes. This is the upward one. It does not hit behind her.
- **Not a look-alike of:** nothing else in the up/down set rides a circle. Bubble's Blow-Up is one bubble growing on the spot.

### Option B: Yoyleland Knife

- **Where it comes from:** Leafy throwing knives at the others from Yoyleland (BFDIA, the van episode; from memory, I am not sure of the episode). This is the show moment the first design had. It is dark for kind Leafy: your call.
- **Shape:** not the two-blade Y. ONE knife, thrown hard up and forward at a steep slant: a thin straight line 100 px long, starting at her hand. Nothing straight overhead, nothing behind.
- **Effect:** bleed 0.7 s.
- **Numbers:** 12.6 + about 1.1 bleed = 13.7. Launch 10.5, up and away. 37 frames.
- **Fit with her kit:** her only move that draws blood; it is narrow, so it must be aimed.
- **Not a look-alike of:** Knife's Rising Stab is his own body hopping up. Needle's and Pin's spikes are straight up. No other row is one slanted thin line with bleed (to be checked by script when built).

## 2. Leafy, down smash (OK, not thoughtless: only if you want it changed too)

Falling Leaf is a U-swing that carries a foe from behind her to the front and launches them. It is about a leaf, not about Leafy.

- **Option A, least work: keep the move, give it her reason.** Leafy is the one who catches and saves people (BFDI season 1, general; from memory). Same shape, same numbers (back 5, front 9.2, launch 10.3), but drawn as a rescue catch: she scoops the foe up from behind her in both arms and sets them down in front, a bit too hard. New name: "I've Got You!". Nothing in play changes.
- **Option B: Metal Leafy.** She eats a yoyleberry and turns to metal for a moment (from memory, and this is the source I am least sure of). She drops straight down with a clang. The hit is her own body and 40 px of floor each side, knocking foes away low and flat. No carry. One hit of 14.2, launch 10.3 (never above Falling Leaf's best case). 37 frames.
- **Option C:** leave Falling Leaf as it is.

## 3. Snowball, up smash

**What went wrong.** "He is the bottom ball, so stack a snowman on him." Snowball is the strong, angry one. He would hate being a snowman.

| | Live today | v2 on the preview | New |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name | Skyburst (generated) | Snowman | **Up You Go** |
| Shape | wedge over the head | three stacked balls, straight up | both fists heaved from chest height up to overhead, in front of him: one short, thick hit |
| Effect | none | none | none |
| Damage / launch | 15.8 / 6.0 | head 15 / 6.0, middle 13 / 5.6 | 15 / 5.9, up and BEHIND him |

- **Where it comes from:** he is the strongest, and he picks the others up and throws them (BFDI season 1, general; from memory).
- **Shape in detail:** a thick capsule from just in front of his chest to above his head, reaching about 96 px up (the snowman reached 107). It hits in front and above-in-front. It misses a foe straight behind him.
- **What is new:** the launch goes over his shoulder, behind him. He chooses the side by which way he faces.
- **Fit with his kit:** his up special is a rising uppercut that slows; this stays on the ground and is a throw.
- **Not a look-alike of:** Needle's slap is a thin crescent with stun for 8.2. No other up smash in the set throws backward.

## 4. Bomby, down smash

**What went wrong.** His special, his finisher and his main smash are all "he blows up and pays for it". Backfire is a fourth. The look-alike script matched it with 23 other moves, so it was given a burn to pass. That is an effect bolted on.

| | Live today | v2 on the preview | New |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name | Rooted Sweep (generated) | Backfire | **Stamp It Out!** |
| Shape | two low lines | one flat dome under him | three fast stamps at his own feet: front foot, back foot, then both |
| Effect | none | burn 1 s, costs him 3% | none, costs him nothing |
| Damage / launch | 26.2 / 11.0 | 22 / 10.6 | 5, 5, 9 (a foe on one side takes 14; pressed against him, 19) / 9.5, low and away |

- **Where it comes from:** he is terrified of fire (BFDI; confident on the trait). A spark drops from his fuse to the floor and he stamps on it in a panic.
- **Shape in detail:** a small patch 30 px in front (frames 4 to 6), a small patch 30 px behind (frames 9 to 11), then both at once (frames 14 to 17), which kicks foes away low. Reach about 55 px each side. 43 frames, same as now.
- **Fit with his kit:** his only strong move that does not explode and does not hurt him. Short reach and less damage pay for that.
- **Not a look-alike of:** Coiny's Penny Spin is one round patch centred on him that shrinks. This is two separate foot patches that alternate. Total is close (14 to 19 against 16.2), so the script must check this pair when built. Flower's stomps are specials that reach along the whole floor.

## 5. Firey Jr., main smash

**What went wrong.** The live move is called Tantrum Flare. v2 made it "Hot Foot": the dash-through shape that five other fighters already have, with burn. The design's own source line says "invented". The tantrum is gone.

| | Live today | v2 on the preview | New |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name | Tantrum Flare | Hot Foot | **Tantrum Flare** (name kept) |
| Shape | steps in and swings a flame (Firey's move, smaller) | a 135 px dash through the foe | he throws himself on his back and kicks: four fast low kicks just in front |
| Effect | burn | burn | burn, on the last kick only |
| Damage / launch | 12 / 9 | 12 / 9 | 2, 2, 2, then 6 = 12 / 9 on the last kick |

- **Where it comes from:** the move's own name, and that he is the small, loud one. A real tantrum, on the floor. (I do not know a show moment for it and will not make one up.)
- **Shape in detail:** one stamp as the tell (6 frames). Then a low box in front, his radius + 30 long and 30 px tall, for 16 frames. The first three kicks hold the foe in place; the fourth flares and launches. Burn time as now (90).
- **Cost:** he is left lying on the floor afterwards (the same 20-frame stun cost as now).
- **Fit with his kit:** his fireball, whirl and ring all reach out; this is the close one, and the only one that is a kid being a kid.
- **Balance:** same total, same launch, same burn. It reaches less than both the old swing's step and the dash, so it is not stronger.
- **Not a look-alike of:** Firey keeps the step-and-swing. No main smash I read is a low multi-kick. Puffball's smash is multi-hit too, so the script must compare those two when built.

## 6. Baseball, Final Smash

**What went wrong.** "He is a baseball, so he runs the bases." His whole kit says he is too heavy to get off the ground, and the design has him running up through the air as a blur. The design also asked you whether to drop the name Ground Pound; no answer came and it was renamed anyway.

| | Live today | v2 on the preview | New |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name | Ground Pound | Home Run | **Ground Pound** (name and idea kept) |
| Shape | shockwaves run along the floor both ways (Yin-Yang's shape) | runs a diamond drawn in the air, then slides | he rocks, manages one tiny hop, and lands so hard the floor dents under him |
| Effect | pure damage | pure damage | crater: pure damage. Rest of his floor: knocked down |
| Damage / launch | 18 / 22, everyone on the floor within 700 px | 18 / 22 once | crater 18 / 22, up and out. Rest of his floor out to 400 px: 4, no launch, knocked down |

- **Where it comes from:** his weight, which is what his special (Too Heavy), his up special (Heavy Hop) and his finisher are all about.
- **Tell:** 40 frames of rocking back and forth to get going. The hop is 30 px at most.
- **Shape in detail:** the crater is 130 px each side of him, on the floor he stands on, 60 px tall. Outside it, every foe standing on the same floor within 400 px is tipped over. Foes in the air are not touched.
- **How to dodge:** be in the air when he lands, or be on another platform.
- **Balance:** weaker than the live one: the full 18 now needs a foe within 130 px, where the live one gives 18 to everyone within 700 px.
- **Not a look-alike of:** Yin-Yang's waves travel; this is one thud with a strong middle. Bomb's two rings are circles that also hit the air; this is floor only.
- **Question for you:** is this still what Ground Pound was meant to be? If you had another picture of it, tell me and I will design that.

## 7. Pickle, Final Smash

**What went wrong.** The live move dropped a pickle on a marked spot (a sky drop). v2 kept the mark and swapped the drop for a video-game crosshair, reasoning that "experience" is a game word. In his kit, Loads of Experience is his belly-flop. So the replacement came from a misread name, and it is still a mark-then-blast like Sidewalky's, Trophy's and Cammy's.

| | Live today | v2 on the preview | New |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name | Loads of Experience | Loads of Experience (crosshair) | **Loads of Experience** (the belly-flop, done sideways) |
| Shape | marks the nearest foe, a pickle drops on the mark | crosshair on the nearest foe, zap from his controller, burst | a flat racing dive forward off a starting block, and the splash where he lands |
| Effect | pure damage | pure damage | pure damage |
| Damage / launch | 24 / 21 | burst 24 / 21, line 6 | dive 6 (pushes forward), splash 18 = 24 at most / 21, same angle |
| Aim | finds the nearest foe within 440 px | the same | none: always 260 px straight ahead |

- **Where it comes from:** his own special, the belly-flop he says he has loads of experience at.
- **Tell:** 36 frames. A starting block comes up out of the floor under his feet and he swings his arms three times.
- **Shape in detail:** a low, flat dive. He never gets higher than 60 px off the floor (lower than a normal jump) and lands 260 px ahead, or at the ledge if that is nearer. His body is a light hit on the way. The landing throws a low splash forward: 120 px long, 70 px tall. That is the big hit. He then lies face down for 30 frames.
- **How to dodge:** be behind him, jump over the dive, or stand more than 380 px away.
- **Balance:** same top damage and launch, but it no longer finds the foe for him, so it is weaker.
- **Not a look-alike of:** not a mark-then-blast any more. Lifering's run is a long armoured sprint along the floor; this is one short dive with a splash at the end.
- **Two things for you to judge:** (1) He starts from his own feet and stays low, so I do not count it as a sky drop, but he does come down on the landing spot. If that still reads as one to you, say so. (2) It is the big version of his own special. The old Final Smashes mostly shared a name with the fighter's special, so I took that as fine.

# Small fixes on moves that are otherwise OK

These need a decision, not a redesign.

1. **Tennis Ball, down (Double Fault):** the slow has no reason. It was added so the look-alike script would pass. Suggest removing it and keeping the lowered damage (front 8, back 11.4), then judging the look-alike by your rule (shape AND effect AND numbers) by eye.
2. **Tapey, main smash (Playback Trap):** the design itself called this optional and noted that you named her old beam as a good one. The CPU also uses the trap about 2.4 times less. Suggest putting the old Solo Track beam back.
3. **Tapey, Final Smash (Rewind):** her finisher is already "Rewind Them Two Seconds" with a tape reaching out. Suggest renaming this one "Eject!" and making the eject the star of the picture.
4. **Fan, Final Smash (Full Blast):** he is a folding paper fan (his own jab is a "paper-edge chop"), but the design draws spinning blades. Suggest he snaps open and flaps himself to make the wind, and the pages are clearly his blog posts.

Also worth knowing: nine of the twelve Final Smashes lost the fighter's own line as their name (for example "You're My Enemy Now!", "Aw, Seriously!", "You Leave Us Alone!"). If those names mattered to you, the new moves can keep them.
