# Move pass, part C: fighters 75 to 102 (Test Tube to Bot), 2026-11-15

You asked for a full pass: find every move that is shape-first, borrowed, generic or thoughtless, and design a replacement from the character.

This is a design document only. Nothing in the game was edited, nothing was run, no picture was looked at.

## What I read

For each of the 28 fighters: the move card text (attack, ranged, special, up, down, finisher), the main smash and its design note, the Final Smash line, and the full moveset (3 tilts, 5 air moves, the side move, 4 throws, the pummel). About 22 moves each, a bit over 600 in all.

## Honest limits

- **I could not check any episode, and I do not know most of these characters well.** Many of them are from the later Inanimate Insanity episodes. For those I judged from the game's own design notes, which name an episode for nearly every special, up special and smash. I did not check those notes against the show. Where I name a show moment below it is from memory or from the game's note, and I say which.
- **I judged from words, not pictures.** If a move's text says "a punch" I took it that a punch is drawn.
- **New numbers are on paper.** Nothing was built or measured.
- **Up and down smashes are not in this pass.** On this build all 28 fighters still have the generated ones (named by shape: "Sky Hop", "Floor Ring" and so on). They are generic by design, and you already ordered own ones for every fighter on 11-12. I did not design 56 of them here.
- Skipped because part A has them: Lifering's Called in a Favor, Soap's Soap Slide, Starfruit's Spoiled Lemon Reunited, Clover's Lucky Break.
- Not touched because you praised or kept them: Tapey's Solo Track beam, Clover's Butterflies, Bot's up special, Blueberry's cookie trap (you picked "Cookie trap" yourself).

## Result: 12 iffy moves on 9 fighters. 19 fighters are FINE.

Only 3 of the 12 need a new design. The other 9 are small fixes: the move stays, with the same shape and numbers, and only what is drawn and what it is called changes.

This part of the roster is in good shape. Almost every special, up special, smash and finisher has a named show moment behind it in the game's notes. That is why the list is short. I did not pad it.

# The three new designs

## 1. Fan, main smash: Nice Save!

**Why it is iffy.** The game's note says where the move comes from: Fan opens up to full size and blocks the goal (the note names the episode A Kick in the Right Direction; I have not checked it). That was first built as a ring around him. The ring looked like another fighter's, so batch 5 swapped it for "he leaps onto the nearest foe". A leap onto the nearest foe is what his finisher already does (Number One Fan Won't Let Go), and it has nothing to do with a save. So the show moment was taken out to pass a look-alike check, and the name no longer fits. This is the same mistake as Leafy's knife.

| | Today | New |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Nice Save! | **Nice Save!** (kept) |
| Shape | hops at the nearest foe (finds them up to 280 px away) and lands on them | he snaps open to full size where he stands: a tall flat wall of fan right in front of him |
| Effect | knocks them down | knocks them down |
| Damage / launch | 13 / 13 (before the smash and fighter multipliers) | 13 / 13, the same |
| Aim | finds the foe for him | none: it is where he is facing |

- **From the character:** the goal save, and that he is a folding fan that opens.
- **Shape in detail:** a 6-frame tell where he folds shut and leans back. Then he snaps open: a wall about 40 px deep and 110 px tall, starting at his front edge, out for 8 frames. It hits in front only, from the floor to above his head. Nothing behind him, nothing far away.
- **Balance:** same damage and launch, but it no longer travels or finds anyone, so it is weaker than today's.
- **Fit with his kit:** his special is the charge, his finisher is the leap. This is the one that stands still, which suits "the reader": he has to call where the foe will be.
- **Not a look-alike of:** the old ring (this is one-sided), MeTag's barrier panes (those appear at the foe, two of them, and clap), any leap. A script should check it against Gaty when built; I did not read Gaty's rows (part A's range).
- **Question for you:** a save should stop something. Should the open fan also stop one shot that hits it? I left that out, because it would make the move stronger than today's. Your call.

## 2. Blueberry, up special: Berry Roll

**Why it is iffy.** Batch 3 made it to replace a hop he shared with Trophy. The idea is "he is a round berry, so he curls up and rolls". Everything else in his kit is the calm strongman lifting things: the palanquin catch, the boulder, the overhead heave, the press. A spinning ball that sucks foes in is not him. The same batch gave Cheesy a spinning wheel that goes up, so this is also a shape that was handed out, with a different effect on each.

| | Today | New |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Berry Roll | **Heave-Ho** |
| Shape | curls into a spinning ball and rolls upward; each turn pulls foes in | a deep squat, then he drives straight up with both arms locked overhead, the way he carried the palanquin |
| Effect | pulls foes in; armoured as he starts; next Attack hits harder | foes over him ride up on his hands and are set down at the top; same armour, same "next Attack hits harder" |
| Damage | 4% a turn | one hit, 8% at most, and never more than the roll's total today (to be measured first) |
| Height | as now | as now |

- **From the character:** his strength and the palanquin he carried (the game's note names The Great Bluish Bake Off; not checked by me).
- **Shape in detail:** the hit is a flat plate on his raised hands, about 50 px wide, only ABOVE him. Nobody beside him is touched and nobody is pulled in. A foe on the plate is carried to the top of the jump and left there with a small pop upward, no real launch.
- **Balance:** it loses the pull and the hits to the sides, so it is weaker as an attack. The jump itself is unchanged.
- **Fit with his kit:** special = lift and slam in front; this = lift overhead.
- **Not a look-alike of:** Goo's Raise Your Hand (an uppercut at the top, stuns), Jack's rising grab (throws them behind), Magnet's Climbing Gear (hauls up whoever is beside her). Snowball's planned up smash "Up You Go" is also two fists overhead, but it is a ground smash that throws backward for 15; a script should still compare the two.

## 3. Test Tube, ranged attack: "fires a small shot straight ahead"

**Why it is iffy.** It has no name and no idea: a small shot, 5%, very long reach. Her special is already a straight, very long shot (the dart, 6%). So her ranged attack is a weaker, blank copy of her own special.

Two options.

- **Option A, the smallest: keep the move, give it a reason.** Same line, same reach, same 5%. It is drawn as a bubble of her own green compound fizzing out of her open top, and called **Fizz Pop**. Her air attack already bursts into bubbles, so it matches. Nothing in play changes. It stays a second straight long shot.
- **Option B: make it a different job from the dart.** Fizz Pop is a slow bubble that drifts straight ahead and pops after a medium distance (about 220 px instead of about 440). 4% instead of 5%. No poison (her up and down specials own the poison). The dart stays her only long shot. Weaker than today on reach and damage.

- **From the character:** only from what she is (a tube of fizzing compound) and from her kit. I do not have a show moment for it and will not make one up.
- **Not a look-alike of:** Taco's Lemon Spit is an arc with a slow for 8%. This is straight, plain, 4 or 5%.

## Fighters with no arms whose Attack is "a punch" (4)

Box, Candle, Tea Kettle and Starfruit have no arms in the game (their rigs list none, and their own movesets say so: Box is "armless", Candle is "armless", Tea Kettle is "armless", Starfruit is "armless"). But the card for each says the Attack is "a punch just in front". It is the default attack row with a default description. Cammy and Bot are armless too and theirs were done properly (a headbutt, two kicks).

| Fighter | Today | New, from what they have |
|---|---|---|
| Box | "a punch" | **Flap Flick**: one flap flicks out and back. Her forward tilt is already Flap Slap; this is the short version. |
| Candle | "a punch" | **Nudge**: a small push with her mind, a ripple right in front of her, eyes closed. Her kit is telekinesis; she does not hit people. |
| Tea Kettle | "a punch" | **Spout Peck**: she pecks forward with her spout. Her down tilt is already Spout Poke along the floor; this is the standing one. |
| Starfruit | "a quick punch" | **Point Poke**: he turns and pokes with one of his five points. His forward tilt already jabs the mic "with his head". |

If any of them is in fact drawn with a headbutt or a kick already, then only the card text is wrong, and the fix is one line each.

| Move | Today | New |

## Bonesaw: Rev and Run (1)

- **Today:** "revs the saw and runs forward sawing at whoever is in the way."
- **Why:** he is a bone saw, a hand saw. Only a power saw revs. The line was written for a chainsaw. (His air move Buzz Spin has the same slip in its wording, "buzzing"; I did not count it, the spin itself is fine.)
- **New:** **Saw Through**: he runs forward with long push-and-pull strokes, the last stroke pushing them off. The same hits in the same places. It matches his special (three strokes) and his forward tilt (Saw Stroke).

# Looked at hard, and left alone (not counted)

These are the ones I nearly flagged. I am listing them so you can overrule me.

- **Cheesy, up special, Cheese Wheel.** Same batch and same spinning shape as Berry Roll. I left it because a wheel of cheese is a visual pun, and puns are Cheesy. One question: the roll also slows foes. I could not find a reason for the slow in the notes. If it was added only to make the move differ from the other spinning ones, it is the same case as Tennis Ball's slow, which you removed. I do not know that it was; I am asking.
- **Test Tube, smash, Unstable Compound.** A flask left on the floor that poisons: the standard floor trap. It is her fourth flask move. It fits an inventor, so I called it fine, but it is the weakest "fine" in my range.
- **Magnet, smash, By Popular Demand.** It pulls foes in and hits them, and so does her special. The note gives it its own show moment (she joins "by popular demand" and a crowd follows her), it pulls a crowd where the special pulls one, and it stuns. Kept.
- **Box, smash, Sinker.** She hops at the nearest foe and drops on them. I first read it as Baseball's slam, but the note is clear: it is her sinking like a stone (Lifering's "We've got a sinker!" was about her). Kept.
- **Spikey's nine moves named "Spike ___".** Plain names, but each one is honestly what a ball of spikes would do. Kept.
- **Two names used twice in one kit:** Cheesy has a tilt called Punch Line and a smash called Punchline; Clover has a back air called Lucky Break and a smash called Lucky Break. Naming only.
