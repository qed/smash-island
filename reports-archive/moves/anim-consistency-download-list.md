# Art consistency by character: download list (animation batch 1)

The owner asked "use the s4 art" and "jst try to make it consistent by character". This list goes through the nine batch-1 fighters. For each one it finds which pictures come from a different art era than the rest of that fighter's set, picks the era to match, and lists the wiki files that would replace the odd ones.

**Nothing in this list has been downloaded.** Every number comes from the wikis' own API: the file lists, `imageinfo` (size, dimensions, upload date), image usage, and the gallery and asset pages. The art was judged from the pictures already in the game and the files already downloaded for batch 1. For files not downloaded yet, the judgement rests on where the wiki files them (the episode or season section, the Assets page), their captions and their dimensions. Rows marked **pick by eye** need a look once they are downloaded. Each of those rows has an alternate listed, but alternates are not counted in the totals.

How the era was judged:
- **Inanimate Insanity.** The "2024 Pose" renders the game uses for Box, Goo, Paintbrush and Tissues are drawn in the Season 4 design. Box's render measures the same body colour (206,173,111) and outline (120,102,70) as her S4E3 and S4E6 pictures, and Goo2024Pose sits beside his S4 forms without a seam. So for these fighters "Season 4" and "matches the render" mean the same target. Teddy Bear's render is her Season 4 sticker art.
- **BFDI** (Donut, Sidewalky, Lollipop, Bell). Each of them uses one design across the BFB-era and TPOT-era assets (the BFDI wiki's "Changes" lists: Bell "BFDIA 13-TPOT 20", Donut "BFDIA+", Lollipop "BFDIA 13-IDFB" plus small BFB tweaks). Their batch-1 pictures measure the same body colours as their renders.

## Donut: consistent, nothing to download
His render (TPOT, 2025) and his official face set (Donut C/L ...0001, BFB-era assets from 2018) use the same design. So do the 2025 frames (DonutUp happy, the roll, the kick) and the BFB pictures (Screeched, Angry). Every body measures inside his normal shading range (about 240,205-214,133-159). The one oddity is "Donut L Smile0001.png", a 169px asset from 2012: it is the same design, and 169px is still bigger than his disc in the game.

## Sidewalky: consistent, nothing to download
Every source is from his one season (uploads January-March 2026), the same art throughout.

## Lollipop: consistent, nothing to download
Her render is LollipopTPOT18. The screech frames (2019), the open-mouth asset (2018), the flung frames (2021, TPOT) and the pieces (2019) all measure the same heliotrope as the render (205,120-121,250-251). The laugh face comes from a 2018 JPEG frame laid on her own render: same era, just a little soft from the JPEG.

## Bell: consistent, nothing to download
Her render, before-ring, ding, avoid, angry and happy (2020-2021, TPOT) match the turn and zapped bodies (2018, BFB). They all measure exactly the same copper (216-217,153-154,117), in the one design that runs from BFDIA 13 to TPOT 20.

## Goo: consistent, nothing to download
Everything is Season 4: the 2024-pose render, the official S4 forms and mouths (uploaded 2026), the muscular arm (S4E4), the raised hand (S4), "Goo happy s4". His scream, the one blurry odd one, was rebuilt today from his official S4 form and mouth (see anim-goo-scream.png).
