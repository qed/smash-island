# Hitbox visuals audit: every hit area readable from its art

2026-10-19. Game file: `smash-bal/artifacts/V1/index.html`. Line numbers are from commit 0991d8a; a "~" means approximate. Nothing in the game was changed for this report.

## The goal

The owner, 2026-10-19: "give shockwaves and other things like that sprites. its too hard to know whats in or out of hitboxes without having hitboxes on, which is banned in competitions."

So with the hitbox view off, every attack's art has to show its real hit area: the same shape, the same size, and the same frames.

Almost every hit in the game is measured to the target's outline (`hurtGap`). If the art is drawn at exactly the hit's radius, one simple rule holds: **if any part of your body touches the drawn shape, you are hit.** Every fix below draws from the same numbers the hit uses, so the art can't drift away from the hit later. The few hits that test the target's centre instead of its outline break this rule. They are listed under "Side bugs" and question 3.

The audit found 75 mismatches. Duplicates were merged, and findings that covered several different moves were split into one row per move, which gives **88 rows**.

What the problem labels mean:
- **Invisible**: nothing marks the hit area, or the art is somewhere else.
- **Drawn smaller**: the art covers only part of the hit area.
- **Drawn larger**: the art promises more reach than the hit has.
- **Too brief**: the shape is shown for only part of the time the hit is live, or after it has already landed.

Most fixes use the same small toolkit of render-only shapes: a **ring**, a **wedge** (fan), a **streak** (capsule) and a **band** (rectangle). Each comes in a dashed "tell" version that fills in during a wind-up and a solid "live" version for the frames the hit is active. The shapes are anchored in the world and sent over the net. See the build order at the end.

## A. Shared systems

### A1. Melee swings: one fixed arc for every move (`drawSwipeArc` ~59995)

Today every melee move draws the same white arc: radius 32-41 px, centred 8 px forward and 4 px up, reaching about 50 px, always on the facing side, and starting at 25% of the swing timer. The move's real fan, capsule or circle is never drawn. **The one fix:** when `hitShape`, `mvStrike` or `gbTick` runs, record the shape on the fighter. This is render-only, works like DEBUG_HITS but is always on, and is sent over the net (`f._atkMove` already reaches clients as `swingMove`, and the frame is `_atkLen-_atkAnim`). Then draw that shape for its live frames plus a 3-frame fade: a fan as a translucent annular wedge from r0 to r1 across a0..a1 with a bright rim at r1, a capsule as a rounded streak 2r wide, and a circle as a ring. Everything is mirrored by facing.

## Side bugs found (behaviour, not art)

1. **`_spinR` and `_spinKb` are never reset.** Donut's Sprinkle Roll is 30 px with an outward push until she uses her up-special. After that it is 56 px with an upward launch for the rest of the match. Fix: set both wherever `_sawing` is set (Saw and Donut specials: 30 and 'out').
2. **Remote's down-special (battery ~40892) does nothing.** It finds the nearest foe within 200 px and then runs an empty branch. It needs an effect before there is anything to draw, and what that effect is, is the owner's call.
3. **Flower's down-special has no height check.** It hits grounded foes on any platform within 120 px across (question 2).
4. **Hits that test the centre, not the outline.** A ring drawn at R won't match these: Bomby's bomb (110) and finisher (95), the Final Smash lock catch, the reel pulls, Taco's arms (210), Liy's switch (95), Bomb's shout box, Black Hole and Blender (question 3).
5. **Plunge landings** deal damage from the feet but test the stun from the centre, 24 px higher (question 5).
6. **Woody's leave-behind stun** has no invulnerability check.

## Questions for the owner

1. **Up-special swings hit all the way around the caster** (a full ring, behind too). Should we keep that and draw it as a ring, which changes no balance? Or make it a forward arc? A forward arc is narrower, so it would be a nerf. Suggestion: draw the ring.
2. **Flower's down-special hits grounded foes on other platforms.** Should we draw the wave on every surface in range, or limit it to her own level? Limiting it is a nerf. Suggestion: draw it everywhere it hits.
3. **Centre-tested hits** (Side bugs 4): should they switch to the outline, so "touch the shape and you're hit" is true everywhere? They would catch slightly more, a small buff. Or keep them and draw their rings smaller?
4. **Style:** a translucent fill with a bright rim, or the rim only? Suggestion: fill alpha about 0.2-0.35, a full-colour rim, drawn only on the live frames, so a 4-player fight doesn't turn into soup.
5. **Plunge stun:** move the stun test to the feet (one ring), or draw two rings?

## Suggested build order (shared systems first, biggest impact first)

1. **Shape toolkit.** Ring, wedge, streak and band, each with a dashed tell and a solid live mode. They are render-only, anchored in the world, recorded as fx entries, and sent over the net (`f._sm` and `_sawing` don't cross the net today). Every later step calls these.
2. **Melee hit-shape trail (A1).** Record the shape in `hitShape`, `mvStrike` and `gbTick` and draw it in place of `drawSwipeArc`. This covers about 920 moves: every jab and grab, plus 102 fighters times 7 tilt and air slots. It is the art people see most, and the biggest single win. Because it is a big shared system, it deserves one test: the drawn shape equals the hit shape.
3. **Wind-up tells and smash patterns (A2).** `startWindup` with a shape argument, then the burst ring, beam band, plant and leap tells, the lunge wedge and the reel circle. This covers about 70 smash kits and every wind-up special.
4. **Up-specials (A3).** The cast-point ring for about 56 rising swings, 14 spin auras (with the `_spinR` fix), 8 plunge shadows and rings, and 3 leave-behind rings.
5. **Finishers and pops (A4, plus the B3 finisher row).** One impact disc covers the shared finisher (about 77 fighters) and the 14 own finisher rows, then `popUpBurst`.
6. **Counter and reflect stances (A4).** The outline aura for the window, and the riposte streak to the attacker.
7. **Traps, shots and zones (A5, and the boss shot row in C).** A decal at pr.r, dashed while arming. Art drawn at 2r. Flat zone decals 2r wide.
8. **Final Smash and assists (A6).** The R2 tell, the lock catch edge (after question 3), and the assist-trophy areas.
9. **Per-fighter specials and down-specials (B1, B2, then B3).** Each is about one toolkit call at the move's own numbers.
10. **Bosses (C).** Two's drain disc, Four's capture ring, the dragon's tail swath, the MePhone4S lane, and a non-area wind-up cue.

After each layer, check it in game with the hitbox view on and then off: the edge of the art should sit exactly on the edge of the hitbox.
