# Move pass, part A: the 13 flagged moves, and fighters 1 to 37 (2026-11-15)

You asked for "the 13 and a full pass": every move that is shape-first, borrowed, generic or thoughtless, with a replacement that comes from the character.

This is a design document only. Nothing in the game was edited, nothing was run or built, no picture was looked at.

## Honest limits (read these first)

- **Every show source is from memory.** I could not check an episode. Where I am unsure I say so.
- **My memory was wrong twice during this pass.** I was about to flag Golf Ball's Final Smash (Freeze Juice) as borrowed from Gelatin, and her down special (Announcer Crusher) as borrowed from Flower. The game's own notes quote the show for both (she freezes Evil Leafy with freeze juice; she stopped the Crusher with her body). I dropped both. Treat my other sources with the same doubt.
- **Numbers are on paper.** They are the game's raw row numbers (the smash damage you see in a match is 1.5 times the row). No new number is higher than the move it replaces.
- **Nothing was run through the look-alike script.** Pairs that need it are named.
- **What I read:** for each of the 37 fighters, the jab, ranged attack, three specials, finisher, neutral smash, Final Smash, the three tilts, five aerials, side move and four throws.
- **What I did not judge:** the up and down smashes of fighters 13 to 37. In this build they are still generated from the neutral smash ("Rising Lunge", "Floor Ring"...). They are generic by construction and you already ordered own ones for everybody on 11-12, so listing 50 of them here would add nothing. Fighters 1 to 12 have the v2 ones, already reviewed.
- **Off-limits moves were left alone:** Bell down, Gelatin down, Fanny neutral, Barf Bag, Marker down, Ruby's smash, Needle, Ice Cube's Cold Snap special (you picked it on 10-23), Puffball's feel and Grow, Pen's Cap Sentry, Lightning's finisher, Naily's smash, Clover's butterflies.

## Result

**21 iffy moves:** the 13, plus 8 found in the pass (4 real redesigns, 4 that only need a new name and picture).
**5 small name fixes** (the move is fine, the name says something else).
**6 borderline** moves listed and left alone.
**27 of the 37 fighters are FINE.**

### 1. Leafy: Leaf Blower
