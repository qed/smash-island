## The CPU's up smash (review round)

**Why CPUs never pressed one.** A CPU only decides to smash when the foe is "in reach", and in reach means level with it (less than 44 px up or down). The up smash was only chosen, at that moment, for a foe more than 60 px ABOVE it. Both can never be true together, so the up smash was never asked for. (The down smash is chosen for a foe close and level, which does happen, so CPUs did use that.)

**The fix.** A single new decision, next to the CPU's up-special anti-air: on the ground, smash ready, a foe 60 to 150 px above it and within 70 px across, not rising away. Then it presses the up smash at the smash's own rates: its normal smash rate if it saw an opening (the foe stunned, frozen or mid-swing), 0.6 of that at kill percent, 0.12 of that otherwise. Nothing else about when a CPU smashes was changed.

- **Did CPUs get stronger? I see no sign of it.** Stocks lost went from 26 to 27 over four matches, which is nothing. Per match: 9 -> 8, 4 -> 5, 7 -> 7, 6 -> 7.
- **What shifted:** down smashes 6 -> 3. With numbers this small I cannot tell that from chance: one early different decision changes everything after it in a match, so the two runs are not the same match played twice.
- **The weak point: only 2 of 11 up smashes landed.** The charge takes 24 to 32 frames and the foe above has usually moved by then. So the CPU now uses the move, but mostly misses with it; a missed smash leaves it open, which if anything makes it a little weaker, not stronger. I did not raise the odds or add prediction to push the hit count up: that would be tuning, and it is the kind of thing that does make CPUs stronger. If you want it to hit more often, that is a separate decision.
- Of the batch 2 fighters in these four matches (Puffball, Ice Cube, Pen, Rocky, Golf Ball, Book, Ruby, Gelatin): Pen, Rocky, Golf Ball and Ruby pressed their up smash; Puffball, Ice Cube, Book and Gelatin did not. So All Aboard and Served Cold have still not been seen through a CPU (they work when pressed: dummy trial and online check).
