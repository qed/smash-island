# Move pass, part B: fighters 38 to 74 (Pillow to Salt), 2026-11-15

You asked for a full pass: find every move that is "iffy" (shape first, borrowed, generic or thoughtless instead of coming from the character) and design a replacement from the character.

This is a design document only. Nothing in the game was edited, nothing was run, no picture was looked at.

## Result

37 fighters read. **14 iffy moves on 12 fighters: 7 to replace, 7 that only need a small fix (a name or a drawing).** 25 fighters are FINE.

I flagged few on purpose. You said "DUDE YOU DONT CHANGE EVERYTHING!!!", so a move is only here if it is borrowed from another fighter, repeats the fighter's own move, falls from the sky, or says something the character is not.

## Honest limits (read these first)

- **I could not check any episode.** Every show source is from memory, and I say where I am unsure.
- **Five fighters I do not know from the show at all: Rose, Fern, Ruler, Sidewalky, Profily.** For them I could only check whether the kit agrees with itself. If one of their moves is wrong for who they are, I would not have seen it.
- All new numbers are on paper. Nothing was built or measured. None is higher than today's.
- I read each move's words, its smash row and its hit shapes in the game file. I did not read the full code of every move.
- "FINE" means "I found nothing truly thoughtless", not "this is the best possible move".

## What I left alone, and why

- Moves you put back or praised: Pillow's down and her smash (your "hammer-smash bait combo"), Fern's smash, Taco (II)'s neutral, all of Bow, all of Toothpaste (best finisher, "uses her full kit"), Remote's and TV's scramble smashes.
- The v2 smashes already reviewed: Firey Jr., Marshmallow and Apple (main smash); Toothpaste, Rose, David, Woody, Baseball and Pickle (Final Smash).
- Part A's flagged moves in my range: Match's Strike Anywhere, Ice Cube's Blast Chiller, Roboty's All Dashes, Paper's Eighty-Eight Keys.
- Final Smashes you redesigned or looked at on 11-02 to 11-04 (Pillow, Remote, Taco, TV, Profily, Tree, Cake, Donut, Balloon, Taco (II), Marshmallow, Match and the others written by hand). You said "the final smashes are mostly good".
- A Final Smash that is the big version of the fighter's own special (Lightbulb, Nickel (II), Knife, Roboty, Fern, Apple, Salt, Microphone, Firey Jr.). The last review counted that as fine, so I did too.
- Finishers. You picked them from the idea lists, and I found none that is off.

# The 7 moves to replace

Rules kept for every one: no number higher than today's, nothing falls from the sky, and I say which other move it is closest to.

## 1. Saw, smash: Ripsaw Roll

**What is wrong.** Her blade rolls along the floor like a wheel, through everyone, with bleed. Donut has the same roll with slow, and the code note on his says "the same roll". For Donut it comes from what he is (round). For Saw it does not: from memory she is a hand saw with a handle, and a hand saw does not roll.

| | Today | New: **Handle's Off!** |
|---|---|---|
| Shape | a blade rolls along the floor a very long way (about 840 px), through everyone | she swings so hard that the blade flies off its handle: one blade, straight ahead at chest height, 260 px, through everyone |
| Effect | bleed | bleed, the same length |
| Damage / launch | 12 / 7 | 12 / 7 |
| Cost | cooldown | the same cooldown: she holds only the handle until the blade is back |

- **Where it comes from:** Saw losing her handle in BFB (from memory: I am fairly sure it happened, not sure of the episode or how).
- **Why it is not stronger:** same damage, launch and bleed, and it reaches less than a third as far. It flies straight, so it does not follow the floor down a slope or over a ledge.
- **Fit with her kit:** her special and down special are close-range grinding. This is her one reach move, as the roll was.
- **Closest other moves:** Apple's Pencil Pair (two thrown pencils that go through, 8%, no bleed, a special). Different effect and numbers.

**Small fix on the same fighter.** Her special (Spin Cycle), down special (Defensive Spin) and Final Smash (Spin Cycle Overdrive) are all "a spinning blade round her". That is a circular saw. Smallest change: keep every shape, number and name, and draw them as fast back-and-forth sawing strokes. If you see her as a spinning saw, ignore this.

## 2. Taco, down special: Retractable Arms

**What is wrong.** This is Taco (II)'s move. The code note says it: "She has Taco (II)'s Retractable Arms now" (a kit fix, because her salsa puddle never hit anyone). The two are the same in shape, effect and number: the arms drag the nearest foe in, long reach, 6%, stun. Taco's only adds a small salsa puddle. By your own rule that is a real duplicate.

**Why I am asking instead of just replacing.** Her Final Smash, which you redesigned on 11-02, is named Retractable Arms and uses the arms to shove the jawbreaker. So you may want her to keep them.

- **Option A, no change:** the two Tacos share the arms. Nothing to build.
- **Option B: Stuck Inside.**
  - **Where it comes from:** BFB 2, where she was stuck inside the jawbreaker (the same moment her special comes from; from memory, fairly sure).
  - **Shape:** a jawbreaker closes round her for 0.6 s. She cannot move. Hits on her do less, as a normal brace. The first foe who hits her with a close hit gets stuck to the candy.
  - **Effect:** that foe is rooted for half a second. No drag, no stun.
  - **Numbers:** 6%, as now. The salsa puddle stays as it is (2%, slow).
  - **Fit with her kit:** her special sticks a foe in a jawbreaker from far away; this does it up close, to someone who attacks her.
  - **Closest other moves:** Ice Cube's Brittle Counter and Pickle's Wrong Eye Patch are stances too, but they hit back for damage (12% and more). This roots.
  - Her Final Smash would keep its name and picture; only its link to the down special goes.

## 3. Woody, smash: Splinter Scramble

**What is wrong.** His special is a dash forward (12%, splinter traps). His smash is a shorter dash forward that does less (9%, slow, and costs him 4%). So the smash is his own special, weaker.

| | Today | New: **Splinter Scramble** (name kept) |
|---|---|---|
| Shape | dashes 135 px forward through the foe | he squeaks and bolts 135 px BACKWARD; splinters burst off him at the spot he left, a round patch about 50 px wide |
| Effect | slow | slow, the same length |
| Damage / launch | 9 / 9 | 9 / 9 |
| Cost | 4% to himself, short stun | the same |

- **Where it comes from:** he is scared of everything. Running away is the most Woody thing there is, and he is balsa wood, so he sheds splinters.
- **Why it is not stronger:** same numbers, and the foe now has to be right next to him when it starts. He is not untouchable while he runs.
- **Fit with his kit:** special = toward the foe, smash = away from the foe. Two different choices instead of one.
- **Closest other moves:** Balloony's smash pushes him back a little after a hop. No other smash is a retreat.

## 4. Dora, down special: Jabby Brace

**What is wrong.** A short armour brace with a stun on whoever is in front (no damage). Bell and TV had a brace of the same kind (armour and a stun ring), and theirs were already replaced. It says nothing about Dora.

| | Today | New: **Get Digging** |
|---|---|---|
| Shape | braces on the spot; stuns foes within about 40 px in front | digs straight down, is under the floor for 12 frames, comes back up in the same place; the burst of dirt stuns foes within about 40 px of the hole |
| Effect | armour 20 frames, stun 16 frames | cannot be hit for 12 frames, stun 16 frames |
| Damage | none | none |
| Cost | none | she is stuck half out of the hole for 10 frames after |

- **Where it comes from:** Dora digging at great speed in BFDIA ("Get Digging"; from memory, and I am not sure of the details).
- **Why it is not stronger:** being unhittable is better than armour, so it is shorter (12 frames against 20) and she pays 10 frames after. Only on the ground; in the air it does nothing.
- **Fit with her kit:** everything else she has is fast and in your face. This is her one way to make a hit miss.
- **Closest other moves:** Rose's Curtsy (14 frames unhittable, no stun). Part C's Bonesaw has a v2 smash called Burrow that also goes underground, but that one is an attack with damage.

## 5. Tree, special: Timber

**What is wrong.** "Drops a heavy branch from the sky onto the nearest foe after a short warning." It is a sky drop that aims itself, and you said "sky drops suck". A tree's branches come off the tree, not out of the sky.

| | Today | New: **Timber** (name kept) |
|---|---|---|
| Shape | a branch appears high above the nearest foe and falls | he snaps a branch off his own crown and lobs it in a high arc to where that foe is standing |
| Aim | the nearest foe, long reach | the same foe, the same reach |
| Damage | 11% | 11% |
| Warning | a shadow | the same length of time, but now you see the branch leave him |

- **Where it comes from:** it is his own branch. (I know no show moment for it and will not make one up.)
- **Why it is not stronger:** same damage, same reach, same time to react.
- **Fit with his kit:** he is slow and rooted, and this stays his one long-range move. His finisher bends a branch back; this one throws a branch.
- **Closest other moves:** the other lobbed specials (Paintbrush's paint bomb, Cake's slice). Theirs are thrown where the fighter faces; his still goes to the foe's spot.

## 6. Sidewalky, Final Smash: Physically

**What is wrong.** "Drops a heavy slab on the nearest foe." It is a sky drop in a Final Smash (you wanted all of those gone), and it is the "mark a spot, then blast" shape that Trophy's and Cammy's also have.

**I do not know Sidewalky from the show**, and I do not know what "Physically" refers to. So this comes from what he is (a pavement) and from his own finisher (Step on a Crack). If the name means something, tell me and I will redo it.

| | Today | New: **Physically** (name kept), "Fresh Pavement" |
|---|---|---|
| Shape | marks the nearest foe within 380 px; a slab falls on the mark | he pours a strip of wet pavement along his floor, 380 px ahead; then the slabs tip up one after another, from his end outward |
| Effect | pure damage | foes standing on the strip when it sets are stuck for 12 frames, then thrown up |
| Damage / launch | 28 / 20 | 28 / 20, the same angle |
| Aim | finds the foe | none: straight ahead on his own floor |

- **Tell:** 26 frames while the strip pours (the same as today's lead time).
- **How to dodge:** jump, or be on another platform. Foes in the air are not touched.
- **Why it is not stronger:** same damage and launch, but it no longer finds the foe and it misses anyone in the air.
- **Closest other moves:** Roboty's Final Smash (antennas spring up from the floor on both sides, 10%). This one is forward only, roots first and hits much harder.

## 7. Knife, smash: Caltrops

**What is wrong.** Caltrops are already the third trick in his special, and his down throw. As a smash they are the stock floor trap with bleed, the same thing the last review flagged on Pin's Pushpin. The code note says the smash used to be Blade Rush, "he rushes through you with the blade, and it strips armour and stances", and that it was swapped in a look-alike batch.

**Proposal: put Blade Rush back.** A dash that strips armour and stances is a different move from the other dashes by your own rule (different effect). It is also more Knife: he goes straight at people.

- **Numbers:** not above today's 11 damage and 8 launch. I do not have the old row; whoever builds it should take the old numbers and cap them at these.
- **Question for you:** was Blade Rush one of the moves you were happy to lose? If so, leave Caltrops.

# The 7 small fixes

No shape or number changes in any of these.

1. **Saw: the spinning blade.** See under Saw above: draw sawing strokes.
2. **TV, smash "Signal Jam".** Remote's Final Smash is also "Signal Jam" (and her down special is "Remote Jam"). TV's down special is "Pause" and so is Remote's up special. Suggest TV's smash becomes "Dead Air" or "No Signal". The two Pauses are different moves; rename one only if it bothers you.
3. **David, down "Big Armor".** Plain armour with nothing behind it. He has two lines in the show, "Aw, seriously!" and "No!" (from memory, fairly sure). Suggest the same armour, the same length, named "No!": he says no, and the hit mostly does not happen.
4. **Fern, names of normal moves.** Trunk Swing, Branch Back, Canopy Rise and Seed Drop are Tree's words; a fern has fronds and spores, and Fern's own finisher already uses spores. His neutral air is called Leaf Blower, which is Leafy's smash. Suggest Frond Swing, Frond Back, Frond Rise, Spore Drop and Frond Whirl.
5. **Ruler, smash "Straight Edge".** His down special and his forward air are also called Straight Edge. His special is Measure Twice, so the smash can be "Cut Once".
6. **Salt, smash "Trend Setter".** Her down special and down throw have the same name. In the smash she and Pepper run straight through you; suggest "OMGA!" (their squeal, from memory).
7. **Baseball, smash "Out Of The Park".** The name is a batting word, the move is a hop onto the nearest foe, and his up special says he is too heavy to hop. It is also his third "lands on you" move (up special, finisher, this). Smallest change: keep the move and name it for what it is, for example "Heavy Landing". If you want it changed: he stays on the floor and bowls one turn forward, knocking the first foe up and out (16 / 14, no slow). I do not recommend that one, because it is the common lunge shape and his finisher already starts with a roll.

# Things in groups (not counted above, no change proposed)

These are true of many fighters at once. I list them so you know; fixing them is a project, not a move.

1. **Up and down smashes.** All 37 fighters in my range still have the generated ones ("Rising Lunge", "Floor Ring" and so on: a swing over the head, a low sweep). They are generic by how they are made. You already ordered own up and down smashes for everyone; only the first 12 fighters are designed. That is 74 moves waiting, and I did not design them here.
2. **Tilts and aerials.** For most fighters these are the same eight stock shapes with a themed name: the neutral air is a circle round the body that hits several times (33 of my 37), the down air is a straight drop that spikes, the up tilt is an arc "up and over ahead". Fourteen fighters are almost shape for shape the same: Pillow, Remote, Rose, Saw, Taco, Woody, Dora, Firey Jr., Fern, Ruler, Balloony, Profily, Cake, Donut. Hand-shaped ones: Toothpaste, Match, Tree, Ice Cube, and parts of TV, Sidewalky, Roboty, Balloon and Pickle. Normal attacks are plain in most fighting games, so I did not count these as iffy. If you want them done, the cheapest way is one signature normal per fighter, not all eight.
3. **The armour brace.** David, Sidewalky, Tree and Roboty each have "armour for under a second" as their down special. For a slab of pavement and a rooted tree it fits. For David it is the small fix above. Roboty's "Tank Armour" fits his role in the game more than the character.
4. **Two balloons.** Balloony's Air Leak (a jet zips him forward, 10%) and Balloon's Quick Air Release (he rockets forward, 9%) are close in shape, effect and number. Balloony's finisher and Balloon's smash are both "he pops and comes back". Each comes from being a balloon, so neither is thoughtless, but they are the closest pair in my range.
5. **Two vans.** Match's Final Smash and Pencil's are both the FreeSmart van crossing the stage. The code note gives a show source for Match's (BFDIA 5), and you worked on Pencil's yourself, so I left both.
6. **Words, not moves.** Ice Cube's tilt and aerial lines say "him" and "his"; her other lines say "her".
