# Animation plan: Puffball-style picture sets for every fighter

You asked on 2026-10-19 for "animations should be like pb's animations", and chose "Plan first". This is that plan.

**Nothing has been changed or downloaded.** No game file or test was edited, and no image was saved. Every proposed pose below was picked from its wiki file name or gallery caption. Nobody has looked at the images yet, so please check each one by eye before approving it.

Scope: the 102 playable fighters (hidden fighters left out). 59 are from BFDI and 43 from Inanimate Insanity.

---

## 1. What Puffball has today

Puffball is the only fighter with a full picture set for a move. Her pictures are in `artifacts/V1/assets/sprites/`.

| Picture | When it shows | Where it came from |
|---|---|---|
| `puffball.png` | Base picture, most of the time | BFDI wiki `Tpot_renders0042.png` |
| `puffball-spit-1.png` to `-4.png` | Her X (ranged) move. The four frames are puff (eyes squeezed), spit (rainbow mouth), recoil (surprised) and settle (smile) | Made in-house: `scripts/make-puffball-spit.mjs` bends her own picture. The rainbow uses the colours of her barf (`File:Barf rainbow long.png`). No borrowed art |
| `puffball-meteor.png` | The whole Meteor Puff dive (`f._plunge`) | Cut out by hand from the BFDIA 6 frame `File:Bfdia6 prize (23).png` (`scripts/clean-puffball-meteor.mjs`, manifest row `"Puffball (meteor)"`) |
| `attacks/rainbow.png` | Her projectile | — |

**How it works in the game** (`artifacts/V1/index.html`, line numbers as of today):

- **Timing.** The spit frames change by time into the swing. They start at ticks 0, 3, 8 and 13 of the 18-tick swing (`PUFF_SPIT_AT`, line 53488), and the shot leaves on the spit frame.
- **Drawing.** Her entry `FIGHTER_ANIM.Puffball` (line 54783) has a `poses` table and a `body(f)` hook. The hook draws the chosen pose through the shared `drawSpriteBody` (line 58987) under a different name, `"Puffball (spit)"`.
  - She keeps her float, her lean during the spit, and the hit and burn tints.
  - The frame layer (drawn-on faces) and the limb animation look fighters up by name, so neither draws anything over a pose picture.
  - `over()` draws the rainbow stream. `spitFlash: false` and `diveFlash: false` stop the white attack flash hiding the frames.
- **When she's hit,** the spit frames stop and her base picture shows. The dive picture stays.
- **On a skin** (she has two: Frozen and Thinking Hat), the picture swap is skipped. `frPuffballPose` (line 57365) fakes the four frames and the dive on the skin's own picture, using the frame layer.

**Everyone else.** The other 101 fighters have one picture. Eight of them swap in a single show picture now and then: Box, Goo, Lifering, Candle, Yin-Yang, Spikey, Cammy and Tissues. Leafy and Bubble swap their body for a drawn shape (blade dash, popped shards) rather than a picture. All 102 already have frame-layer rows and move animations, which is why they still look alive with one picture.

## 2. How adding a set works

The game doesn't find pictures by file name on its own; every picture is named in the code. Adding a set to one fighter needs:

1. **The picture files.** Each is `<fighter>-<pose>.png` in `artifacts/V1/assets/sprites/`, 200px tall with a transparent background.
   - `scripts/fetch-sprites.mjs` resizes and checks face, transparency and facing. `scripts/audit-sprites.mjs` re-checks the left/right flag.
   - Fetching is a download, so it needs your permission.
   - The no-borrowing route is to bend the fighter's own picture, the way `make-puffball-spit.mjs` did.
2. **Credits.** A row in the sprite manifest (`scripts/sprite-manifest.json`, or `scripts/sprite-manifest-inanimateinsanity.json` for II) and in `CREDITS.md`, with the source address.
3. **The table entry** in `FIGHTER_ANIM[Name]`. Most fighters already have one for lean and effects. It gets:
   - `poses: { key: renderSprite("file.png", {imgW, imgH, flip}) }`
   - a `body(f)` that picks the pose from the move state and draws it through `drawSpriteBody`, like Puffball. The other existing way, `FIGHTER_ANIM._g1PoseBody` (line 54852, used by Tissues and others), draws the picture flat with no float or lean.
   - **Suggestion:** rather than about 100 hand-written hooks, generalise Puffball's tick table into one per-fighter data table keyed on the move label `f._atkMove` (for example `'ember.ft'`), plus hurt, launched and win. One shared `body()` would then read it. Online clients already receive the move label.
4. **Online play.** `body()` should only read values the host already sends: the swing timer, kind, length and move label, the dive, hurt, smash charge, flash, damage percent, armour, countering, frozen, burn and Paintbrush's rage. States the host doesn't send yet need adding to the sent list, or online players see the plain picture. The tables flag each one; Section 5 lists them.
5. **Win pictures.** The result screen (`#winStage`, line 1644) draws winners through `frWinPose` (line 56786): the base picture with a drawn face doing a hop, sway or twirl. A win picture needs a small hook there.
6. **Skins.** Picture swaps are skipped on skins. Each skin either gets a frame-layer pose hook like `frPuffballPose`, or keeps the plain picture.
7. **Optional extras.** A custom lean (`atkDrive`), a mouth position (`anchor`), and effects in `over()`. A pose picture gets no limb swings unless it gets its own `LIMB_RIG` row (via `trace-limbs.mjs`).
8. **Tests.** `test/puffball-anim.test.js` is the template. Without a loaded image, `spriteImage` returns null and the plain picture draws, so existing tests are unaffected. One shared test for the pose table should cover everyone, rather than one test per fighter.

**Size.** Today's sprites average about 30 KB each (the sprite folder is 12 MB for 166 pictures). If every row below were done, that's roughly 550 more pictures and 15-20 MB, so load time is worth checking.

## 3. How to read the tables

- **Has now** is base picture + swap pictures, then skins. For example, "1+2 · 1 skin" means a base, 2 swap pictures and 1 skin.
- **Proposed sprites** lists each moment and the look for it. "Hurt" is the hit stun, "KO" is being launched or knocked out, and "Win" is the result screen.
- **Where the poses could come from:**
  - `Code-formatted names` are exact wiki file names. For BFDI rows the file page is `https://battlefordreamisland.fandom.com/wiki/File:<name>`; for II rows it is `https://inanimateinsanity.fandom.com/wiki/File:<name>`.
  - "Quoted names" are gallery captions or file stems. Find them on the fighter's gallery page: `https://battlefordreamisland.fandom.com/wiki/<Name>/Gallery` or `https://inanimateinsanity.fandom.com/wiki/<Name>/Gallery`.
  - **official** means the show's own production drawings: the wiki "Assets" sections, and for II, AnimationEpic's released Season 4 source files. II rows use **AB** for the [Season 4 bodies page](https://inanimateinsanity.fandom.com/wiki/Assets/Bodies/Inanimate_Insanity_IV) and **AF** for the [Season 4 faces page](https://inanimateinsanity.fandom.com/wiki/Assets/Facial_features/Inanimate_Insanity_IV).
  - **screenshot** means an episode frame that needs cutting out by hand, like the meteor picture.
  - Everything else is a gallery "Poses" cut-out. These are show art cut out by wiki users, and quality varies. Names with "VHSy", "removebg" or "pixian ai" were cut out by tools.
  - **draw** means nothing matching was found, so the frame would be made by bending the fighter's own picture.
- **Notes** gives a difficulty rating and anything to watch. The ratings drive the tiers in Section 5.

**Copyright.** All named files are show art (jacknjellify for BFDI, AnimationEpic for II), the same standing as the pictures the game already uses. Fan-made bodies, artist-credited remakes and gory files are left out. No official asset pack from jacknjellify was found. A fan-uploaded zip on itch.io ("thebfdiarchive") is of unknown origin, and I'd leave it alone.

### Suggested order (most existing poses first)

1. **A first batch of fighters with ready-made official frame sets**, the closest thing to Puffball's spit:

2. **The rest of Tier 1.** Start with the fighters whose swap code already exists (Lifering, Candle, Cammy, Yin-Yang), then BFDI and II in roster order.

A cheaper option, if a full set per fighter is too much at once: give everyone just **Hurt, KO and Win** first. Those three moments have a source for almost every fighter. Hurt already reaches online players, and Win only needs the one `frWinPose` hook.
