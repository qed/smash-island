# Finisher ideas v2: every finisher is a move

**Your rule (2026-10-19):** every finisher (V + C) is a move that acts on opponents (a hit, throw, launch, grab, projectile, a trap or terrain that catches or hits, or a counter that strikes back); a buff, field or stance may only come along as a bonus.

Nothing here is built yet. 67 of the 102 rows changed from v1: 66 ideas were reworked or replaced, and Ruler only got a new title. The other 35 already were moves and are kept word for word. Your file-ride idea for the hidden fighter stays exactly as you described it. Every row was checked against that fighter's Final Smash, smash (V), specials and side special in the current game, and against the other 101 rows for repeats.

**The mix:** grabs, throws and pulls 16 · projectiles 16 · strikes 15 · traps that catch 12 · summons that attack 10 · terrain that hits 9 · dashes, leaps and rides 8 · counters 6 · combo starters 5 · launchers 3 · swaps 2.

A † after a title means the idea needs a new engine system (listed after the tables).

## Ideas that need a new engine system (7)

1. **Bomby, Hot Potato Fuse:** a status that passes from fighter to fighter on touch.
2. **Tennis Ball, Deuce Rally:** a ball that any fighter's attack sends back.
3. **Saw, Cut a Hole in the Ledge:** switching a ledge off for a few seconds (the crumble zone is the closest piece).
4. **Nickel (II), Roasted Live on Air:** damage when an attack misses.
5. **Test Tube, Shrinking Formula:** changing a fighter's size for a few seconds.
6. **Fan, Number One Fan Won't Let Go:** one fighter riding on another.
7. **Tapey, Rewind Them Two Seconds:** remembering where a foe stood 2 s ago (the Clock assist rewinds damage, not position).

Three v1 systems are no longer needed: Bell's pendulum swing, Lollipop's attack-speed buff and Clover's fizzle chance. Your file-ride example still needs the attack-speed buff, so that piece is now built for that one fighter alone.

Smaller stretches of what exists, not new systems: a slap-fight mash contest (Coiny, built on mash-to-escape); a thrown foe used as a rolling ball that grows (Snowball, built on Soap's throw-them-as-ammo); a moving whirl that carries foes (Leafy, the belt zone on a travelling hazard); summons people can stand on, built the way Spongy is (Book, Puffball, Blueberry); and a new Firey assist row for Firey Jr.

## Two questions before anything is built

1. **The cooldown for the long ones.** Some still leave something behind for 3 to 6 s: Pin's corkboard, Pen's sentry, Book's castle, Ruby's prism, Puffball's bridge, Basketball's shot clock, Bell's doorbell, Lightning's cloud, Money's piggy bank, Fern's fernlings, Sidewalky's crack, Cake's Tosser, Nickel (II)'s roast, Box's decoy, Starfruit's amp and Blueberry's boulder. That is longer than today's 74-frame finisher cooldown. My suggestion: one at a time (using it again replaces the old one), with the cooldown starting when it ends. Is that OK, or should each one get its own cooldown?
2. **The 21 fighters who already have their own finisher:** Firey, Leafy, Needle, Pin, Snowball, Bomby, Bubble, Teardrop, Flower, Tennis Ball, Blocky, Coiny, Pen, Pencil, Golf Ball, Rocky, Book, Fries, Gelatin, Nickel and Puffball. Today these are mostly plain big hits on a 74-frame cooldown. Should these new ideas replace them, or should the current ones stay for now?
