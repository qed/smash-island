## Tree's online mismatch (review round)

- **Cause:** the host packed each fighter's damage for guests as the number rounded to 0.1. Any hit that is not a multiple of 0.1 left the guest up to 0.05 off. Tree's damage setting of 1.04 makes his new down smash 21.84, which a guest showed as 21.8.
- **Fix:** the host now sends the damage itself, unrounded. The guest still SHOWS it to one decimal, as before. One line changed (netFighter).
- **Proof:** Tree's down smash through the fake-socket pair: before 0.04 off, after 0. The other 22 moves in the main run: gap 0.
- **Side effect I know of:** a damage number in a packet can now be longer (21.84 instead of 21.8). I did not measure packet size.

## The CPU's down smash (review round)

**Why seven moves were never pressed in the first round's 8 matches. Two reasons, one of them a real choice problem.**

1. **Rarity (not a choice problem).** A CPU only thinks about a smash on an opening (the foe stunned, frozen or mid-swing) or at kill percent, with the foe in reach. When it did, it asked for the down smash 18% of the time. In those 8 matches, 32 fighters pressed 22 up or down smashes in all. Nickel and Donut did not press a neutral smash once either.
2. **The choice ignored the move (a real problem).** That 18% die was rolled on the generated sweep's distance (the foe within about 88 px, level), whatever the fighter's own down smash looks like. Own down smashes are short, one-sided, or, for Dora, hit only BEHIND her, and the CPU always pressed facing the foe: so her Dirt Flinger could never land from a CPU.

**The fix (CPU choice only; nothing about WHEN a CPU smashes changed).** A fighter with an own down smash now asks for it when the move would actually reach the foe where it stands (any of its boxes, for the way it will face), half the time; if it would not reach, it does not ask for it. A down smash that hits only behind is pressed with the CPU's back to the foe. Fighters with the generated sweep keep the old rule. This applies to all 36 fighters with an own pair, batches 1 and 2 included.
