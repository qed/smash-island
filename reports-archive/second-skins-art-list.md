# Second skins: the art list (one new regular skin for every fighter who has only one)

You approved a second regular skin ("Later, yes", then "start") for every fighter who has only one. This is the list of pictures to download, so you can approve them **by name**. **Nothing was downloaded and nothing in the game was changed.** Every size below comes from the wikis' own API (`imageinfo`: pixel size, file size, url); no picture was opened. All 56 were checked against the API again on the evening of 2026-10-26 (pixel size, file size and url all still match), and the fighter list was counted again from the game as it is now (ii-dlc at `d5d9e09`): still the same 60 fighters with one regular skin.

## The short version

- **60 fighters** have exactly one regular skin today (Halloween costumes not counted). Tree and the three prize fighters already have two each, so they are not on this list.
- **56 have a picture picked** (26 from the BFDI wiki, 30 from the Inanimate Insanity wiki).
- **4 have no clean picture** at all: Money, Bonesaw, Magnet and Dough. Those could be drawn in code instead (see the end).
- **Total download if you approve all 56: 12,430,124 bytes (11.85 MB) at full size.** The skins pipeline (`fetch-sprites.mjs`) only asks the wiki for a 200 px tall copy, so the real download is much smaller.
- Status: **34 OK**, **16 need a look** after download (edges, empty space, face showing, a small change), **6 doubtful** (the picture is real but looks close to the fighter's normal look): Pin, Blueberry, Cammy, Microphone, Tea Kettle, Tissues.
- Changed in this second pass: **Bomby** now gets Bracelet Bomby (TPOT 4, an official picture) instead of a fan's cut-out of a purple Bomby; **Tapey** now has a picture (Blindfolded Tapey, S4E3, joining the other Blindfolded skins); **Dough** moved to the no-picture list (his render is already his ghost look, and his living look is the Solid Dough skin he already has).

## How the pictures were picked

- Each pick is a real look the fighter has in the show: a costume worn in an episode, a state they were in (tangerine juice, Yoyle metal, ghost), or a different season's drawing.
- **Same art group as the fighter's render.** BFDI fighters with a BFB/TPOT render only get BFB/TPOT art; the five BFDIE fighters (Money, Fern, David, Ruler, Toothpaste) only get BFDI/BFDIA/IDFB/BFDIE art. Inanimate Insanity has no art groups; Season 3-4 art was used where there was a good one.
- Not the same idea as the skin they already have, and one character, face showing, no words on it.
- Shape: "wide:tall" compares the wiki picture with the fighter's render. Close numbers mean the skin fits the fighter's box without changes. The wiki pictures often have empty space round the character; the pipeline trims it.
- Prices follow the old skins: **75** for a costume or a prop, **115** for a whole-body change (colour, damage, a different season's look).
- Some picks come in small families like the old skins (Yoyle Metal, Tangerine, Snotty, Hard Hat, Backpack). New families: **Twinkle** (BFB 6: Book, Grassy, Cake, Donut, joining Twinkle Bell), **Announcer** (BFDIE 3: Fern, David, Toothpaste), **Safety Vest** (II Season 4: Box, Lifering, MeTag), **Ghost** (Bow, Knife). Tapey joins the old **Blindfolded** family.
- Prize fighters A/B/C are left out (they have their own skins).

## After you approve

Each approved picture goes through the usual skins pipeline: download, look at it (one character, face showing, no words, clean edges), trim, a line in `scripts/sprite-manifest-skins.json`, a thumbnail in `COS_SKIN_THUMBS`, a credits line in `CREDITS.md`, and the new rows go at the **end** of the skin rows in `COSMETICS` (append-only for online codes).
