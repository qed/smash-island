# Six: the new 6th move (phase 3 only): three options for the owner (2026-11-10)

The owner asked for a new move from her show moments, used from phase 3 only (phase 3 starts at 25% HP). All three are placed on you at the lock, grow in over 12 frames, hit once per turn (at most one boss hit), keep her hittable, and have a visible body tell. The builder first recommended ADDITION!, but the owner's other pick, the TELEKINESIS ZONE, is also a pull that closes on you, so the owner chose ROUND THE WORLD; it is in its own `sixRoll*` functions. Fuller designs of all three are in `attack-options-six.md` (section 3).

## 1. ADDITION!  (NOT CHOSEN: too close to the telekinesis zone the owner also picked)
- What it does, in plain words: a glowing plus sign grows out of the floor 400 px from you. For two seconds it drags every fighter near it along the floor toward its middle. Then it flashes and snaps shut: whoever is in the middle is hit and thrown up and toward the middle of the stage.
- Tell: she plants both hands forward, palms open and glowing light blue, leaves stream across the floor toward the spot (that stream also shows which way the pull goes), then the plus sign's own glow rises for the last 20 frames.
- Dodge: walk or run out of the well (a run is faster than the drag, so you always net a way out), or simply stand anywhere beyond about 150 px of the middle at the flash. Do not run through the middle. It is the only move that makes a fighter who hangs back come in.
- Canon: the subscriber specials, where she and Nine set both Sevens beside an addition sign that forces them together; the plus-sign flowers of the Equation Playground.

## 2. ROUND THE WORLD  (THE OWNER'S PICK, built)
- What it does: her bubble swells and she curls up inside it, then rolls across the room in three big arcs (four in the last stretch), each landing where you stood when the bubble squashed flat. It hurts while an arc is live. A pop of her bubble mid-roll ends the move and dazes her.
- Tell: the swell, the curl, then the bubble squashes flat on the floor for 12 frames before each launch.
- Dodge: step about 200 px out of the landing, or walk in under the middle of the arc (the underside is high enough there); do not jump under the top; a ledge is no shelter.
- Canon: the 700,000 special, where Seven kicks her out of the count and she spins around the globe; flight and levitation on her page.

## 3. BUBBLE BLOWER 7000
- What it does: a toy bubble machine rolls out beside her and for about three seconds spits big bubbles at you, its funnel nodding from low to high. The machine has a little health: smash it and every bubble pops and the move ends.
- Tell: she pulls the machine out, yanks the cord twice, the funnel's first nod.
- Dodge: jump the low bubbles, stay down under the high ones, or run in and smash the machine.
- Canon: she blows bubbles in "Alone" and the Number Playground Chronicle names this toy (non-canon newspaper). Honest note: it is a second bubble move beside BUBBLES!.
