# Up smash and down smash: a distinct move for every fighter

Design only, nothing built. Local report (the real names of the three hidden prize fighters appear here and must never reach game code, comments, the ledger or a commit; in the game they are PZ_A, PZ_B, PZ_C).

## Rules used

1. Each move fits the fighter's object, kit and identity, with a name and one plain line of what the player sees.
2. Each is classed by motion type for the swept hitboxes: SWING (arc) and SPIN (rotation) sweep frame by frame; THRUST, SLAM, BURST, LAUNCHER, PROJECTILE and TRAP/PLACE do not.
3. Up smash = vertical family: it hits above or arcs overhead and launches up (about 80-90 degrees). Down smash = low family: it hits both sides at the feet or sweeps along the ground and launches low and outward (about 15-25 degrees above flat). A down thrust is a quick left-and-right pair so both sides are covered.
4. Numbers: damage is 0.80-0.95x the fighter's own neutral smash (neutral = SMASH_SPEC dmg x SMASH_DMG_MULT 1.5, rounded; the 7 hand-written or no-hit neutrals use the generator's base 18 or 11, flagged in the note). Launch strength is 0.7-0.9x the neutral smash's and never new kill power; no status effect. The 24-frame charge ring is kept, then 4-5 start-up frames: always 20+ frames from the first visible sign. Recovery grows with damage as the generated smashes do.
5. Balance posture: the nine reflector fighters and Clover are held to 0.80-0.82x damage and 0.7-0.75x launch. The weak ten get 0.95 / 0.92x damage, about 20% more reach and an easy-to-land angle, but not more launch than the neutral smash. The three prize fighters and the six event fighters sit at 0.88 / 0.82x.
6. A kit's own up or down special is never repeated: each row's note names the specials it avoids.
7. No two moves share a name, and no two fighters of the same archetype share a concept.

**Quota problem (needs the owner's eye):** "no more than 12 per motion type per direction" cannot hold with 8 types and 112 fighters (8 x 12 = 96). The cap used is 14, and every type is exactly 14 in both directions (112 = 8 x 14).

**Roster covered:** 112 rows = 103 regular cup fighters, 6 event fighters that are playable rows (8-Ball, Black Hole, Clock, Cloudy, Pie, Spongy) and the 3 hidden prize fighters. Foldy, Stapy and Beach Ball are assist trophies with no fighter kit and no neutral smash, so they are NOT designed.

## The 8 concepts I am least sure about

1. **Donut, up (Glaze Pad, TRAP/PLACE):** a trap does not feel like an up smash; may read better as a LAUNCHER.
2. **Cloudy, up (Raincloud Pop, TRAP/PLACE):** overlaps his neutral bolt-from-the-sky smash; the cloud may need to look clearly different.
3. **Black Hole, up (Gravity Pop, BURST):** a pull-then-pop next to his Event Horizon pull; must not become a second pull.
4. **Pillow, down (Cushion Pop, LAUNCHER):** a lifter in the low family; foes must stay low, not be popped high.
5. **Basketball, up (Fast Break, SPIN):** near the Alley-Oop up special (also a rising move); the spin must look sideways-on.
6. **Magnet, down (Horseshoe Clamp, THRUST):** two arms closing from both sides near Polarity's pulls; keep it a plain clamp, no pull.
7. **Blocky, up (Cube Elevator, SLAM):** a rising cube is closer to a launcher or placed object than a slam; the class may change.
8. **Woody, up (Stump Spring, TRAP/PLACE):** his kit is light and evasive; a placed spike may feel too heavy, maybe a plain upward scramble instead.
