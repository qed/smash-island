# Skin renames, 2026-11-02

The owner: "skins could be better named." 118 of the 320 skins were renamed so the name says what the picture shows. Ids, picture files, prices and row order did not change: only the `name` text did.

**What counted as a weak name**

- (a) The leading word is a state, material, season or theme word shared by 3 or more skins (Blindfolded 16, Yoyle 9, Frozen 7, Season 5, Rockstar 5, Twinkle 5, Sweater 4, Snowy 4, Tangerine 4, Zombie 4, Cracked, Burnt, Snotty, Ghost, Announcer, Safety Vest, Hard Hat, Purple, Witch).
- (b) It read "Season N <Fighter>".
- (c) It is a vague adjective or a lore or era word that does not show the look (Damaged, Happy, Evil, Upgraded, CGI, BFDIA, Early BFB, Camera #1, Inner Flame, Thinking Hat).
- (d) It is clumsy (Half-Frozen Toothpaste, Glowing Gold Spoon, Tree the Green).
- Left alone: the 9 prize skins and the 104 other Halloween costumes (costume-role words such as Chef, Detective and Vampire are fine), except Chef Taco (II), which loses the clumsy (II).
- Only the 16 blindfold skins still share leading words (Orange 5, Lime 5, Blue 4, Gold 2), because their pictures differ only in team colour.

**Checks**: 320 skins before and after; all 320 names unique ignoring case; every new name is 27 characters or fewer and keeps the fighter's name; saved wardrobes store the skin id, so nothing saved or online changes.
