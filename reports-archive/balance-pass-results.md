# Balance pass: the results

*2026-10-28. The build measured is g-balance 707feec, the balance pass on top of ii-dlc 4b3397f. 26 fighters played 30 matches each, 780
matches in all, with 0 errors. **Then the plan's fallback was applied once** (g-balance 7300c53: Magnet's cut backed off, one more number
for 10 buffed fighters still at the bottom), and those 11 fighters played their 30 matches again: the "after adjustment" column and the
section [After the adjustment](#after-the-adjustment). Nothing was pushed.*

**How it was played.** The rules are the yardstick's: 2 stocks, Hard CPUs on both sides, Goiky Field with the off-stage room, items
off, sides alternating, and every KO credited to the move that landed it.
- **BFDI 15:** the yardstick's 15 BFDI mid-pack CPUs, with the win-rate run's own seeds. This gives a straight before/after.
- **Mixed 15:** the bottom study's 8 II foes (Blueberry, Bonesaw, Test Tube, Pickle, Fan, Silver Spoon, Paintbrush, Spikey), with that
  study's seeds, plus its 7 BFDI foes (Pen, Tennis Ball, Fanny, Firey Jr., Lightning, Profily, Yellow Face). The 7 BFDI matches use
  **new seeds**. With the old seeds they would replay the same matches as the BFDI 15 and be counted twice.
- As the plan says, **Barf Bag keeps her old smash (launch 8.5) when she is a foe**, so the yardstick does not move. Her buff counts only
  when she is the fighter being measured.
- **Noise:** a 30-match total is about +/-2 to 3 wins. CPU numbers are only a rough guide (you beat Expert CPUs without being hit).

**Targets on the 30:** Firey, Magnet and Bomby 22-26. The buffed fighters 8-16 (above 18 is an overshoot, 5 or fewer is still at the
bottom). Toothpaste and Match are report only.
