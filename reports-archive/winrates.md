# Smash Island win rates, and the moves that win them

*Run 2026-10-28 on the ii-dlc checkout (smash-bal). 102 fighters, 1530 matches, 0 errors. Measurement only: nothing in the game was changed or tuned.*

**How it was measured:** each fighter played 15 matches, 1 vs 1, against the same 15 mid-pack Hard CPUs (2 stocks, Goiky Field with the new off-stage room, items off). 50% means average: as strong as a typical mid-pack fighter. One 15-match run is noisy: a fighter's real result could easily be about 2 wins higher or lower, so a 7/15 and a 9/15 fighter may really be the same strength.

The 15 opponents were Pen, Tennis Ball, Fanny, Puffball, Remote, Firey Jr., Lightning, Profily, David, Nickel, Saw, Barf Bag, Coiny, Gaty and Yellow Face. When a fighter was one of those 15, a stand-in took that slot (for example, David played Grassy instead of himself). The whole roster averaged 44%, a little under 50%, because the 15 opponents are solid middle-of-the-pack fighters.

"KOs" counts stocks taken. "Falls" counts the fighter's own stocks lost with no hit in the 3 seconds before (a self-destruct). "Top KO moves" are the moves that took those stocks, with how many each. "jab" is the real basic attack. "finisher (no name)" means the game has no name stored for that finisher.

## Things to know about this run

- **Time limit:** 563 of the 1530 matches (37%) ran to the time limit. 417 of those had equal stocks and were decided on damage alone. Fighters who rarely finish KOs (and very defensive ones) get many of these.
- **Draws:** 3 (Candle vs Gaty, Pillow vs Coiny, Clover vs Barf Bag). Each ended with both fighters on the same stocks at 0% after a respawn.
- **Same fighter, two numbers:** the 15 opponents also appear in the table as fighters, with their own 15-match results (for example Firey Jr., Pen, David).
