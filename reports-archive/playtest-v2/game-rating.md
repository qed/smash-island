# How good is the game? (Playtest v2 rating)

**Important: these are 80 simulated players, not real people.** Each one is a persona (a made-up player with an age, a skill level and a patience limit) driven by a test bot. Their scores come from what the bot logged in their matches and menus. Some problems in the logs come from the bot, not the game. The main ones: it walks off ledges on purpose to act like a beginner, it can't see the smash charge-up, it can't jump gaps in RUNNING!, and it takes longer menu routes than a real player would. Those cases are marked as guesses below. Use this as a guide, not a final verdict.

## Headline numbers (all 80 players, out of 10)

| Measure | Score |
|---|---|
| **Overall (average)** | **6.17** |
| **Overall (median)** | **6** |
| Fun | 6.33 |
| Feel (controls, how it plays) | 5.69 |
| Clarity (easy to understand) | 5.99 |
| Content (amount to do) | 7.76 |
| Would keep playing ("yes") | 45% (36 of 80), the rest "maybe" |
| Would recommend ("yes") | 36% (29 of 80), the rest "maybe" |

How the overall scores spread out: 8/10 from 1 player, 7/10 from 24, 6/10 from 43, 5/10 from 12. **Nobody scored it below 5, and nobody said "no"** to keep playing or recommend. Every answer was "yes" or "maybe".

**In short: the content is strong (7.8), but feel (5.7) and clarity (6.0) pull the overall score down to about 6.**

What stands out:
- **Kids gave the lowest clarity (5.2).** They kept falling off in the first seconds, which is partly the bot. The menus also had too many words for them, and Esc didn't take them back.
- **Veterans are the most willing to keep playing (62%)**, but only 33% would recommend it. They like the roster and the bosses, but they don't trust their inputs and teams games time out.
- **Old-laptop players gave the worst feel score (4.4) and nobody in that group would recommend it.** Moves didn't come out and fighters seemed to teleport. Some of this is probably their low frame rate (a guess).
- **Explorers rated content almost perfect (8.9)** but fun only 6.0. They spent most of their time in menus.
- **The happiest players were the Boss Rush sibling rivals and the World Cup fans** (almost all 7s and the only 8). The **unhappiest** were the 11-12-year-old BFDI fans who kept falling off, and the old-laptop players.

## Top 5 things people loved

The count is how many of the 80 players named it as a highlight.

1. **Big, chaotic matches with lots of damage (34).** For example 1,031 dealt in an Ice FFA, 1,124 on TLC, and 1,012 in a Pillars teams game. When a match gets going, it's a real scrap.
2. **The huge roster (20).** Players picked a different fighter almost every match and liked playing BFDI characters they already know. Kids and parents enjoyed picking characters most of all.
3. **Boss Rush (19).** Lots of different bosses, each with a pattern to learn, and retrying is instant. It's the best-rated mode among kids and veterans.
4. **Extras and tournaments (15).** Wardrobe, Vault, Quests, Store, Level Creator, World Cup and Flag Cup. The World Cup and Flag Cup ran cleanly every time.
5. **How to Play and the practice modes (12, tied with fast starts at 12).** The tutorial was usually finished in a few clicks, and the CTF practice finished almost every time. Getting from the title to a match takes 7-17 s for players who click straight through.

Also mentioned: clean, fair 1v1s (9), and custom key remapping that worked and stayed saved (5).

## Top 5 things that dragged the score down

The count is how many players named it as a problem.

1. **Matches that stall to the 3-minute limit with no KO (25).** This happened most in Teams, but also in FFA (Canyon, Skytower, Fortress, Grand Plains, Yoyle). One veteran dealt 731 damage and still nobody was KO'd. Five CTF players had the related problem below. This was the most common complaint, and it hurt veterans the most.
2. **Menus too long or too wordy before the first match (22).** It took 63-93 s for some first-timers to reach a match, and 89-97 s for some to reach Training. Collectors spent a long time on the reward screens. Part of this is the bot taking longer routes than the real 3-click path (a guess about how much).
3. **Moves that didn't come out (21).** Mostly smash (V), but also specials, grabs and once a plain attack. The bot can't see the smash charge-up, so many smash cases are probably the bot (a guess). Specials and the jab are harder to explain that way. Either way, this is why feel is the lowest score.
4. **Evil Forest blowouts (20).** Players took 400-1,373 damage there and were KO'd by the damage cap at 230-453%. One player saw a percentage pass 1,000% ("thought the game had broken"). Players called it "a blender" and "a damage dispenser". Note that Forest CTF was praised. The complaint is about the Evil Forest stage.
5. **KO'd in the first seconds at 0% (16).** This hit kids most. The bot's beginner setting walks off ledges on purpose, so this is partly the bot, but it's the main reason kids' scores were low.

Also mentioned: fighters teleporting, getting stuck inside platforms or freezing in the air (15). Esc not going back on menu screens (13). Empty CTF matches with 0 damage dealt and 0 taken on the Sky, Mines and Factory maps (8). Evil Leafy KOs showing "?" with no attacker, so players didn't know what hit them (6). RUNNING! lost within seconds every time (5, mostly the bot). WASD doing nothing on a fresh game, with no key hint (4). How to Play step 8, "Press V once to SMASH", not moving on until the smash lands (3). Share, Sound and Level Creator Clear showing no visible change (4; the focus group says they do give feedback in a real browser).

## Verdict

The simulated players rated **Battle for Smash Island about a 6 out of 10: a fun, generous free fan game that doesn't feel finished in a few places.** Nobody hated it and nobody said no, but most players were a "maybe". The content is clearly the strength: the roster, Boss Rush, the tournaments and the extras all scored highly, and several players said it feels bigger than a fan game. The score is held back by how matches play and end, not by how much there is to do. Teams and some FFAs time out with nobody KO'd, Evil Forest wipes players out, presses sometimes seem to be eaten, and new players fall off at 0% before they've done anything. Menus are slow for first-timers, and Esc doesn't go back on them.

**What would raise the score most, in order** (my estimates, not measured):
1. **Make matches finish.** Look at why high-percent fighters in Teams and FFA aren't being KO'd. Players were sitting at 100-731 damage dealt with no stock lost.
2. **Calm down Evil Forest** so damage can't climb into the hundreds or past 1,000%.
3. **Check that moves always come out, especially specials and the jab.** For smash, show the charge more clearly so players can tell it's charging and not eaten.
4. **Add the cheap clarity fixes:** Esc goes back on every menu screen, a "controls: arrows, change in Settings" hint at the start, How to Play step 8 moves on when V is pressed, and Evil Leafy KOs show what hit you.

Items 1-3 address the two lowest scores (feel and clarity) and the three most common complaints. Together they would probably move most "maybe" players to "yes", and the overall score from about 6 toward 7 (a guess).
