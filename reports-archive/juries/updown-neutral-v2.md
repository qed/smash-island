(b) State hashes (every numeric/string/bool field of every fighter, plus counts of projectiles, beams, summons and particles, every 15 frames, 1800 frames, 2 seeds) over 23 matches of 4 fighters that cover all 92 playable fighters outside both lists: base vs A, base vs B.
(c) For A, the 12 fighters themselves in 6 matches (3000 frames): base vs "A with DSM_OWN emptied". This proves `rad`, `chain`, props, the SW_EXC pins, `dsmBodyK` and the FB table change nothing when no row uses them.
(d) Generated up/down rows, move-list text, MOVESETS and SMASH_SPEC of all 111 fighters, base vs each branch.

- B: 23 groups x 2 seeds identical to base (0 differ). ## 4. Jank
- My jank.mjs: A 168 cases (12 fighters x up/down x [hit, whiff, hit while winding up at frame 3, hit at frame 12, in the air, at the stage edge, into a shield]), B 49 cases (7 fighters x the same 7). 260 frames after each: any leftover `_sm`, `_windup`, `_mv`, `rooted`, `_combo`, `_nsv`, lag or hitstun? **None in any case; the fighter was free again every time** (A: 32 to 50 frames after a hit, shorter than the old moves in nearly every case; B: 17 to 40). Cancel paths (burrow, splat, halves, the chain moves) are clean. The "lost a stock" and wandering I saw in the stage-edge and some Marshmallow/Tissues cases are my harness (a still fighter on a small flat stage) and happen in the base build too. Tissues' sneeze recoil moves him less than the old beam's back-step (294 px vs 495 px of drift in my case).
- CPU: I did not run new CPU stuck tests for A. Builder's: 0 errors, 0 stuck in 12 CPU matches, but CPUs rarely press up/down smashes (6 of 24 came out through the CPU path), so "every A move works when a CPU throws it" is not proven. B (my Apple run): 0 errors, 76 smashes.
- What the four players would feel:
  - New kid: Snowball up, Bomby up, Bubble up, Tennis Ball up and Coiny up do not hit a foe standing right beside you (they hit overhead only; in my 50 px test they did 0). By design and the move text says where they hit, but it is a trap.
  - Competitive: the multi-step moves can deal far less than their listed total when the foe leaves range. In my pinned-foe test Pin down did 5.2 of 18, Leafy down 9.2 of 14.2, Teardrop 2.6 to 3.2 of 9; with a foe that is free to move (the online run) Pin down did 18.7. So the real value is in between, and several (Pin down, Teardrop up/down, Coiny down) will probably be clearly weaker than the move they replace. Not a break; a feel risk for the owner.
  - Party host: Bomby down costs him 3% on every use, also on a whiff or a shield hit (the text says so). Harmless.
  - Owner: no sky drops anywhere (Marshmallow's parcel slides in along the floor). All 31 props draw without an exception on a stub canvas, but no human (builder or me) has seen them.
