# Boss dodge audit (2026-11-09) -- measurement only; index.html untouched

Data: `boss-dodge-audit.csv` (one row per boss / phase / attack, 280 rows, 43 cells) and `boss-dodge-audit.tables.md` (the per-row table), both from pass 1 (`out3`). Script: `scripts/boss-dodge-audit.mjs` on g-bossaudit (commit 2140b24b); the scratchpad copy `...\scratchpad\bossaudit\boss-dodge-audit.mjs` has two later rules (a KO never counts as a dodge; a hazard hit taken while dodging counts) and pass 2 (`out4`) is running with them in the background -- when `batch4.log` ends in BATCH-DONE, re-run cells `0 1` and `0 2`, then `node scripts/boss-dodge-audit.mjs --merge <out4> smash-bal/reports` refreshes the CSV. Pass-1 caveat: some oracle "runL/runR" lines dodged by running off the stage edge (a KO) or into the pitcher plant (hazard); the 18/12-frame bot rates are not affected (they keep inside 6-10% of the width), the ORACLE column is optimistic.

Method. Real monolith in jsdom; each trial rebuilds the gauntlet scene (`spawnBossRushBoss`), sets the phase by HP, places the fighter (floor left / middle / right / the platform), forces the picker (`_pickForce`; One and Steve Cobs through their own `oneNextMove` / `cobsNextMove`) and counts every hit from the wind-up's first frame until `bossAttackOver`. Bots never attack: STILL (gives the real window: first hit frame); HUMAN-18 / HUMAN-12 react to what was visible 18 / 12 frames ago (shots with position, velocity and delay-mark, the marked spot, the boss body, the platforms) with the normal keys (run, held jump, double jump, fast fall); ORACLE tries ~130 scripted lines per spot (stand, run, jumps/hops/double jumps/fast falls at 13 offsets, hop rhythms, shield, to-platform, 0/4/8-frame reactors) and stops at the first 0-damage line. Roster: 3 seeds x 4 spots = 12 trials per bot per row; One / Steve Cobs: 1 seed x 4 spots. Verdict as asked: FAIR = h18 >= 70% and oracle 100%; HARD = h18 >= 40% and oracle 100%; UNFAIR = h18 < 40% or oracle fails.

READ THE VERDICTS WITH THIS: the bot reads shots and the marked SPOT, not tell shapes (a row, a cross, a hill, a vine row, a pull). Rows with low h18 but an oracle dodge found on line 1-3 ("still" or "run one way") are most likely bot blindness, not unfairness -- they need a human look. The HIGH findings are the rows where the oracle fails.
