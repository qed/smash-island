# BFDI Fighter Move & Animation Design

This document collects per-fighter move/animation designs for the Battle for Dream Island (BFDI) roster in Smash Island. Each fighter maps an **episode-grounded moveset** onto a shared, **exaggerated-cartoony animation** language (heavy squash/stretch, rubber-hose limbs, comic impact puffs) that plugs into the `FIGHTER_ANIM` hook already present in the code.

Every move is anchored to something canon (a running gag, an episode beat, or the character's literal object form). Where a move is an extrapolation rather than literal show canon, the **grounding** column flags it. Nothing here invents moves outside these designs — it is a faithful consolidation of the source material.

**Slot legend:** `X` basic melee · `down+X` ranged · `C` neutral special / signature · `up+C` up special / recovery · `down+C` down special · `hold V` smash / biggest charged hit · `X+C` cinematic finisher.

A note on color: nearly all hex values below are **approximate** reconstructions of the flat BFDI palette. The official wikis rarely publish exact hex codes (often only color *names*), so color-pick from an official asset before finalizing.

## Firey

**Summary:** The original BFDI character and Season 1 winner (406 votes) — a living flame who is energetic, competitive, hot-tempered in the most literal sense, and defined by his rivalry-turned-friendship with Coiny (299 slaps in Season 1). Everything he touches burns; his one great weakness is water.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| down+X | Ember Spit | Hocks a small arcing ember that bursts into a brief flame patch on landing, applying a short burn DoT (light hit + lingering tick). Good zoning/edge-guard tool. | Inhales — flame squashes and swells round, flame-tip cheeks puffing huge — then spits a glowing pip of himself; a mini Firey-Jr-shaped flamelet arcs out trailing smoke and splats into a wobbling fire puddle. | Firey ignites things at range; the spat flamelet references Firey Jr., the smaller flame that splits from the Firey concept. |
| C | Living Flame | Close-range ignition burst that sets any struck opponent ON FIRE — moderate hit + strong burn status ticking over several seconds (stacks if reapplied). Simple black BFDI face — two oval eyes, a plain curved mouth — shifting readily between neutral, grinning (running), and panicked (falling/high). Two thin black noodle arms and legs, no hands/feet, no hair or accessories. Can naturally stretch tall/flare or squash into a small pilot-light. Hex values are approximate — verify against an official BFDI asset.

## Leafy

**Summary:** The beloved BFDI Season 1 runner-up (~236 votes, Squashy Grapes) — relentlessly cheery and helpful on the surface but morally complex, with a genuine ego about her own kindness ("77 levels of niceness") that flips to entitled, vindictive fury when rejected. ## Bubble

**Summary:** A female translucent soap bubble and one of BFDI's most beloved fragile characters (3rd place, Squishy Cherries) — kindhearted, cheerful, anxious, best friends with Pencil. Famous for her vowel-swapping speech ("yoy" for "yay," "Yoylecake!") and her defining trait: she pops at the slightest touch, and keeps coming back.

## Blocky

**Summary:** A red anthropomorphic wooden building block — THE prankster of the cast, whose "Blocky's Funny Doings International" (BFDI, the show's namesake acronym) is a catalog of ~11 pranks. In Season 1 a mean-spirited bully whose jokes maim or kill; his cruelest signature is rigging Recovery Centers into an infinite recover-and-instantly-die loop. First contestant ever to rejoin after elimination.

| down+X | Needle Volley | Throws a spread of 2-3 sharp needles as fast, low-arc piercing projectiles; small damage each but they 'pop' shielded/inflated foes for bonus effect. Good zoning. | His rubber-hose arm stretches WAY back off-screen, wobbling, then whips forward like a slingshot flinging a fan of glinting needles that whistle; his cube body recoils with a comic 'sproing.' | Wielded Pin and Needle to try to pop Bubble in 'Take the Plunge: Part 1' — sharp objects as thrown weapons. |
| C | Funny Doings Trap | Deploys a rigged mini Recovery Center as a placed trap. Any foe that touches it (or is knocked in) is 'recovered' and instantly crushed for a burst of damage. One active at a time — signature control/zoning. | Whips out a tiny cardboard Recovery Center that pops open with a 'ding'; a wobbling trampoline unfolds and Spongy plops on. On connect the foe is boxed in by rigged Recovery Centers and caught in a rapid recover-and-die loop of escalating hits, ending in a big launch. High damage, hard to escape. | Snaps his fingers and a ring of Recovery Center boxes plus Spongy-trampolines slams down around the victim. Face mirrors Flower's: mischievous, angled/slanted eyes and a smirking upturned smile (frown variant flips downturned). When hit he chips, cracks along the grain, and sheds splinters/sawdust — lean into wood deformation (creaking, warping, toppling like timber).

## Pencil

## Rocky

**Summary:** A small igneous/quartzite rock contestant defined by one enormous gag: he barfs. Strange, chaotic, child-like — a passive "liability" who mostly sits with a blank happy smile and barfs on a whim. Lost the ability to talk in 2010 and mostly "speaks" only the noise "bulleh" while vomiting. The Official Character Guide frames him as a "vomiting superhero" who uses barf to propel himself, melt things, and make art — voted 1st into BFDIA. Rare flashes of genuine rage (his TPOT elimination) hint his strangeness may be partly a choice.
