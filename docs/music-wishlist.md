# Music wishlist — owner's tracks

Tracks the owner wants in the game, and where to buy them. **None of these ship with the repo.**
They are commercial recordings; they go in the in-browser **Custom Music picker** (Settings →
Custom Music), which stores them in IndexedDB on that device only. Nothing is uploaded, so this is
private listening rather than redistribution. Do *not* put them in `assets/music/custom/` — that
folder ships to every visitor.

## Cheapest legitimate basket

All of these live across three Toby Fox albums, all DRM-free on
[tobyfox.bandcamp.com](https://tobyfox.bandcamp.com):

- **DELTARUNE Chapter 1 OST** — Raining Somewhere Else
- **DELTARUNE Chapter 2 OST** — $7, 46 tracks — Killer Queen, BIG SHOT
- **DELTARUNE Chapter 3&4 OST** — ~$9 — TV Time, Hammer of Justice, Black Knife, Cutie Mew Mew Magic

Roughly **$25 for all three**, which also brings along Rude Buster, THE WORLD REVOLVING, and the
rest of the boss library. Buying individual tracks elsewhere (iTunes/Amazon, ~$1.29 each) only wins
if the list stays under about six songs.

Also worth having for the boss/miniboss playlists: the **UNDERTALE Soundtrack** ($9.99, 101 tracks —
Megalovania, Hopes and Dreams, Spider Dance, Death by Glamour) and **Christopher Larkin's** Hollow
Knight albums (per-track purchase available; Gods & Nightmares is the boss-rush album).

## How the slots work

Each slot is a **playlist**, not one file — load several tracks and the game picks one per context,
never repeating back to back. Boss Rush re-rolls on every new boss, so a gauntlet cycles the whole
boss library. The intense slot fires at clutch time (last stock, 90%+ damage, boss under 25%).
