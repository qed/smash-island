Frequently asked questions

Short, honest answers about Battle for Smash Island.

Play now (free, in your browser) ▶

Is Battle for Smash Island free?

Yes. It is free to play in your browser, with no download, no account and no ads.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No. Open smashisland.vercel.app in a browser on a computer and press Play. Your progress (unlocks, looks, key remaps) is saved on that computer by the browser.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts and no sign-in, including for online play: a room code is all you share.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

No. It is made for a computer with a keyboard, and it is not set up for touch screens.

Is this an official Battle for Dream Island or Inanimate Insanity game?

No. It is an unofficial fan game made by a fan of the object shows. Battle for Dream Island belongs to jacknjellify and Inanimate Insanity belongs to its creators; this game is not made or endorsed by them.

How do I play online with friends?

Press Play with Friends on the title screen and create a room. You get a four-letter room code; share it or the link, and your friends join from their own computers. The host picks the mode and stage and starts the match. See online play.

How many fighters and stages are there?

102 playable fighters from Battle for Dream Island and Inanimate Insanity, and 16 stages, plus the Capture the Flag maps and the Level Creator. You start with a few fighters and unlock more as you play. See the fighter list and the stage list.

How do I report a bug or send feedback?

The game's Options screen has a Thanks roll and a tip jar, and the title screen has a Share button. For a bug, note the fighter, the stage, the mode and what happened, and send it to the owner through the channel where you found the game (the game is posted by its maker on BFDI fan communities). Keeping the description short and exact helps it get fixed.
