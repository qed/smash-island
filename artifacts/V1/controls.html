Controls

The default keys, and what they do together.

Battle for Smash Island is played on a computer with a keyboard. These are the default keys, read from the game's own key table; every one can be remapped on the Controls screen, and the remaps are saved on your computer. Multiplayer is online, so there is one set of keys.

Default keys

KeyWhat it does
←Move Left
→Move Right
↑Jump
↓Down / Crouch
XAttack (+ ↑/↓: tilts; + an arrow in the air: aerials)
CSpecial (+ arrows; with Attack: grab)
VSmash (with Special: finisher; + ↑ / ↓: up and down smash)
ZSprint (tap while moving) / Shield (hold; the strong fighters get a Reflector instead)
EscPause
SpacePut flag down (Capture the Flag)
PTeam plan menu (Capture the Flag)
BNext piece (Capture the Flag editing)

Combinations (from How to Play)

  • ↑ jumps; press it twice for a double jump. A quick tap is a short hop, a held press a full jump.
  • ↑ or ↓ with X on the ground: tilts. In the air, any arrow with X: aerials.
  • ↑ + C: up special. ↓ + C: down special.
  • X + C close to a foe: grab. Then X pummels and an arrow throws.
  • V: smash. A ring fills for about half a second, then it goes off by itself toward the nearest foe. ↑ + V: up smash; ↓ + V: down smash. Some smashes break shields: watch for the glow.
  • V + C: finisher.
  • Hold Z: shield (it blocks hits, not grabs). Tap Z while running: sprint. Nine strong fighters get a shot-turning reflector instead of a bubble.
  • ↓ in the air after the top of a jump: fast fall. Hold ↓ to drop through a platform.
  • Against a wall in the air, hold toward it and press ↑: wall jump (once per wall).
  • When hit, hold a direction to bend your launch a little; tap directions during the hit freeze to slip out of a multi-hit move.

The in-game How to Play page, the Move list on the pause menu and the All moves tab on every character card show each fighter's moves with their keys.
