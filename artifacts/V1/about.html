What is Smash Island, the free BFDI fighting game?

Battle for Smash Island is a platform fighter in the style of Super Smash Bros., made by a fan for fans of the object shows. Every fighter has their own moves drawn from the show: Firey's flames, Bubble's bubbles, Bow's chairs, Tennis Ball's gadgets, and many more. Knock your friends off the stage, or launch them past the edge of the screen.

Ways to play

  • Free-for-all and Teams on stages from the shows, some with real edges to fall from.
  • Play with friends online: make a room, share the code, pick your sides.
  • Capture the Flag: hide your flags, build your base, then raid the other team's.
  • Boss Rush: team up against the show's biggest villains.
  • World Cup tournaments and a new Daily match every day.
  • Unlock fighters as you play, earn Win Tokens, and dress up your favourites.

Controls

Arrow keys to move and jump, X to attack, C for specials, V to smash, and V + C for your finisher. Hold Z to shield (a few strong fighters get a projectile-reflecting pane instead), tap Z while you run to sprint, and press ↑ or ↓ with V for an up or down smash. Grab is X + C. Every move is listed on the fighter select screen.

Frequently asked questions

Is Battle for Smash Island free?

Yes. It is free to play in your browser, with no download, no account and no ads.

Is it an official Battle for Dream Island or Inanimate Insanity game?

No. It is an unofficial fan game made by a fan of the object shows. Battle for Dream Island and Inanimate Insanity belong to their creators.

Is this the same as the other "Battle for Smash Island" projects?

No. Several fan projects share this name, including a fan wiki series and other games. This one is a free platform fighter with 102 Battle for Dream Island and Inanimate Insanity characters that you play in your browser, with online play, Boss Rush and World Cup tournaments.

What does BFDI stand for?

BFDI is Battle for Dream Island, the object show whose characters star in this game, along with the characters of Inanimate Insanity.

How many fighters are there?

There are 102 characters to fight with, each with their own specials, smash, finisher and Final Smash. You unlock more as you play.

Can I play online with friends?

Yes. Choose Play with Friends, make a room, share the code and pick your sides. You can also play Capture the Flag online.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

It is made for a computer with a keyboard, so it is not set up for phones or tablets.

How do I shield, sprint and smash?

Hold Z to shield, tap Z while running to sprint, and press V to smash. Hold up or down with V for an up or down smash. The in-game How to Play page and each fighter's move list explain the rest.

What are Boss Rush, the World Cup and the Daily match?

Boss Rush is a team-up against the shows' biggest villains. The World Cup is a tournament of 16, 32 or 48 teams. The Daily match is a new challenge match every day.
