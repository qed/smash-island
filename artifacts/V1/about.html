Battle for Smash Island

A free, fan-made BFDI fighting game you play right in your browser. Pick from 102 characters from Battle for Dream Island and Inanimate Insanity and fight it out on Smash Island.

Play now ▶

No download, no account. Works on a computer with a keyboard.

What is Smash Island?

Battle for Smash Island is a platform fighter in the style of Super Smash Bros., made by a fan for fans of the object shows. Every fighter has their own moves drawn from the show: Firey's flames, Bubble's bubbles, Bow's chairs, Tennis Ball's gadgets, and many more. Knock your friends off the stage, or launch them past the edge of the screen.

Ways to play

  • Free-for-all and Teams on stages from the shows, some with real edges to fall from.
  • Play with friends online: make a room, share the code, pick your sides.
  • Capture the Flag: hide your flags, build your base, then raid the other team's.
  • Boss Rush: team up against the show's biggest villains.
  • World Cup tournaments and a new Daily match every day.
  • Unlock fighters as you play, earn Win Tokens, and dress up your favourites.

Controls

Arrow keys to move and jump, X to attack, C for specials, V to smash, and V + C for your finisher. Every move is listed on the fighter select screen.
