What's new

The game’s own change list, newest first.

13 November 2026 Update 4

  • Directional influence: hold a direction as a hit lands and your launch bends that way, up to 15 degrees, with the same speed. Tap directions during the hit freeze to slide a little (6 px a tap, 18 px at most per hit), enough to slip out of a later hit of a many-hit move. It does not work while you are grabbed, frozen, rooted, asleep or caught by a Final Smash. CPUs use it too: Easy rarely, Expert almost always.
  • Swinging attacks now hit as they swing. A tilt or aerial that sweeps an arc hits only the part of the arc it has reached so far, tip first, so a far-away foe is no longer hit on the very first frame. Reach, damage, launch and frame counts are the same; only when each part can hit changed. The streak is drawn where the hit is.
  • Frame budget: fast, strong specials, smashes and finishers now leave you stuck for a moment afterwards (18 to 28 frames for strong ones, 8 to 13 for weak ones, 12 to 20 for slow smashes), so a foe can punish a miss or a block. A move that connects recovers much sooner, so combos still work. Throws still come out on the press. Hard and Expert CPUs punish a fighter who is stuck.
  • Very fast smashes, specials and finishers that could knock you out now show a short gather (glow, dust and a rising whistle) of at least 6 frames before they hurt (up specials and throws are not changed). No single move holds a foe without control for more than 45 frames (roots, holds, pins and freezes all stop at 45).
  • Run speed now follows weight: the lightest fighters run about 25% faster than the middle, the heaviest about 25% slower. Heavy fighters no longer get the old extra slowdown on top.
  • Air speed follows weight too: about 10% faster steering in the air for the lightest, 10% slower for the heaviest. The floaters (Puffball, Balloon, Balloony, Bubble) steer about 12% better than their weight says, and Tree stays slow in the air.
  • Shield breakers: twelve neutral smashes now break a raised shield in one hit (the shield pops into the normal long stun and half the smash's damage goes through). Each one charges with a hot red glow so you can see it coming. Hard and Expert CPUs drop their shield when they see it. Up and down smashes do not break shields. The smash line on the fighter card says "Breaks shields."
  • Unique moves: forty-six fighters had their tilts, aerials, specials or smashes remade so they no longer look like each other. Names are in the balance notes below.
  • Final Smashes now stop at walls. A shot, hit or catch from a Final Smash no longer goes through solid rock or a wall, so Bow's chairs and others cannot hit a foe they cannot reach. Open stages are unchanged.
  • Bow's finisher now comes out on small platforms and ledges: her chair tower can topple off a ledge or the other way, shrinks under a low ledge, and a finisher that finds no room on the ground gives the shared swing instead of a stray neutral special.
  • Boss fights are fairer: more time and room to dodge. Swarm Wave now drops bugs in lit columns with a clear gap; the Sonic Blast has a longer phase 2 wind-up; You Must closes its hooks slower; Private shutters are narrower with the floor marked; Torture Time's panes shut later; the Announcer's crusher has a longer tell; the Purple Dragon, MePhone4S and Four got longer wind-ups on several moves; Consequences phase 3 was slowed and now shows where it will land. Firey Speaker Box's TLC now pauses where it lands before each ring, marks the next landing, keeps to the side it started toward instead of turning mid-chain, and its rings are spaced out and shorter; the high twin ring that could not be jumped is gone. Damage and knockback are unchanged.
  • Capture the Flag: after 4 minutes with no capture, CPUs send more attackers (a fifth more each minute) so stalemates on maps like Crystal Deeps and Coral Deep end. Escape now closes the picker and goes back a screen on the Flag Cup screens. The map check shows a "Checking routes..." panel with a percent and the seconds so far.
  • Esc now goes Back on the menu screens (Settings, Controls, Stats, How to Play, Training, the vault, quests, shop, wardrobe, rankings, World Cup setup, fighter select, and the lobby when you are not in a room). How to Play has a Back button, and a step-8 smash that misses says "Missed! Try again closer". If you press keys that do nothing (W A S D or I J K L on the default arrows), the pause or result screen shows a one-time line naming the keys that work.
  • New players: your first 3 matches show a small strip with your own keys (it follows your remaps; turn it off in Settings), the lesson and How to Play use your real keys, and the arrow-keys hint can now show during a match. Gamepads: Start pauses, LB or LT shields, and the pad works the pause menu.
  • Online rooms: a check mark shows who has picked a fighter, and the host is asked before starting with players who haven't; with 6 or more players in Free-for-all (which holds 5) the host is offered Teams; if the host leaves, you can make a new room in one click; a join that fails now says the room may be full or offline.
  • More of the show's own pictures on attack props: Fries' darts, Soap's cloth and vacuum, Money's coins and bags, Marshmallow's saw blade, Poppy's kernels, Teddy Bear's TV, Coiny's monitor, Book's quill, Profily's cactus, Stapy's staple, Pie's pie, Spongy's cannonballs, Microphone's dodgeball, Pickle's needle and Firey Jr.'s big fireball.
  • Trophy's handles are now part of his body instead of swinging like arms.
  • Online: game snapshots are smaller (slow fighter details are sent only when they change).
  • Fixes: respawning on Yoyle Needle now puts you on a ledge instead of dropping you through the whole shaft, and Frozen Pond's outer start spots no longer drop you off the edge at 0%. Damage can no longer run past the knockout limit from hazards, burn or bleed: a fighter who is over it is knocked out. Being knocked out now frees you from any hold (a stomach, a grab). The KO banner names Evil Leafy, the lava or the boss instead of a blank. In Teams, a base shared by its own team no longer hurts that team. CPUs on Yoyle Needle fall off the ledges less.
  • New Teams map: Teams on Yoyle Needle now takes place INSIDE the giant needle: a walled maze of rooms around a central shaft, with express belts, a zipline, levers that hold the lifts and rails, and berry bushes. It grows with the number of players and has four layouts.
  • New Teams map: Teams on The TLC now takes place inside the chamber: a sealed steel box with bouncy walls, a sliding lid you can be launched through, hatches and trapdoors that flash and sound a klaxon before they open, cage lifts, a scoop arm, and four different bases. It grows with the number of players and has four layouts.
  • The gorges on Yoyle Canyon, Sky Towers and Grand Plains are now deadly: fall well below the floor and stay there, out of hitstun, and you are knocked out (a red line shows the depth). You still have time to jump or up special back out, and the KO banner says you fell into the gorge.

Money's and Book's Final Smashes now throw their coin bags and books from the fighter across the stage, and Paper's two pianos roll along the floor (both now connect, so it hits harder than before). Final Smash shots still stop at walls.

8 November 2026 Update 3

  • New stages with shapes of their own: Dream Island (floating islands over the sea, no ground at all), The TLC (a steel bowl with walls and steps; you are knocked out over the top or down the hatch, and a launch into a wall bounces off it), and Yoyle Needle (a tall shaft of zig-zag ledges with four moving ledges that carry whoever stands on them).
  • More new stages: Frozen Pond (a slippery bowl between solid rims), Shifting Isles (the stage changes shape every minute, with a warning glow and a low tone before it moves) and Windy Heights (a long bridge where a gust pushes everyone every ten seconds, with a short warning first).
  • Event contests: the third match of some event weeks is now a contest with its own goal. Cloudy: hold all nine kinds of item for 10 seconds; every third separate attack on you steals one. Eraser: Spleef, thirty floors of tiles that fall two seconds after you touch them, last one standing wins. Spongy: dig out to a corner exit, find your buried moves on the way, and the first ones out fight stronger.
  • Event matches show a short goal line for the first 5 seconds, so you know what the twist or contest asks of you.
  • Spleef shows a slim depth bar at the right edge in place of the minimap: the 30 floors from top to bottom with a dot for each fighter.
  • A small CONTEST tag marks each event fighter's third match on the event page and the select screen.
  • Balance: no fighter was changed in this update.

6 November 2026 Update 2

  • New: SHIELD. Hold Z for a bubble that shrinks as it blocks and breaks into a long stun at zero. It lowers in 5 frames and still blocks for 3 frames after you let go. In the air or in hit-stun you cannot shield. Out of the shield each move has its own wait: a jab 8 frames, a down tilt 12, a smash 22, a special 30 and a grab 28 (a shield grab is a hard read). A grab on a shielding fighter breaks the shield and stuns the grabber for 40 frames, with no damage and no throw: the shielder is not stunned, but cannot shield again for about 2 seconds. The CPU no longer prefers to grab shielders. Z can be remapped.
  • New: REFLECTORS. Toothpaste, Magnet, Tennis Ball, David, Puffball, Firey, Bomby, Lollipop and Pencil have no bubble: their Z is a reflector pane that turns shots back faster (1.8 times the speed, 1.3 times the damage, at most 12% a shot). Melee and grabs go through it.
  • New: SPRINT. Tap Z while you are running on the ground to sprint at 1.4 times your run speed, for as long as you keep running the same way. Stopping, turning, jumping, attacking, getting hit or raising a shield ends it. Hold Z while running and the shield (or reflector) comes up 6 frames later than from standing still; standing still, Z works as before. No cost, no meter.
  • New: UP SMASH and DOWN SMASH for every fighter (hold up or down with the smash key; press up and smash together and the jump is cancelled). Left, right and neutral smash are unchanged. A smash no longer waits while you hold the key: it goes off when the charge ring fills.
  • Balance: every plain jab now hits 2 to 4% a hit (it was up to 12%): the old order is kept, so the strongest jabs are still the strongest. Teardrop's jab is 1.5%. Boosted jabs shrink by the same ratio. The nine weakest fighters (Cammy, Lifering, Balloon, Starfruit, Tea Kettle, Box, Paper, Tissues, Apple) have 12% more jab reach.
  • Balance: the plain jabs of Firey, Cake, Match, Magnet, Pencil, Rose, Snowball, Tapey, Book and Barf Bag launch less, so a jab no longer knocks out from centre stage so early; Black Hole's up throw launches a little less.
  • Balance: Puffball's air bop is 8% (was 13%). Clover's jab is 2.3% (was 5%) and her boosted jab 6.4%. Clover's butterfly chain weakens its hit-stun as it goes on (full on the first two catches, then 80%, 60% and at least 45% of it), so a foe caught in a long chain has more time to act, and her finisher spins up for 24 frames (was 12), so a foe a jab or tilt just hit can get out before it connects.
  • Balance: Lightning's up air now launches much harder: it knocks out a mid-weight foe from about 122% (was 158%).
  • Fairness: dash specials now start with a 16-frame wind-up with a glow, dust and a rising whistle, and a hit on the fighter cancels the dash: Bomb, Snowball, Leafy, Balloon, Pickle, Fan, Liy, MeTag, Woody, Bot, Teddy Bear, Donut's Sprinkle Roll, Saw's and Taco's spin rush, Sidewalky's slam, Balloony's air leak and Paintbrush's Heat Explosion. Snowball's finisher can be escaped by mashing and lasts at most 45 frames. Every freeze lasts at most 45 frames. The Speaker Box boss's phase 3 SONIC BLAST! winds up for longer and leaves a wider safe gap.
  • Fairness: you are knocked out exactly at the edge of the screen (the camera can no longer pass the KO line). Finishers (V + C) cannot be used in the first 8 frames after you leave hitstun. A Final Smash catch lands about a third of a second after the freeze (22 frames, was 10) and its reach is at most 300 pixels, so it can be seen coming and dodged.
  • Comeback fighters glow with an aura, a ring and pips that grow with their comeback points; Nickel's body heats up as his bonus grows.
  • Final Smashes: every fighter card shows its Final Smash name and one plain line about it (Kit tab, All moves tab and the pause Move list). Pencil's van drives across the stage; TV's spider is drawn like the show's robot spider; Blueberry's and Cake's have a name banner, a dim, a flash and a camera push-in; Pillow's has her Journal standing beside the book; 25 props now use the show's own pictures (Gelatin's sign, Poppy's pan, Tennis Ball's trebuchet and balls, Pin's balloon, Bow's chairs, Match's van and many more); Gelatin's dining room stays on the floor; the camera stays on Pencil's van.
  • Controls and menus: key remaps are saved on your computer (Escape and R cannot be given to an action); in Teams and Capture the Flag every fighter has a shape under its team ring so teams do not depend on colour alone; quitting to the title asks you to confirm in Boss Rush, the Daily and online matches.
  • Game feel and art: name tags for up to 6 fighters that no longer cover each other, a louder KO banner that says who was knocked out, jump and attack presses are buffered, online guests predict better through held keys, a small bump when you take damage, steadier screen shake, hit sounds with some pitch variety, a ring and extra sparks on every hit, a soft shadow under fighters, flat-based clouds, softer Final Smash darkening, a Goiky Field horizon that blends into the water, a glow behind the winners, locked fighter cards with a padlock, readable dark skins (Scorched Baseball too), and a Store line that says when costumes return. The crowd faces on the floor are hidden in Boss Rush, Teams, Capture the Flag and Final Smashes.
  • In a small window the name tags, damage numbers, stock dots, standings and KO banner stay larger and easier to read; in a normal window nothing changes.
  • Events: the event rotation moved forward seven weeks. The event match twists are in: 8-Ball's Basket Into X (one floating basket hands out the items) and Lick Your Way to Freedom (jawbreakers drop, and one in three has somebody inside), Cloudy's The Collection (you keep the kinds of item you pick up, and a full set is a Final Smash), Eraser's Take the Plunge (last on the bar: the floor is a thin slab that drops away from both ends, two thirds of it by the end, with bigger crashes) Spongy's Get Digging (a hard landing digs a hole in the floor), and Pie's Cake at Stake (berries rain from above: touch one to heal a little) and 500 Steps (a staircase of platforms rises one step at a time, with the throwables on the top step).

3 November 2026 Update 1

  • Every fighter's Final Smash now opens with its own short entrance animation before the blast comes out.
  • Bow's Teleport no longer cancels her speed: if she is hit and teleports, she keeps flying the way she was thrown. It also no longer comes back when she is hit, only when she lands.
  • Dozens of fighters got brand-new staged Final Smashes: they catch a foe and take them through a little scene, and a landed one now does about 100% (before, most did 10 to 30%). Bow's is eight chair slams that she swings herself, 12.5% each, and Pencil hops into her van first.
  • A Final Smash that does not knock a foe out now always leaves them at least in the orange.
  • 20 fighters got their own finisher (V + C) in place of the shared heavy swing: Nickel (II), Test Tube, Saw, Fan, Soap, Tissues, Yin-Yang, Starfruit, Blueberry, Cherries, Clover, Jack, Magnet, MeTag, Poppy, Silver Spoon, Tapey, Tea Kettle, Teddy Bear and Bot.
  • Two hidden fighters got finishers of their own.
  • The camera shows more when you are guarding an edge: the view is wider, it pulls out to show a fighter recovering near you together with the ledge, and small arrows at the screen edge point at recovering fighters and at anyone launched above the top of the screen.
  • 118 skins have clearer, look-based names.
  • Test Tube's darts no longer home in, and fighters who are scrambled or knocked out show a small mark.
  • Balance: Bubble no longer survives her first KO each stock; to make up for it she is heavier (weight 66) and her Raft Bump hits harder.
  • Balance: Bow's chair slams launch a standing foe from higher up, so the heaviest fighters (Tree, for one) can now be knocked out by them from 0%.
  • Balance: Nickel's Final Smash was toned down so the heaviest fighters survive it at 0% (they are knocked out from about 10% to 23%).
  • Balance: Clover's Final Smash is now called Luck Chain, with a slightly weaker launch.
