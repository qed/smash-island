What's new

The game’s own update notes, newest first.

Play now (free, in your browser) ▶

Update 13 25 November 2026

  • Sparring Figures, in Training: a CPU that learns your habits, counters them and levels up to 50. After a match it lists your weaknesses.
  • Every up special is its own kind of recovery: teleports, tethers, glides, hovers and more. Some leave you helpless.
  • Grab now beats shield (shield beats attack, attack beats grab). Single-screen stages have bigger blast zones. Getting hit off a ledge no longer gives you a jump or your up special back.
  • Smashes: most fighters have their own neutral, up and down smashes. They reach about as far as the old ones and start sooner, short ones hit hard up close, and CPUs know them. Bow's up smash is a lasso that catches through shields.
  • Limit: replaces comeback scaling for 11 fighters, like Naily and Ruler. Take 100% and your neutral special, down special and neutral smash each get one powered use.
  • Gelatin: Ice Breaker is back, with Steak Sizzle, the cycling Daily Specials finisher and Spikeball Headphones. Cherries' finisher is a command grab.
  • Rose: Thorn Lash is a vine you can see coming and dodge.
  • Multi-hit attacks now hold the foe until their last hit, so the finisher connects. Tree's Growth Rings and Sticker's Backstab can KO.
  • Marshmallow: her smash's spring pad bounces once and stays a few seconds; jump on it to spring her and her teammates up high.
  • Original characters: four moves per slot, their own up specials and drawn shots. Simpler OC editor and event pages.
  • Move cards show every effect and any cost to you; frame data counts from your key press.
  • 8-Ball's contest: a new room layout every match, assists with three moves each, and it plays online. CPUs collect one type at a time and swap with a purpose, swaps are quicker, and assists set foes up for your finishing hit. Dig Games: sky walks, themed ground and caves with supplies.
  • Boss Rush: Springy gets new phases; DODGEBALL!, THE TLC and SCREECHY!!! are fairer. Bubble's smash no longer misses under platforms.
  • Online: the game rejoins by itself after a drop, guests no longer jitter, and a room says when this week's event can't be played there.
  • Fixes: Final Smashes, air finishers, cup menus, and CPUs in Capture the Flag, Faultline, Dig Games and Sticker's maps.
  • Nerf: Black Hole's smash and finisher, Puffball's smash, Ruby's beam, Bomby's mine and Rose's Thorn Lash are weaker.
  • Nerf: side smashes launch less for Yin-Yang, Puffball, Microphone, Bottle, Nickel (II), Eraser, Pickle, Candle, Dora, Baseball, Bomb, Cammy, Lightning, 8-Ball, Stapy, Jack and Bomby.
  • Buff: Needy, Jammy, Starfruit, Lifering, Fern and Snowball hit harder; Roboty reaches and runs farther; Cloudy falls faster; Cammy's Snapshot stuns longer; Spongy starts with water; Sidewalky smashes sooner; Pin gets Point First; Needy, Balloon, Snowball and Sticker are heavier.
  • Buff: Needy's and Jammy's damage boosts now really work. Spongy and Sticker hit harder, Sticker's peel launches farther, and the CPU Spongy, Needy and Sticker stop wasting their specials. Trophy's neutral smash KOs at 90%.
  • Buff: the multi-hit smashes of Beach Ball, Bomb, Cake, Tree, Basketball, Bot, Salt, Clock, Lifering, Woody, Naily, Saw and Firey Jr. now hold you to the launch; Sticker's Backstab launches farther. Nerf: Gelatin's and Dora's jabs launch a little less; Bomb's, Saw's and Donut's spins hit smaller each time, about the same in total.

Update 12 20 November 2026

  • 8-Ball's contest is like Pokémon: weaken wild assists, then walk into them to catch them. Carry 6 with 3 out fighting (A swaps); 3 of one type give your own hits its power. Each room favours its own types, so a set of 3 is easier to build.
  • Assists are show characters now, not fighters: 20 new ones (like Avocado, Price Tag and Winner) in 8-Ball's contest and the Assist item. Toilet and MePad use the show's pictures, and MePad hits harder.
  • Spongy's Dig Games has districts: each land has its own weapon (bow, trident, knives, axe, spear), and parts from that land, some of them buried, power it up. Everyone starts at the cornucopia, which empties after the first fight. CPUs now build their weapons too.
  • Sticker's Cheaters Prosper: CPUs find balls by looking and know only some shortcuts, so they can be beaten.
  • Black Hole's Back to the Top: computer players now use their up special to catch a ledge, and land on a lower ledge instead of falling past them all.
  • Beach Ball's volleyball: the ball no longer bounces twice off the top of the net and drops dead, and the beach has a pier, huts, a crowd, a scoreboard and more.
  • Cloudy's collection: every item has its own power you can feel and see on the fighter, like a glide from the Pinwheel, a freeze from the Hailstone, a guard from the Trophy and a bolt from the Lightning Bottle. Picking one up says what it does.
  • Every contest map is fully decorated, each room and area with its own look. In Clock's and Stapy's contests you hold Up to go through a door, like everywhere else.
  • Yoyle Needle: the lever now shifts the needle: rooms slide to new places and the express belts split into short belts.
  • Original characters are open: make up to eight in the editor and play them in Free-for-all and Teams, against CPUs or online.
  • Event fighters' skins are now real looks from the show, not recolours. More show pictures: Yellow Face's megaphone, Needle's magnet, the monitor thrown at Evil Leafy, and the Purple Dragon's sign, Recovery Center, key and chainsaw.
  • Frame data for every move: the Moves list gives startup and end lag, measured in the game, and the website shows every move's hitboxes frame by frame.
  • New up and down smashes of their own for Apple, Bracelety, Firey Jr., MeTag, Pickle, Poppy, Profily, Silver Spoon, Balloon, Bomb, Fern, Goo, Microphone, Pillow, Taco (II) and Tea Kettle.
  • Grabs stay out for 3 frames instead of 1.
  • Bow's Final Smash also catches a foe standing beside her, not only below her.
  • Simpler menus: one Stats screen, a shorter World Cup and Flag Cup setup, and How to Play is just your keys and Try it.
  • Fairer Boss Rush: FURIOUS ROAR!, BLACK HOLE!, TORTURE TIME!, THE TLC, SEEKERS! and WIND LOTTERY! give you more room or time to dodge.
  • CPUs on the big Teams maps stand still far less, pull the TLC's lever, wait properly for lifts and reach heal springs more often. Yoyle Needle Teams is fairer with 4 to 6 players.
  • Online: when someone else hosts, your fighter snaps back far less and other fighters move more smoothly. A room says when this week's event is offline, and guests ride Yoyle Needle's short belts smoothly.
  • Fixes: attack while holding someone pummels; holding C no longer repeats a special; Up just after V is the up smash; holding Up through a door no longer jumps you; Cherries no longer trips on their own oil; Dough's copied specials hit properly; Capture the Flag CPUs no longer wall themselves in on Mines.
  • Nerf: Cherries: her side smash launches less, so it no longer knocks out from very low damage.
  • Nerf: Ruler: hits deal 14% less.
  • Nerf: Bot: hits deal 10% less, runs a little slower, and The One and Only! hits softer.
  • Nerf: Toothpaste, Grassy: hits deal 10% less; Grassy's finisher launches less.
  • Nerf: Bomby: hits deal about 10% less. David: hits deal 8% less. Bomb: Collision comes back slower.
  • Buff: Starfruit: hits deal 15% more and One-Hit Wonder launches farther.
  • Buff: Dough, Lifering, MeTag, Candle: their KO moves launch farther (Dough's hits also deal 10% more; MeTag's jab reaches farther).
  • Buff: Foldy: Paper Cut Dash ends sooner (end lag 42 to 26 frames).
  • OCs: the Grappler default hits a little softer; utility moves leave you open for less time; Hex Ring, Tar Burst, Shove Wave and Trap Hop hit harder.

Update 8 18 November 2026

Stand on supplies to take them (better near the cornucopia), craft a map from paper, ink and a compass, and watch for the arena's fire, flood and sandstorm.
  • Clock's Fast Forward: each era is 5 times bigger, with pits to fall in. Plant a seed in the past and 8 gears only you can take appear in the future.

Update 7 18 November 2026

  • Event contests are simpler: each one keeps its main idea and its best few extras. No more how-to cards before a contest, and the event page shows just the goal.
  • Spongy's Dig Games: after the first minute a storm closes the arena in on the cornucopia. In tunnels you now fall, and climb up the walls.
  • 8-Ball's contest is shorter: the jawbreakers start falling by 1:30. Two assists of the same family hit harder.
  • Pie's 500 Steps now climbs 1000 steps.
  • Black Hole's contest starts slower, and computer players climb away from the hole before they fight.
  • Salt: Pepper now copies every move and step a beat late. Knock her away and she runs back, but she still copies Salt first.
  • Buff: Jammy: several moves start 2 frames sooner and reach further.

Update 6 18 November 2026

  • Event contests: the third event match of 8-Ball, Black Hole, Clock, Pie, Stapy and Foldy is now a contest with its own map and goal (the match names are kept).
  • 8-Ball's Lick Your Way to Freedom: a big candy factory with three rooms, chutes, conveyor belts and a lift loop. Deal 50 damage to a jawbreaker yourself to break it, and the assist inside is yours for the rest of the match (only 8 exist, at most 2 each). Jawbreakers come in four flavour families (fire, ice, heavy and sneaky, shown by colour and picture) and a rare giant, and later in the match they fall from above (a shadow shows where). Also: a candy press over a sticky tray (with a warning), licking with Down for a sugar rush, gumball machines and wrapper decoys. 6 stocks, last one standing wins.
  • Black Hole's Back to the Top: an endless climb away from a black hole that rises faster and faster; the last one left wins. Comets knock you down a ledge, a hit sends a foe toward the hole only when you are above them, and knocked-out fighters come back as ghosts who throw comets. Air jumps and up specials come back only when you land. On the way up you pass Yoyleland, Goiky, the clouds, the Moon and deep space (with an asteroid belt), each with its own ledges and sky. Watch for lightning in the clouds, gravity flips that pull you sideways (hold toward a wall to run up it), satellites, ledges that shake and break, falling stars (a stored jump), rocket parts (three build a rocket that flies you up 12 ledges; three hits knock one loose) and a rare lifeboat.
  • Clock's Fast Forward is now a time-travel heist over three big maps (the past, today and the future) joined by clock doors (press Down in one); the most gears at midnight (3 minutes) wins. Plant a seed in the past and 8 gears only you can take appear at that spot in the future; bury gears in the past's time capsule and dig them up in the future for 50% more. A KO drops half your gears. A rewind pickup (Space) snaps you back 3 seconds.
  • Pie's 500 Steps is now a race up a tower of 100 steps (5 points each) to the gold top step, with 1 stock: hits and thrown pies knock you down, damage never knocks you out, and the bottom steps crumble away upward, faster and faster (caught by it, you are out). The tower has a bakery, a kitchen and a pantry, full of shortcuts and traps: lifts (some break halfway), a pie cart, a dumbwaiter, outside ledges in the wind, oven vents that blast you up, pantry shelves, wrong-way escalators, cream spills, rolling pies, a chef who throws pies at the leader, laundry chutes that drop you 20 steps, and crumb sprays.
  • Stapy's The Search Party is now a lighthouse hunt in the dark: from a plaza in the middle, climb the four lighthouses (every floor is a parkour course; light all the lamps on a floor to open the hatch to the next) and stand on your lever at the top to light a piece of your emblem. The first to light all four pieces wins. Stand on a rival's lit lever for 5 seconds to turn their piece off. Outside it is dark except for your own light, and the plaza has sea-storm waves, two boats' searchlights and a foghorn (hold shield on the ground to brace). CPUs climb like people, with more slips at lower levels.
  • Foldy's Carried Off is now a long wind race: the wind carries everyone along and grows stronger, hits push you back against it, and nobody is knocked out (a fall puts you back a little). Take the high deck or the sheltered low route, catch paper planes, and hold Z to brace (blocks cannot move you). The course runs through four areas: Paper City (mail tubes that shoot you ahead, and folding walls), the Windmill Stretch, the Storm (lightning, tornadoes, and a big gap crossed by hot-air balloons) and the Folding Bridges, with updrafts, kites and a flock of cranes to catch on the way. First across the line wins.
  • Cloudy's The Collection now takes place on drifting sky islands (falling between them is a KO). There are 15 kinds of item in 5 sets of 3; each item gives a power while you hold it, and each finished set a bonus. Hold all 15 for 3 seconds to win (was all nine for 10). Only the third hit on you knocks an item loose (no more one-hit drops). New: a storm front, a rainbow slide, a thief crow, a glazier's bench, a jar you can pour into a puddle, a swap pedestal, a lost-and-found box, wind gusts, an item backpack and bushes to hide items in.
  • Eraser's Spleef keeps its rules and gets more: rubber tiles that bounce you once, glass tiles that go fast, steel tiles that hold two people, and hidden trapdoors (tinted once the first one drops). Landing on a rival's head breaks their tile. Ink spills make tiles break twice as fast, rubber stamps come down after a growing shadow (they break tiles and shove, no damage), floor 15 is a conveyor, and knocked-out fighters come back as ghost erasers who drop crumbs that rub out a tile. A sharpener waits at the bottom.
  • Spongy's contest is now The Dig Games, a Hunger Games-style battle: you and 7 others, 4 stocks, last one standing wins, on a huge map of hills, stone towers, snowy peaks, caves, a mine, fossil beds and an ice cave, everyone starting far apart. Every jab and tilt digs. Your smash, specials and finisher are buried around the map (until you find one, that button does a jab or tilt), and a lost stock drops one of them. Mind your hunger and thirst: eat berries (purple ones are poison) and fish, drink at wells and the river. Sponsor crates and the feast (from 1:30, then every 30 seconds) bring help; footprints, noise marks and a cannon for every stock lost show where the others are.

Update 5 16 November 2026

  • New Teams map: Teams on Shifting Isles now plays on The Faultline: four different bases (a butte with a cave, an arch, a quarry with a heal spring, a temple), the Hourglass in the middle, and island plates that slide to a new shape every minute (a rumble, a shake and coloured outlines warn you first), with shuttles over the gaps, sandfalls and quicksand.
  • New Teams map: Teams on Windy Heights now plays on The Long Span: a lighthouse beacon, a long plank bridge stepping down the hillside with a broken span crossed by a gondola, a nest of huts hung under the high end, an anchor rock, an old ruined bridge and a turning windmill, with gusts that push you along the bridge.
  • Yoyle Needle Teams: with two teams, each map size now uses the two bases that are the most evenly matched in how fast they reach the middle (on average one side used to take 1.7 times as long; now it is about 1.1). Three and four teams are unchanged.
  • CPUs on Yoyle Needle Teams now watch the ends of ledges instead of walking off them.
  • Controls: a key tapped and released very quickly between two game frames now still counts, so quick double jumps and quick specials are never lost. Taps during the Final Smash slow-motion count too.
  • A special that is refused (Remote with no battery, or a fighter who has been hushed) now gives a short low tick and a grey puff, so you know why nothing came out.
  • CPUs now go for the knockout on a foe at high percent who is in reach, so long matches on the big stages (like Yoyle Canyon) end sooner.
  • Boss fights: no more phase name cards. A new phase is still marked by its sound, puff, shake and flash.
  • Fixes: a dash finisher could get stuck against a wall that pushed back and hold its targets for ever; it now ends there normally. Capture the Flag on the Forest map starts much sooner (the map check was wrongly tearing down the CPUs' builds).
  • Balance: no fighter's moves changed in this update.

Update 2 6 November 2026

  • New: SHIELD. Hold Z for a bubble that shrinks as it blocks and breaks into a long stun at zero. It lowers in 5 frames and still blocks for 3 frames after you let go. A grab on a shielding fighter breaks the shield and stuns the grabber for 40 frames, with no damage and no throw: the shielder is not stunned, but cannot shield again for about 2 seconds. The CPU no longer prefers to grab shielders. Z can be remapped.

Stopping, turning, jumping, attacking, getting hit or raising a shield ends it.
