Play online with friends

A four-letter room code is all your friends need.

Battle for Smash Island has online multiplayer built in. Press Play with Friends on the title screen, create a room, and you get a four-letter room code. Share the code or the link and your friends drop straight in: no setup, no IP addresses, no account.

Play now (free, in your browser) ▶

In the lobby

  • Every player and their fighter show in the lobby; the host sets the mode, stage and rules and presses Start.
  • Free-for-all, Teams and Capture the Flag can all be played online. Pick your sides before the match.
  • The results screen shows each player, and the host can call a rematch.
  • If someone is on an older build of the game, the lobby says who needs to refresh.
  • Guests predict their own moves through held keys, so the game stays responsive on an ordinary connection.

One keyboard per computer

Multiplayer is online only: each player uses their own computer and keyboard, and joins the same room. There is no same-computer two-player mode.

Joining

Got a code? Open Play with Friends, type it into the box and press Enter, or open the link your friend shared. Pick your fighter from the portrait grid and wait for the host to start.
