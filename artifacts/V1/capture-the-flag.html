Capture the Flag

Hide your flags, build your base, then raid the other team’s.

Capture the Flag is a team mode of Battle for Smash Island on huge maps of its own. Each side hides five flags, then the match proper begins: cross the map, find the other team's flags and carry them home while keeping yours safe. There are no stocks; a knockout is a trip home, or to the enemy's jail.

Play now (free, in your browser) ▶

How a match goes

  • The hiding phase: every door stands open while you place your flags. Space puts your flag down or picks it up again, P opens the team planning menu, and B picks the next piece when you are building your side. Doors lock when the phase ends.
  • Jail and rescue: a knocked-out raider can end up in the enemy jail. A teammate who holds the jail door for two seconds frees them; after a jailbreak the door cannot be opened again for a while, and a prisoner is let out on their own after 90 seconds.
  • Keys, doors and boosts: coloured keys open matching doors, and boost pads give a super jump.
  • Carrying: everyone moves faster in this mode, but a flag carrier is slower and jumps less.
  • Radar: after three minutes with no capture, and every minute after, every flag still out is shown for five seconds.
  • Dashers: in this mode only, dash fighters such as Leafy, Liy and Woody can jump out of a dash for a hyper or a super, Celeste-style.

Maps and players

The game shipped two maps first, Castle Yoyleberry and Crystal Mines and Coral Deep, and matches run from 2 to 8 a side. Before your first match the game offers a short practice. You can play it online with friends from the same room code as any other match, and run a Flag Cup tournament of CPU teams from the World Cup hub.
