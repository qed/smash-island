Bomby

Risk/reward · Battle for Dream Island · how Bomby plays in Battle for Smash Island

Battle for Dream Island · Risk/reward · weight 120 (heavy) · slow runner

Bomby is a playable fighter in Battle for Smash Island, a free fan-made platform fighter you play in your browser. The game lists Bomby as a Risk/reward from Battle for Dream Island. Kit in one line, as the select screen says it: Light My Fuse → detonate for huge radius, hurt self.

Bomby's moves

The move lines below are the game's own, from the character card. Damage is the first hit on a standing foe.

Attack (X)
Attack — a quick swing at whoever is right in front of you (short reach). On hit: 3%.
Ranged (X at a distance)
Ranged — a low sweep around your feet that hits both sides (short reach). On hit: 3%.
Special (C)
Light My Fuse — you blow up, hitting everyone around you (short reach), hardest up close; it costs you 14%. On hit: 18%.
Up special (↑ + C)
Short Fuse — a pop at your feet rocket-jumps you up and rattles anyone right beside you (short reach). On hit: 8%, stuns.
Down special (↓ + C)
Proximity Mine — sets a mine just ahead of you that arms after a moment and waits about 6 seconds; the first foe to step on it sets it off. Only one is out at a time: laying a new one removes the old. On hit: 30%.
Smash (V)
Big Bang — press V once: it charges for about half a second, then goes off by itself toward the nearest foe. Hold ↑ or ↓ with V for an up or down smash.
Finisher (V + C)
Finisher — you explode, hitting everyone around you (short reach); it costs you 4%. On hit: 18%.
Shield / Reflector (Z)
Reflector — no shield bubble: Z raises a pane that turns shots back faster (1.8 times the speed, 1.3 times the damage, at most 12% a shot). Melee and grabs go through it.

Tips for Bomby

  • Shield breaker: no. The smash does not break a shield outright; wear the bubble down or grab instead.
  • Defence: a reflector pane, so shots can be turned back but melee is not blocked.
  • Ranged attack: yes (Ranged), so there is an answer at range.
  • Effects in the kit: stun.
  • Weight 120: heavy for this roster (lighter fighters fly further from the same hit).
  • Ground speed: slow (0.84× the standard ground run).
