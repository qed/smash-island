Nickel (II)

Zoner · Inanimate Insanity · how Nickel (II) plays in Battle for Smash Island

Inanimate Insanity · Zoner · weight 73 (light) · slow runner

Nickel (II) is a playable fighter in Battle for Smash Island, a free fan-made platform fighter you play in your browser. The game lists Nickel (II) as a Zoner from Inanimate Insanity. Kit in one line, as the select screen says it: Double Zinger → sarcasm as a shockwave, stronger the more they have taken.

Nickel (II)'s moves

The move lines below are the game's own, from the character card. Damage is the first hit on a standing foe.

Attack (X)
Attack — a zinger in a speech bubble in front of him (short reach). On hit: 2.5%, and more the more they have taken.
Ranged (X at a distance)
Ranged — fires a small shot straight ahead (very long reach). On hit: 5%.
Special (C)
Double Zinger — two remarks at once blow everyone near away and shred anything in the air (medium reach). On hit: 8%.
Up special (↑ + C)
Legs Only — no arms, so he pedals both legs in a spin that kicks everyone around him (short reach). On hit: 4 to 7%, stuns them.
Down special (↓ + C)
Experiment 626 — no hit: Light Nickel stays put while Dark Nickel goes to work, faster for five seconds, and his next Attack (X) hits much harder; after the five seconds he snaps back to where he split.
Smash (V)
Baby Host! — press V once: it charges for about half a second, then goes off by itself toward the nearest foe. Hold ↑ or ↓ with V for an up or down smash.
Finisher (V + C)
Roasted Live on Air — he mutters a roast and kicks the foe right in front of him in the shin (short reach). Roasted, they are live on air for 5 seconds: each attack of theirs that hurts no fighter costs them 3%, with a laugh track. A boss is just kicked. On hit: 7%.
Shield / Reflector (Z)
Shield — hold Z for a bubble that shrinks as it blocks and breaks into a long stun at zero; it blocks hits, not grabs. Tap Z while running to sprint.

Tips for Nickel (II)

  • Shield breaker: no. The smash does not break a shield outright; wear the bubble down or grab instead.
  • Defence: a shield bubble; hold Z to block hits (not grabs).
  • Ranged attack: yes (Ranged), so there is an answer at range.
  • Effects in the kit: stun.
  • Weight 73: light for this roster (lighter fighters fly further from the same hit).
  • Ground speed: slow (0.92× the standard ground run).
