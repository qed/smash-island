Saw's moves

Every move in full, in the game's own words. Damage is the first hit on a standing foe. Frames are 1/60 of a second, counted from the press: startup is the frames from the key press to the hit (1 = the frame right after it), active is how long it can hit, end lag is how long you cannot act after. "Not measured" means the game has no frame numbers for that move yet. In the game, the fighter card and the pause menu have a short Moves list.

Hitboxes frame by frame. Under each move: one panel per frame, from frame 0 (the frame the move starts; the press can wait a few frames before that, which the table counts) to the end of its end lag, recorded from the game itself on a miss (a still foe far away), with the camera on the fighter and the ground as a grey line. The number on each panel is its frame; … means frames were skipped. Throws, the pummel, the shield and the Final Smash have no strip. Colours:

Shield, Final Smash and movement

ShieldZ

Shield — hold Z: a bubble in your colour blocks every hit and shot (a grab still gets you). It shrinks as it takes damage and refills when you let go; at zero it breaks and you are stunned for 2 s. It comes up 4 frames after the press and costs 5 to drop; jump leaves it at once, and it cannot be raised in the air. Out of it: grab 28 frames after you raise it (a miss leaves you open), jab 8, down tilt 12, smash 22, special 30 (the bubble drops when the move starts). Tap Z while moving on the ground to sprint instead (1.4x your run until you stop, turn, jump, attack or get hit); if Z is still held 6 frames after the press, it is a hold and comes up then.
