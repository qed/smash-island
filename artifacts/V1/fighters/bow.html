Bow

Summoner · Inanimate Insanity · how Bow plays in Battle for Smash Island

Inanimate Insanity · Summoner · weight 74 (light) · slow runner

Bow is a playable fighter in Battle for Smash Island, a free fan-made platform fighter you play in your browser. The game lists Bow as a Summoner from Inanimate Insanity. Kit in one line, as the select screen says it: Teleport → vanishes and reappears 240 px the way the arrows point (straight up with none), popping whoever is there, and after a hit she keeps her speed (it comes back only when she lands); ↓ + C sends two telekinetic snowballs out, one each way; the rest is chairs.

Bow's moves

The move lines below are the game's own, from the character card. Damage is the first hit on a standing foe.

Attack (X)
Attack — swings a chair around her (short reach). On hit: 2.6%.
Ranged (X at a distance)
Ranged — a chair sweep that catches both sides of her (short reach). On hit: 5%.
Special (C)
Teleport — she vanishes and reappears 240 px the way the arrows point (straight up with none), popping up whoever is where she appears (short reach). If she was hit this airtime she keeps the speed she was thrown at. Once per airtime: it comes back when she lands, not when she is hit. On hit: 6%.
Up special (↑ + C)
Chair Slam — no lift: three spins, and in the air she drops and the chair hits whoever she falls through, then the floor beside her (short reach). On hit: 13%, stuns them; 5.5% more on the way down.
Down special (↓ + C)
Snowballs — she levitates two snowballs without touching them and throws them at once, one each way, in an arc that comes down about a third of the stage away (long reach). On hit: 6% each.
Smash (V)
Musical Chairs — press V once: it charges for about half a second, then goes off by itself toward the nearest foe. Hold ↑ or ↓ with V for an up or down smash.
Finisher (V + C)
Topple the Chair Showroom — her telekinesis stacks three chairs under her, one after another, and she rises on top (about half a second); then she tips the tower over forward: whoever it falls through is caught on it and dragged down with it, and it crashes down along the floor ahead of her (long reach), with her landing at its far end. The crash hits everyone it brought down and everyone it lands on, once. Hold back while it stacks to topple it the other way. A hit on her before it falls knocks the tower down harmlessly and lets go of whoever it caught. On hit: 12%, stuns them.
Shield / Reflector (Z)
Shield — hold Z for a bubble that shrinks as it blocks and breaks into a long stun at zero; it blocks hits, not grabs. Tap Z while running to sprint.

Tips for Bow

  • Shield breaker: no. The smash does not break a shield outright; wear the bubble down or grab instead.
  • Defence: a shield bubble; hold Z to block hits (not grabs).
  • Ranged attack: yes (Ranged), so there is an answer at range.
  • Effects in the kit: stun.
  • Weight 74: light for this roster (lighter fighters fly further from the same hit).
  • Ground speed: slow (0.91× the standard ground run).
