Magnet

Crowd Puller · Inanimate Insanity · how Magnet plays in Battle for Smash Island

Inanimate Insanity · Crowd Puller · weight 118 (heavy) · slow runner

Magnet is a playable fighter in Battle for Smash Island, a free fan-made platform fighter you play in your browser. The game lists Magnet as a Crowd Puller from Inanimate Insanity. Kit in one line, as the select screen says it: Polarity → C'mon, Bestie! pulls the nearest foe in and hits them as they arrive, Like Poles Repel blasts everyone away, and metal fighters (Knife, Silver Spoon, Needle, Saw...) are pulled from further and shoved harder.

Magnet's moves

The move lines below are the game's own, from the character card. Damage is the first hit on a standing foe.

Attack (X)
Attack — a punch just in front of her (short reach). On hit: 3.1%.
Ranged (X at a distance)
Low Sweep — a quick sweep at the feet straight ahead that pops them up (short reach). On hit: 5%.
Up tilt (↑ + X)
Uppercut — a rising swing over the head that launches them straight up (short reach). On hit: 5%.
Special (C)
C'mon, Bestie! — pulls the nearest foe in front toward her (medium reach) and hits them as they arrive; metal fighters are pulled from further and hit harder. On hit: 12%.
Up special (↑ + C)
Climbing Gear — a straight climb that hauls up whoever is right beside her (short reach), and her next Attack (X) hits for much more damage and more launch. On hit: 7%, stuns them.
Down special (↓ + C)
Like Poles Repel — after a short wind-up, a blast all around her (short reach) shoves everyone away hard; metal fighters are shoved further. On hit: 7%.
Smash (V)
By Popular Demand — press V once: it charges for about half a second, then goes off by itself toward the nearest foe. Hold ↑ or ↓ with V for an up or down smash.
Finisher (V + C)
Stuck Together Like Fridge Magnets — a pulse magnetises the nearest foe in clear sight (long reach). For the next 1.5 s they are dragged into the nearest other foe and stuck to them, both rooted; with no other foe they are just rooted. A wall or a gap in the floor stops the drag. On hit: 4%, then 6% to each when they clash; stunned 0.4 s.
Shield / Reflector (Z)
Reflector — no shield bubble: Z raises a pane that turns shots back faster (1.8 times the speed, 1.3 times the damage, at most 12% a shot). Melee and grabs go through it.

Tips for Magnet

  • Shield breaker: no. The smash does not break a shield outright; wear the bubble down or grab instead.
  • Defence: a reflector pane, so shots can be turned back but melee is not blocked.
  • Ranged attack: yes (Low Sweep), so there is an answer at range.
  • Effects in the kit: stun, root.
  • Weight 118: heavy for this roster (lighter fighters fly further from the same hit).
  • Ground speed: slow (0.84× the standard ground run).
