Shield, Final Smash and movement

ReflectorZ

Reflector — hold Z: a pane in front of you turns enemy shots back at their owner, 1.8x faster and 1.3x the damage (at most 12%). It takes 6 frames to come up, lasts up to 36 while Z is held, and leaves you open for 22 frames after (then 30 more before you can raise it again). It does nothing against melee or grabs. The strong fighters have this instead of a shield. Tap Z while moving on the ground to sprint instead (1.4x your run until you stop, turn, jump, attack or get hit); if Z is still held 6 frames after the press, it is a hold and comes up then.
