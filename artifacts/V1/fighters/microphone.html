Microphone

Zoner · Inanimate Insanity · how Microphone plays in Battle for Smash Island

Inanimate Insanity · Zoner · weight 68 (light) · average runner

Microphone is a playable fighter in Battle for Smash Island, a free fan-made platform fighter you play in your browser. The game lists Microphone as a Zoner from Inanimate Insanity. Kit in one line, as the select screen says it: Yell at the Ball → shout a thrown ball forward; her button toggles Loud and Quiet.

Microphone's moves

The move lines below are the game's own, from the character card. Damage is the first hit on a standing foe.

Attack (X)
Attack — an open-hand slap just in front of her (short reach). On hit: 2.5%.
Ranged (X at a distance)
Ranged — a shout that pushes hard on both sides of her (short reach). On hit: 4%.
Special (C)
Yell at the Ball — tosses a ball, then press again to shout it forward, fast (long reach). On hit: 5%, and 13% once she yells at it.
Up special (↑ + C)
Feedback — a squeal throws her up, and whoever is beside her (short reach), and sends a soundwave straight ahead (long reach). On hit: 8%, stuns them; the soundwave does 4% and stuns.
Down special (↓ + C)
Power Button — no hit: the button on her chest switches her between Loud (her hits land harder and push further) and Quiet (she takes less herself).
Smash (V)
Glacier Breaker — press V once: it charges for about half a second, then goes off by itself toward the nearest foe. Hold ↑ or ↓ with V for an up or down smash.
Finisher (V + C)
Stuck Button, Point-Blank Blast — she grabs the foe right in front of her (short reach), jams her Power Button on both settings at once, and blasts them away point-blank. While she holds them she takes less from hits and is not knocked out of it. Whiffs on empty air. On hit: 12% (15% in her Loud mode).
Shield / Reflector (Z)
Shield — hold Z for a bubble that shrinks as it blocks and breaks into a long stun at zero; it blocks hits, not grabs. Tap Z while running to sprint.

Tips for Microphone

  • Shield breaker: no. The smash does not break a shield outright; wear the bubble down or grab instead.
  • Defence: a shield bubble; hold Z to block hits (not grabs).
  • Ranged attack: yes (Ranged), so there is an answer at range.
  • Effects in the kit: stun.
  • Weight 68: light for this roster (lighter fighters fly further from the same hit).
  • Ground speed: average (1.00× the standard ground run).
