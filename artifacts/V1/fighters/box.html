Box

Keeper · Inanimate Insanity

Box is a Keeper from Inanimate Insanity. In one line: Everything Packed → opens her flaps and packs away the shot coming at her; open them again and it comes back out, hers

Box's moves

The game's own move text. Damage is the first hit on a standing foe. In the game, the fighter card's Moves list adds frame data.

Attack (X)
Flap Flick — one flap flicks out and back just in front of her (short reach). On hit: 2.5%.
Ranged (X at a distance)
Ranged — a low kick at the feet (short reach). On hit: 3%.
Special (C)
Everything Packed — her flaps open and pack away the nearest shot coming at her; press again and it flies back out, hers and 20% harder. With no shot to catch, the flaps snap shut on whoever is just in front of her (short reach). On hit: 5%.
Up special (↑ + C)
Transcending Time and Space — she blinks out and back in, higher up, briefly untouchable, with a jump back, and knocks whoever is where she reappears (short reach). On hit: 5%.
Down special (↓ + C)
Lifeless Body — she goes limp as her old lifeless body; for half a second hits do little over half and barely move her, then the limp body flops over onto whoever is beside her (short reach). On hit: 4%.
Smash (V)
Sinker — press V once: it charges for about half a second, then goes off by itself toward the nearest foe. Hold ↑ or ↓ with V for an up or down smash.
Finisher (V + C)
Which One Is Box? — she ducks into herself and blinks back along her floor (up to 160 px; the other way if there is no room, or straight up), leaving an identical empty box where she stood. For 8 s, the first foe to walk up to the box or hit it, with a swing or a shot, springs its flap: a boxing glove on a spring punches out at them (medium reach). The box stops a shot. On hit: 12%.
Shield / Reflector (Z)
Shield — hold Z for a bubble that shrinks as it blocks and breaks into a long stun at zero; it blocks hits, not grabs. Tap Z while running to sprint.

Tips

  • The smash does not break shields: wear the bubble down or grab.
  • Has a ranged attack (Ranged).
  • Effects: plain damage and knockback.
  • Weight 86 (medium); lighter fighters fly further. Ground speed: average (1.02× the standard ground run).
