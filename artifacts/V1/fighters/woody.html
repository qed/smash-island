Woody

Evasive · Battle for Dream Island · how Woody plays in Battle for Smash Island

Battle for Dream Island · Evasive · weight 66 (light) · quick runner

Woody is a playable fighter in Battle for Smash Island, a free fan-made platform fighter you play in your browser. The game lists Woody as a Evasive from Battle for Dream Island. Kit in one line, as the select screen says it: Fraidy Dash → panic-dash with fear splinters.

Woody's moves

The move lines below are the game's own, from the character card. Damage is the first hit on a standing foe.

Attack (X)
Attack — three quick jabs just in front of him (short reach); the third one launches. On hit: 3 hits of 3%.
Ranged (X at a distance)
Ranged — a low sweep around his feet hits both sides (short reach). On hit: 4%.
Special (C)
Fraidy Dash — dashes forward, untouchable (medium reach), hitting anyone he runs into; three splinters drop behind as 8% traps. On hit: 12%.
Up special (↑ + C)
Scream — hops up screaming, with a jump back, and every foe right next to him (short reach) flinches. On hit: no damage, stuns.
Down special (↓ + C)
Dodge — no hit: slides back a little and can't be hit for a third of a second.
Smash (V)
Splinter Scramble — press V once: it charges for about half a second, then goes off by itself toward the nearest foe. Hold ↑ or ↓ with V for an up or down smash.
Finisher (V + C)
Fainting Plank Flop — he squeaks in fright, then faints and topples forward like a felled plank, slamming everyone along the floor ahead of him (short reach) and knocking them down. Hold back while he squeaks to fall the other way. He lies there half a second after. Jump it, or stand back. On hit: 12%.
Shield / Reflector (Z)
Shield — hold Z for a bubble that shrinks as it blocks and breaks into a long stun at zero; it blocks hits, not grabs. Tap Z while running to sprint.

Tips for Woody

  • Shield breaker: no. The smash does not break a shield outright; wear the bubble down or grab instead.
  • Defence: a shield bubble; hold Z to block hits (not grabs).
  • Ranged attack: yes (Ranged), so there is an answer at range.
  • Effects in the kit: stun.
  • Weight 66: light for this roster (lighter fighters fly further from the same hit).
  • Ground speed: quick (1.10× the standard ground run).
