Spikey

Burst · Inanimate Insanity · how Spikey plays in Battle for Smash Island

Inanimate Insanity · Burst · weight 92 (medium) · average runner

Spikey is a playable fighter in Battle for Smash Island, a free fan-made platform fighter you play in your browser. The game lists Spikey as a Burst from Inanimate Insanity. Kit in one line, as the select screen says it: Can't Hold It In → hold it in, let go, and every spike shoots off at once; he is bald until they grow back.

Spikey's moves

The move lines below are the game's own, from the character card. Damage is the first hit on a standing foe.

Attack (X)
Attack — a spike poke just in front of him (short reach). On hit: 2.8%.
Ranged (X at a distance)
Low Sweep — a low spike roll at the feet straight ahead (short reach). On hit: 3%.
Special (C)
Can't Hold It In — hold it in, let go, and every spike shoots off at once, all ways; bald until they regrow (long reach). On hit: 4% a spike, 6% held full.
Up special (↑ + C)
Hover — he lifts off and hovers up, turning, armoured for a moment; whoever touches his spikes is bumped away (short reach). On hit: 6%.
Down special (↓ + C)
Spike Grab — hooks the foe right in front on a spike, carries them along, then flings them (short reach). On hit: 8%.
Smash (V)
BE THE WHEEL! — press V once: it charges for about half a second, then goes off by itself toward the nearest foe. Hold ↑ or ↓ with V for an up or down smash.
Finisher (V + C)
Told You Not to Touch — bristles for 0.8 s, his spikes glowing red. The first close hit on him is blocked, and he answers with a spiked headbutt lunge at whoever threw it (short reach). Shots get through. On hit: 12%, and bleeding.
Shield / Reflector (Z)
Shield — hold Z for a bubble that shrinks as it blocks and breaks into a long stun at zero; it blocks hits, not grabs. Tap Z while running to sprint.

Tips for Spikey

  • Shield breaker: no. The smash does not break a shield outright; wear the bubble down or grab instead.
  • Defence: a shield bubble; hold Z to block hits (not grabs).
  • Ranged attack: yes (Low Sweep), so there is an answer at range.
  • Effects in the kit: spike (sends them straight down), armour.
  • Weight 92: medium for this roster (lighter fighters fly further from the same hit).
  • Ground speed: average (1.00× the standard ground run).
