Shield, Final Smash and movement

ShieldZ

Shield — hold Z: a bubble in your colour blocks every hit and shot (a grab still gets you). It shrinks as it takes damage and refills when you let go; at zero it breaks and you are stunned for 2 s. It comes up in 4 frames and costs 5 to drop; jump leaves it at once, and it cannot be raised in the air. Out of it: grab 28 frames after you raise it (a miss leaves you open), jab 8, down tilt 12, smash 22, special 30 (the bubble drops when the move starts). Tap Z while moving on the ground to sprint instead (1.4x your run until you stop, turn, jump, attack or get hit); if Z is still held 6 frames after the press, it is a hold and comes up then.
