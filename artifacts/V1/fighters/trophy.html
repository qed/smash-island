Trophy

Bully · Inanimate Insanity · how Trophy plays in Battle for Smash Island

Inanimate Insanity · Bully · weight 88 (medium) · slow runner

Trophy is a playable fighter in Battle for Smash Island, a free fan-made platform fighter you play in your browser. The game lists Trophy as a Bully from Inanimate Insanity. Kit in one line, as the select screen says it: Blackmail → snaps a photo; whoever's caught hits soft, and his next special shows everyone the picture.

Trophy's moves

The move lines below are the game's own, from the character card. Damage is the first hit on a standing foe.

Attack (X)
Attack — a punch just in front of him (short reach). On hit: 2.3%.
Ranged (X at a distance)
Ranged — a low kick at the feet (short reach). On hit: 3%.
Special (C)
Blackmail — a camera flash catches one foe ahead: their hits go soft, and his next special shows everyone the picture for 10% and a stun (medium reach). no hit: the photo itself.
Up special (↑ + C)
Horseplay — a jock's jump with a rising knee that pulls foes in and stuns them (short reach), and his next Attack (X) hits much harder. On hit: 6% (10% up close), stuns them.
Down special (↓ + C)
Sore Loser — a short hop and a flat-footed stomp just in front of him (short reach). On hit: 9%, knocks them down.
Smash (V)
Off the Iceberg — press V once: it charges for about half a second, then goes off by itself toward the nearest foe. Hold ↑ or ↓ with V for an up or down smash.
Finisher (V + C)
That's Mine Now, Thanks — a gold gleam, then a short swipe right in front of him (short reach). Every foe it hits loses their buffs to him: their Attack Up, speed and armour are his now, for the time they had left. On hit: 9%.
Shield / Reflector (Z)
Shield — hold Z for a bubble that shrinks as it blocks and breaks into a long stun at zero; it blocks hits, not grabs. Tap Z while running to sprint.

Tips for Trophy

  • Shield breaker: no. The smash does not break a shield outright; wear the bubble down or grab instead.
  • Defence: a shield bubble; hold Z to block hits (not grabs).
  • Ranged attack: yes (Ranged), so there is an answer at range.
  • Effects in the kit: stun, knockdown, armour.
  • Weight 88: medium for this roster (lighter fighters fly further from the same hit).
  • Ground speed: slow (0.95× the standard ground run).
