Naily

Glass zoner · Battle for Dream Island · how Naily plays in Battle for Smash Island

Battle for Dream Island · Glass zoner · weight 78 (medium) · quick runner

Naily is a playable fighter in Battle for Smash Island, a free fan-made platform fighter you play in your browser. The game lists Naily as a Glass zoner from Battle for Dream Island. Kit in one line, as the select screen says it: Nail Volley → fast piercing jabs, low weight · Smash = I NAILED IT!: a dash that ends in one big pin-launch.

Naily's moves

The move lines below are the game's own, from the character card. Damage is the first hit on a standing foe.

Attack (X)
Attack — a quick jab right in front of you (short reach). On hit: 3.3%.
Ranged (X at a distance)
Ranged — fires a fast shot straight ahead (very long reach). On hit: 5%.
Special (C)
Nail Volley — fires three fast nails in a tight fan that go through foes (reaches the whole stage). On hit: 8%.
Up special (↑ + C)
Hit It on the Head — rises, hangs a moment, then drops and hammers the ground on both sides (short reach). On hit: 9%, stuns.
Down special (↓ + C)
Spike Trap — sets a spike trap just ahead of you that lasts about 5 seconds; after a short wait, the first foe to step on it takes the hit. On hit: 9%.
Smash (V)
I NAILED IT! — dashes forward; whoever she runs into takes 16% and bleeds, then she jabs back behind her for 7% more and is stuck for half a second. Hold ↑ or ↓ with V for an up or down smash.
Finisher (V + C)
Tack It, Then Hammer Home — flicks a tack straight ahead (very long reach; hold back during the flick to send it the other way) that sticks in the first foe it hits. Your next hit on them within 3 s hammers it home, driving them into the ground for half a second; kept away from for 3 s, it drops out. On hit: the tack 3%; the hammer +8%.
Shield / Reflector (Z)
Shield — hold Z for a bubble that shrinks as it blocks and breaks into a long stun at zero; it blocks hits, not grabs. Tap Z while running to sprint.

Tips for Naily

  • Shield breaker: no. The smash does not break a shield outright; wear the bubble down or grab instead.
  • Defence: a shield bubble; hold Z to block hits (not grabs).
  • Ranged attack: yes (Ranged), so there is an answer at range.
  • Effects in the kit: stun, spike (sends them straight down).
  • Weight 78: medium for this roster (lighter fighters fly further from the same hit).
  • Ground speed: quick (1.11× the standard ground run).
