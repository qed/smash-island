Knife

Trickster · Inanimate Insanity · how Knife plays in Battle for Smash Island

Inanimate Insanity · Trickster · weight 88 (medium) · quick runner

Knife is a playable fighter in Battle for Smash Island, a free fan-made platform fighter you play in your browser. The game lists Knife as a Trickster from Inanimate Insanity. Kit in one line, as the select screen says it: Bag of Tricks → four tricks in order: the Temporary Paralyzer, a boomerang blade, caltrops, then the Fist Thingy.

Knife's moves

The move lines below are the game's own, from the character card. Damage is the first hit on a standing foe.

Attack (X)
Attack — a quick stab just in front of him (short reach). On hit: 2.8%.
Ranged (X at a distance)
Ranged — fires a small shot straight ahead (very long reach). On hit: 4%.
Special (C)
Bag of Tricks — in order: the Temporary Paralyzer, whose shock spreads and stuns, a boomerang blade, caltrops that draw blood, then the Fist Thingy to drag one in. On hit: 4% for the shock, up to 8%.
Up special (↑ + C)
Rising Stab — hops up thrusting the blade; the tip hits hardest. On hit: 5% up close, 9% at the tip, leaves them bleeding.
Down special (↓ + C)
Fake-Out — no hit: hops back, untouchable for a moment.
Smash (V)
Blade Rush — press V once: it charges for about half a second, then goes off by itself toward the nearest foe. Hold ↑ or ↓ with V for an up or down smash.
Finisher (V + C)
Shove Off the Platform — a quick sly grin, then he slips behind the nearest foe near him (medium reach) and shoves them toward the nearest edge of their floor. Face him when he appears to block it, and he staggers. On hit: 9%.
Shield / Reflector (Z)
Shield — hold Z for a bubble that shrinks as it blocks and breaks into a long stun at zero; it blocks hits, not grabs. Tap Z while running to sprint.

Tips for Knife

  • Shield breaker: no. The smash does not break a shield outright; wear the bubble down or grab instead.
  • Defence: a shield bubble; hold Z to block hits (not grabs).
  • Ranged attack: yes (Ranged), so there is an answer at range.
  • Effects in the kit: stun.
  • Weight 88: medium for this roster (lighter fighters fly further from the same hit).
  • Ground speed: quick (1.09× the standard ground run).
