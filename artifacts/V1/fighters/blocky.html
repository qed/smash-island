Blocky

Trapper · Battle for Dream Island · how Blocky plays in Battle for Smash Island

Battle for Dream Island · Trapper · weight 110 (heavy) · slow runner

Blocky is a playable fighter in Battle for Smash Island, a free fan-made platform fighter you play in your browser. The game lists Blocky as a Trapper from Battle for Dream Island. Kit in one line, as the select screen says it: Prank Rig → drops an anvil trap from above.

Blocky's moves

The move lines below are the game's own, from the character card. Damage is the first hit on a standing foe.

Attack (X)
Attack — a quick swing at whoever is right in front of you (short reach). On hit: 3.3%.
Ranged (X at a distance)
Ranged — a low sweep around your feet that hits both sides (short reach). On hit: 5%.
Special (C)
Prank Rig — drops an anvil from the sky onto the nearest foe after a short warning (reaches the whole stage). On hit: 13%.
Up special (↑ + C)
Block Spawning — you hop up as a block drops straight down just in front of you (short reach). On hit: 6%.
Down special (↓ + C)
Trap — sets a trap just ahead of you that arms in half a second and waits 6 seconds; the first foe to step on it takes the hit. On hit: 12%.
Smash (V)
Anvil Smash — press V once: it charges for about half a second, then goes off by itself toward the nearest foe. Hold ↑ or ↓ with V for an up or down smash. Breaks shields.
Finisher (V + C)
Prankster's Switcheroo — after a short wind-up you trade places with the nearest foe within 320 px, and they land where you stood (on your Trap if it is near). On hit: 8%.
Shield / Reflector (Z)
Shield — hold Z for a bubble that shrinks as it blocks and breaks into a long stun at zero; it blocks hits, not grabs. Tap Z while running to sprint.

Tips for Blocky

  • Shield breaker: yes. The smash breaks a raised shield bubble in one hit.
  • Defence: a shield bubble; hold Z to block hits (not grabs).
  • Ranged attack: yes (Ranged), so there is an answer at range.
  • Effects in the kit: plain damage and knockback.
  • Weight 110: heavy for this roster (lighter fighters fly further from the same hit).
  • Ground speed: slow (0.85× the standard ground run).
