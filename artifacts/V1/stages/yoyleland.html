Yoyleland

A metal deck in low grav. Mind the hatch under the core.

Yoyleland is a stage in Battle for Smash Island. The game describes it in one line: A metal deck in low grav. Mind the hatch under the core.

Play now (free, in your browser) ▶

What the stage is like

  • Size: a single-screen stage.
  • Platforms: 3, 1 of them solid blocks you cannot drop through.
  • Edges: real edges. The ground is 2 separate pieces with drops between them, and it ends on both sides.
  • Hazard: low gravity: everyone jumps higher and falls slower.

Stage features are read from the game's stage table. In Teams and Capture the Flag the arena differs; this describes a plain match.
