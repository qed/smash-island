Game modes

Everything you can do in Battle for Smash Island, a free browser platform fighter.

Battle for Smash Island is a platform fighter: knock your opponents off the stage, and the higher their percentage, the further they fly. These are the modes on the select screen and the title menu.

Play now (free, in your browser) ▶

Free-for-all and Teams

The standard matches. Pick a fighter, a stage and the number of contestants, and fight CPUs of four skill levels or your friends online. Teams puts a team ring and a shape under every fighter so sides never depend on colour alone. Items can be turned off, low, normal or high.

Capture the Flag

Two teams, each hiding five flags on a huge map, then raiding the other side's. Jail and rescue, keys and doors, boosts, a planning menu, and a Flag Cup tournament of CPU teams.

Boss Rush

A co-op gauntlet: you and CPU allies against the shows' villains, each in an arena of their own.

World Cup

A tournament of 16, 32 or 48 teams with a group stage and knockouts. Play it or watch it; solo, 2v2 or 3v3.

Daily match

A new challenge match every day, the same for everyone, with a Share button for the result.

Play with friends online

Create a room, share the four-letter code or the link, and your friends drop straight in. Multiplayer is online only: one keyboard per computer.

Learn: How to Play and Training

How to Play walks through the keys with a short practice; Training is a room with a dummy, a hitbox view and any damage you like. Every fighter card has an All moves tab, and the pause menu has a Move list.

Level Creator

Build your own stage and test how it feels.

Quests, Win Tokens and looks

Three quests a day and three a week pay Win Tokens, which buy looks in the Store. Looks never change how a fight plays. You unlock more fighters as you play.
